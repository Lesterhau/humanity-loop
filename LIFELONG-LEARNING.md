# Lifelong Learning & Intellectual Curiosity Division

## Mission

Raise society's learning capacity across the lifespan so educational gains are not isolated to one generation or class.

The goal is not merely higher test scores. It is:
- curiosity;
- reading/comprehension;
- numeracy;
- scientific reasoning;
- media/information literacy;
- digital/AI literacy;
- historical/contextual thinking;
- learning-to-learn;
- intellectual humility;
- practical problem-solving;
- intergenerational knowledge exchange.

## Design principle

Avoid a generational knowledge cliff in which younger cohorts transform rapidly while older cohorts are left behind, alienated, or distrustful.

Prefer **co-learning systems** that allow children, parents, workers, retirees, and communities to advance together.

- mentorship;

## Key KPI

Do not optimize solely for the highest-performing learners. Track whether the **floor and ceiling rise together** and whether generational learning gaps widen or narrow.
