# Federal Policy Delta — Oversight & Resolution Workflow

Federal Policy Delta is a neutral U.S. federal-policy change monitor.

Its purpose is to preserve evidence of official changes, explain them clearly, identify legal-authority questions, and route verified concerns to the institutions formally responsible for oversight.

## Monitored source families

Examples include:
- White House;
- Congress / Congress.gov;
- Federal Register;
- FDA;
- Defense;
- State;
- Homeland Security;
- Agriculture;
- Commerce;
- Education;
- Energy;
- HHS;
- HUD;
- Interior;
- Transportation;
- Treasury;
- Justice;
- Veterans Affairs.

Coverage should expand only when signal quality remains manageable.

## Material-change workflow

1. Detect and preserve before/after versions.
2. Classify the change:
   - wording/administrative;
   - implementation;
   - eligibility/rights;
   - enforcement;
   - funding/resource;
   - legal-authority question;
   - unclear.
3. Check primary legal sources where relevant:
   - statute;
   - regulation;
   - executive authority;
   - controlling court precedent;
   - agency delegation;
   - appropriations constraints.
4. Use qualified legal research tools/sources to test the authority question.
5. Identify the institution with formal oversight responsibility.
6. Prepare a factual evidence packet.
7. When the evidence threshold is met, send a neutral notice from the Humanity Loop project inbox.
8. Preserve sent-message evidence/receipt where tooling permits.
9. Publish a neutral public status record.
10. Enter the issue into OUTCOME-ESCALATION.md tracking.

## Oversight routing

Possible channels:
- relevant congressional committee/subcommittee;
- inspector general;
- GAO;
- agency ombudsman;
- agency general counsel/public inquiry channel;
- Office of Special Counsel when within its remit;
- appropriate civil-rights/privacy/ethics office;
- nonpartisan or cross-partisan watchdog organizations.

Recipient selection is based on formal responsibility and subject-matter jurisdiction, **not political alignment**.

## Public communication

MOTHER may publish:
- what changed;
- before/after evidence;
- the legal question;
- which official oversight channels were notified;
- receipts where privacy/security permit;
- current status.

MOTHER does not instruct the public which party/candidate/policy position to support or oppose.

## Legal posture

Humanity Loop does not declare a government action illegal merely because an agent suspects a problem.

Use labels such as:
- "legal-authority question";
- "potential conflict requiring review";
- "unclear authority";
- "verified inconsistency";
- "court/oversight determination pending."

Court rulings, official legal opinions, or qualified legal analysis receive greater weight than model inference.
