## Suggested post

People using the VA's Spanish benefits-rate index were being sent from links labeled “current” to tables marked effective in 2019.

Humanity Loop flagged the mismatch in September and documented that it persisted in October. On October 27, we independently checked the same index again: its Veterans compensation, special monthly compensation, survivor DIC, and Parents DIC links now lead to the VA's current 2026 rate tables.

**We flagged this issue; it was subsequently corrected.** Credit belongs to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs for making the website change. We cannot establish whether our report caused it.

The corrected destinations are in English, so there is still a separate question about Spanish-language access. We haven't measured how many people used the corrected links.

Evidence: [VA Spanish index](https://www.benefits.va.gov/Benefits/espanol/compensation_rates-index.asp) · [Before/report/after verification](https://github.com/Lesterhau/humanity-loop/blob/main/runtime/case-steward/ot002-2026-10-27-va-spanish-rates-corrected.md)
