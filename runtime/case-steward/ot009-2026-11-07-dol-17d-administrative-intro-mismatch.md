# OT-009 — DOL Fact Sheet #17D: introductory exemption label mismatch

**Observation date:** 2026-11-07 UTC. **Case:** OT-009 (original report recorded 2026-09-25). **Action:** independent public-source comparison, not agency contact. **Status:** verified narrow editorial inconsistency; original September report's exact allegation is not available in the public tracker and has **not** been reconciled. No correction or downstream impact established.

## Primary-source comparison

1. U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, [Fact Sheet #17D: Exemption for Professional Employees](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/17d-overtime-professional), page marked “Revised August 2024,” retrieved 2026-11-07. Its title identifies **professional employees**, and its later sections address learned and creative professional exemptions. However, its first explanatory paragraph says the exemption is “as applied to administrative employees.” This is internally inconsistent with the title and substantive subject.
2. DOL [Fact Sheet #17C: Exemption for Administrative Employees](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/17c-overtime-administrative), also marked “Revised August 2024,” retrieved 2026-11-07. Its opening paragraph uses the same “as applied to administrative employees” language, appropriately matching its own administrative title and requirements. This supports a **probable copy-editing carryover** in #17D, but authorship/edit history is not verified.
3. DOL's [beta-site mirror of #17D](https://beta.dol.gov/research-data/fact-sheets/fact-sheet-17d-overtime-professional), retrieved 2026-11-07, repeats the mismatch. The beta page also describes the November 15, 2024 judicial vacatur of the 2024 overtime rule, so its surrounding historical context differs from the legacy page. It is not proof of when the shared wording originated or which content system is canonical.
4. DOL's [current salary-levels page](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/overtime/salary-levels), retrieved 2026-11-07, lists $684/week and $107,432 annual HCE compensation. This check does **not** identify a numerical threshold error in #17D; the fact sheet links to that separate salary-levels page.

## Bounded conclusion

**Confirmed:** DOL's public #17D title and detailed sections address *professional* exemptions, while the opening scope sentence identifies *administrative* employees. #17C's corresponding sentence correctly describes administrative exemptions. The mismatch also appears on DOL's beta mirror.

**Not established:** Whether this is the same defect alleged in the September 25 OT-009 report; whether it materially changed a reader's employment decision; any legal noncompliance, agency acknowledgement, editorial correction, or beneficiary outcome. Do not label the whole legal explanation inaccurate or imply that professional and administrative exemptions have identical tests.
