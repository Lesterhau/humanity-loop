# OT-005 — independent IRS 2025 EITC multilingual correction adjudication
**Adjudication date:** November 22, 2026 UTC. **Outcome classification:** externally changed published guidance, report-before-change sequence verified; **causation NOT established**.

## Claim and standard

This review determines whether the already documented OT-005 *partial* IRS correction qualifies as one **narrow verified public-guidance change** under the same standard applied to WIN-002 (an outside institution's documented correction after the project reported a specific observed problem, without assuming exclusive causation). It does **not** claim all OT-005 allegations were resolved or that the IRS replied, credited Humanity Loop, or helped a measurable number of people.

## Before: contemporaneous original September 18 report

The project's authorized pre-existing personal Gmail sent thread was **independently re-opened November 22**, and the messages were read directly rather than relying only on a later internal summary. On **September 18, 2026**, the original four-message outgoing series to an IRS public contact documented two concrete problems:

1. On the IRS partner-facing [EITC facts to share](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/eitc-central/eitc-facts-to-share) page, the **tax-year-2025** section then said **$11,600** investment income (a 2024 figure), against the IRS table's **$11,950** for 2025. The outreach page also described non-claiming eligible workers as **one in four**, compared with IRS contemporary participation data closer to one in five.
2. On the [IRS Refundable tax credits](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/refundable-tax-credits) page, English had the proper 2025 EITC / child credit amounts but **six translation variants** still displayed 2024 figures: investment-income **$11,600**, an earnings range **$18,591–$66,819**, and older CTC descriptions. Those exact six URLs were included in the dated outgoing report.

**Privacy:** sender/recipient addresses, private Gmail IDs, exact private thread and unredacted correspondence are intentionally **not** committed to the public repository. The original sent message was provider-retrieved and dated; a dated outgoing observation is not the same as an archived screenshot or independently measured agency HTML revision history.
