## Proposed *editorial* replacement (human/HHSC review required; not sent)

**English:** “You may present your own case or choose someone, including a lawyer, to represent you. If Health and Human Services assigns an attorney to the hearing, that attorney represents the agency, not you. You may arrange your own attorney at your expense or seek free legal assistance through a local legal aid organization.”

**Español (draft for bilingual editor):** “Puedes presentar tu caso personalmente o elegir a alguien, incluso a un abogado, para que te represente. Si Salud y Servicios Humanos asigna un abogado para la audiencia, ese abogado representa a la agencia, no a ti. Puedes contratar a tu propio abogado por tu cuenta o buscar ayuda legal gratuita de una organización de asistencia legal.”

This wording reflects the **previously documented October 23 HHSC Appeals Division clarification** that its assigned attorney represents the agency, and the independently accessible HHSC FAQs about appellant counsel. It is a proposed clarification, not legal advice or a verified official policy revision.
