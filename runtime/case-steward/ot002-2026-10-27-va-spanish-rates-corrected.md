## Before and report

- Humanity Loop first reported on **2026-09-25** that the Spanish [VA benefits-rate index](https://www.benefits.va.gov/Benefits/espanol/compensation_rates-index.asp) labeled compensation and DIC destinations as current while the destination pages were marked **Effective 12/1/19**.
- VA Digital Media/Web Team replied September 28 with investigation/redirect context; the same exact defect was independently observed again on **2026-10-20**. On October 20, Humanity Loop supplied a bounded new-evidence update in existing authorized project correspondence. This history is recorded in [OT-002](../../OUTCOME-TRACKER.md). No evidence proves the report was the exclusive cause of the subsequent change.

## After: independently checked 2026-10-27

The [same original Spanish index](https://www.benefits.va.gov/Benefits/espanol/compensation_rates-index.asp) now links directly to current **2026** VA.gov rate tables for the four principal current-rate categories previously at issue:

1. **Veterans disability compensation** → https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/veteran-rates/ — heading states 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025.
2. **Special monthly compensation (SMC)** → https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/special-monthly-compensation-rates/ — heading states 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025.
3. **Survivor DIC** → https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/survivor-compensation/dependency-indemnity-compensation/survivor-rates/ — heading states 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025.
4. **Parents DIC** → https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/survivor-compensation/dependency-indemnity-compensation/parent-rates/ — heading states 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025.

The Spanish index currently displays **Last updated October 22, 2026**, but this page date alone does not prove when each link was changed or why. The index's other destinations were not all audited. The corrected rate pages are **English-language**; this result does not establish that full Spanish translations of current tables exist or that broader Spanish accessibility is solved.
