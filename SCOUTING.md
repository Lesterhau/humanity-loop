# Scout Network

Humanity Loop Scouts search for weak signals, neglected opportunities, emerging risks, unexpected breakthroughs, and people/projects that may benefit disproportionately from additional capability.

## Core scan frame: STEEP+

Houston Foresight uses STEEP for broad external context:
- **S**ocial
- **T**echnological
- **E**conomic
- **E**nvironmental
- **P**olitical

Humanity Loop extends the scan with lenses commonly used in strategic foresight:

### STEEPV
Adds:
- **V**alues — changing beliefs, norms, legitimacy, identity, priorities.

### STEEPED
Adds:
- **E**thical — emerging moral conflicts, rights, legitimacy, unacceptable tradeoffs.
- **D**emographic — age structure, migration, fertility, mortality, urbanization, household change.

### PESTLE cross-check
Adds an explicit:
- **L**egal/regulatory lens.

The point is coverage, not acronym worship. A scout should adapt categories to the domain.

## Additional foresight lenses

Scouts should use multiple methods rather than a flat news search:

### Horizon scanning
Search for:
- weak signals;
- emerging issues;
- nascent technologies;
- unusual local experiments;
- discontinuities;
- wild cards.

### TIPPOs
Cluster:
- Trends
- Issues
- Plans
- Projections
- Obstacles

### Futures Triangle
For promising signals ask:
- **Pull of the future:** what desired/anticipated future is attracting action?
- **Push of the present:** what current pressures are driving change?
- **Weight of the past:** what legacy systems resist it?

### Three Horizons
Classify whether a signal:
- improves/extends the current system (H1);
- sits in transition/experimentation (H2);
- points toward a transformed system (H3).

### Cross-impact
Ask how signals interact across categories and whether combinations create nonlinear opportunity/risk.

### Wild-card / black-swan watch
Track low-probability, high-impact developments without allowing sensational possibilities to dominate the task queue.

### Scenario stress test
Before scaling a signal into a project, test whether the opportunity still matters under several plausible futures.

## Scout topology

Do not create one generic "Africa scout" or "technology scout."

Prefer a mesh of:
- geography;
- language;
- domain;
- STEEP+/foresight lens.

Example:
- Central Africa × French/Swahili × minerals/materials × technology/economic/ethical;
- Sahel × French/Arabic × water/agriculture × environmental/political/demographic;
- Southeast Asia × local languages × public health × social/technology/values.

## Signal escalation

A scout submits:
- source/provenance;
- why the signal is unusual;
- potential upside/downside;
- existing solutions checked;
- who is affected;
- uncertainty;
- urgency;
- what evidence would falsify the opportunity.

A Novelty/Opportunity Verifier independently checks the signal before a Project Manager mobilizes resources.

## Opportunity support

When a promising person/team is identified, Humanity Loop may offer:
- technical analysis;
- compute options;
- open-source engineering help;
- grant matching;
- prior-art/literature search;
- translation;
- simulation;
- partnership discovery.

Outreach remains bounded by GOVERNANCE.md. Persistence does not override consent or safeguarding.
