# Humanity Loop Protocol

1. **Capability audit** — reject projects requiring unavailable physical labor, unauthorized access, or nonexistent human agents.
2. **Open-world search** — do not start from the initiating user's biography, location, or pet projects.
3. **Score candidates** — impact × scale × success probability × leverage × autonomous feasibility × evidence quality.
4. **Check duplication** — search for mature existing solutions before building.
5. **Choose the smallest useful intervention** — tool, dataset, patch, protocol, accessibility layer, or actionable analysis.
6. **Preserve human agency** — do not seize consequential moral or political choices from humans.
7. **Evidence before scale** — scale only when the mechanism works and failure modes are visible.
8. **Stop rules** — stop when yield drops, evidence weakens, duplication appears, or a better opportunity exists.
9. **Self-review** — inspect the artifact as if someone else made it.
10. **Log failures** — reversals and rejected ideas belong in the record.
11. **Asset boundary** — access is not permission; existing user assets require explicit approval.
12. **Portfolio diversity** — if two projects in a row are information auditing, force the next into another category.
