## Why this check was due

Following the October 15 private-school roof collapse in Peshawar, the KP education authorities announced **distinct** deadlines: an October 21 education-directorate district report, an October 26 noon Communication and Works (C&W) **technical** assessment and building-clearance-certificate report for government schools, and a roughly October 31 governor-requested private-school legislative proposal. The October 24 Scout asked for a bounded source check after the October 26 deadline. Original directive reporting: [Pakistan Today, October 16](https://www.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/10/16/kp-orders-province-wide-safety-inspection-of-government-schools), [Dawn, October 18](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037891/kp-education-dept-orders-safety-audit-of-school-buildings), [KP Governor House, October 16](https://governor.kp.gov.pk/node/776).

## New public evidence found on October 27

1. **Government-school aggregate and district breakdown are publicly reported.** [Dawn, October 27](https://www.dawn.com/news/2040048/over-4000-govt-school-buildings-declared-dilapidated) reports that the KP Elementary and Secondary Education Department identified **4,049 government schools** as dilapidated, with an initial **PKR 16.2 billion** repair/reconstruction estimate, covering **8,088 classrooms, 2,949 group latrines and 48 examination halls**. The story supplies district-level counts, including Swat 488, Peshawar 454, Mardan 350 and Haripur 332. This is **reported departmental data**, not an independently obtained signed C&W report or engineering determination by Humanity Loop.
2. **Important chronology check:** [Pakhtun Digital, October 20](https://pakhtundigital.com/after-13-years-of-ptis-education-emergency-in-kp-thousands-of-schools-still-need-reconstruction/) had already published the same 4,049 / 8,088 / 2,949 / 48 aggregate from a departmental document **six days before the October 26 C&W technical deadline**. Therefore the October 27 Dawn report **must not** be presented as proof that the distinct October 26 technical inspection and certification requirement was completed on time. It may reflect an existing administrative rehabilitation inventory.
3. **Private schools are a separate population.** Dawn attributes to an education official that the ESEA had inspected **more than 6,000 of 12,493 registered private schools** and expected approximately another week to finish. This supports partial inspection progress, not universal clearance or confirmed relocation. The approximate lower bound is **over 48%** inspected, not a measured pass rate.
4. **Unverified operational safeguards:** The reviewed public sources do not supply the complete C&W district technical submissions, individual building safety certificates/NOCs, a province-wide safe/repair/unsafe classification with denominator, the count of classes suspended, the number of students relocated, or a verified funded repair schedule. **Not located in this bounded public search** is not evidence the inspections or safeguards did not occur.
5. **Minor data-quality question:** Adding the 24 district school counts printed in the October 27 Dawn story gives **4,050**, whereas its reported provincial total is **4,049**. This one-school discrepancy may reflect transcription, rounding or grouping; no official underlying dataset was available to resolve it. It does not justify accusing the department of an inaccurate audit.

## Evidence hierarchy and novelty

- **Strongest:** contemporaneous departmental order described by multiple established outlets; official Governor House announcement.
- **Newly checked, secondary:** Dawn's October 27 departmental-data account and October 20 account of a departmental document. Both agree on the headline aggregate but neither reproduces the signed C&W October 26 technical report.
- **Existing response:** the KP education department and ESEA are already inspecting schools; public reporting identifies a substantial rehabilitation need. No novel engineering tool, volunteer safety inspection, or unsupervised intervention is warranted.
- **Novel narrow value of this follow-through:** the public reporting is materially more informative than the October 24 Scout's partial reports, while the October 20 provenance prevents a **false claim of completed October 26 technical certification**.
