This examines MHEC's separate **Applying for Financial Aid** page at https://mhec.maryland.gov/preparing/Pages/FinancialAid/apply.aspx, **not** the already documented College411 **Additional Financial Aid Resources** page at https://mhec.maryland.gov/preparing/pages/college411/addfinaidreso.aspx (issue #60 and 2026-11-12 source-provenance brief). ## Human-reviewable suggested replacement (not sent)

> Apply for the current academic year's Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) at https://studentaid.gov/. If the form requires a parent, spouse or other contributor, each contributor uses their own StudentAid.gov account to provide information, consent and sign. The online FAFSA lets you list up to 20 colleges or career schools. After processing, sign in to your StudentAid.gov Dashboard, find your FAFSA under My Activity and review your FAFSA Submission Summary, including your Student Aid Index (SAI). The SAI is a measure used to determine eligibility, not the amount your family is expected to pay. Check Maryland's current state-aid deadlines and any separate school requirements with MHEC and the institution.

**Editorial instruction:** This is a partial *federal-process* replacement only. Before any live-page change, MHEC must separately verify state award calculations, residence rules, current Maryland priority dates, documentation, and which 2026–27/2027–28 FAFSA cycle each paragraph applies to. A broad page rewrite is more appropriate than replacing SAR with a new acronym alone.
