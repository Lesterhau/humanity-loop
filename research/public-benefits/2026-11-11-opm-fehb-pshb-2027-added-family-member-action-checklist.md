# FEHB / PSHB 2027 Open Season — adding a family member: public-source action checklist (DRAFT; human review required)

**Research observation:** 2026-11-11 UTC. **Status:** Independent official-page web-reader re-verification plus a practical applicant-facing aid; not an OPM publication, enrollment instruction from an employing office, agency report, confirmed beneficiary outcome, or evidence of OPM endorsement. **Audience:** eligible FEHB/PSHB enrollees who plan to **add** a family member during the current Open Season. Do not submit personal documents to Humanity Loop.

## What an enrollee can safely do

1. **Check the Open Season deadline:** OPM says the 2026 federal benefits Open Season is **November 9–December 14, 2026**, for plan year 2027. Enrollment methods and processing vary by employing office and by employee/annuitant category. Do not assume that document review must be finalized by December 14; follow the employing office's actual election and verification notices. [OPM 2026 announcement](https://www.opm.gov/news/news-releases/opm-announces-2026-federal-benefits-open-season-as-premium-growth-slows-for-second-consecutive-year/).
2. **Identify whether you are adding someone:** OPM's [current Family Member Eligibility Verification FAQ](https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/special-initiatives/family-member-eligibility-verification/) and [July 15, 2026 BAL 26-202](https://www.opm.gov/retirement-center/publications-forms/benefits-administration-letters/2026/26-202a.pdf) require verification of **100% of elections adding family members** for plan year 2027, including additions that do not change the premium. This is **not** a blanket demand that every unchanged family member in every enrollment resubmit records.
3. **Gather only the documents your designated benefits office requests:** Examples in OPM's [FEHB Family Members reference](https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/reference-materials/reference/family-members/) include a government-issued marriage certificate for a spouse, birth/adoption records for a child, and additional proof for certain spouse, stepchild, foster-child, common-law-marriage or adult-disabled-child cases. These are examples, not a substitute for the office's current list or eligibility decision.
4. **Check whether earlier accepted proof counts:** OPM's current FAQ says acceptable documents already reviewed **during the same plan year** generally need not be resubmitted; proof accepted outside that year may require another review. For repeated elections during the same Open Season, previously completed verification need not be repeated. Ask your office to confirm what is already on file.
5. **Use only the authorized secure agency route:** OPM's FAQ says agency enrollment/upload systems vary and directs system questions to the employing office/shared-service provider. Do **not** email birth certificates, tax returns, Social Security numbers, marriage documents or financial records to an unverified address or to Humanity Loop. OPM's handbook allows redacting Social Security numbers and personal financial information from submitted copies, subject to the receiving office's instructions.
6. **Respond promptly to a verification notice:** OPM's FAQ says the existing **60-day document-submission period** remains unchanged; agency review should be completed within 30 days after receipt and no later than 90 days from the initial notice. These are not permission to ignore the Open Season election deadline or a guarantee of immediate coverage. Ask your employing office about the deadline and how a pending review affects your election.

## Why this clarification matters

As retrieved in the public web reader on **November 11, 2026**, OPM's older [FEHB Handbook Family Members page](https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/reference-materials/reference/family-members/) still says employing offices **are not required** to verify eligibility during Open Season, without stating the exception for **newly added** family members. That general statement conflicts with OPM's newer [FMEV FAQ](https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/special-initiatives/family-member-eligibility-verification/) and [BAL 26-202](https://www.opm.gov/retirement-center/publications-forms/benefits-administration-letters/2026/26-202a.pdf), which explicitly require verifying additions. The older page's document examples can still be useful, but its blanket Open Season sentence should not be relied upon to skip verification.

This is a **documented public-guidance inconsistency**, not proof of an incorrect agency eligibility decision, a failed election, a family losing coverage, or OPM's refusal to update its material. OPM's current FAQ mitigates the risk. No agency has been contacted by Humanity Loop about this finding.
