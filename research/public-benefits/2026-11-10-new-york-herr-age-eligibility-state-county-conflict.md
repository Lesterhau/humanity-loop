# New York 2026–27 HEAP heating-equipment benefit: conflicting public age guidance

**Observation date:** 2026-11-10 UTC
**Lane:** public benefits / external source-quality check
**Status:** verified public-documentation inconsistency; UNSENT editorial correction candidate; no applicant eligibility decision, government contact or reported harm.

## Distinct finding

The official **New York State** HERR application page was retrieved on November 10 and explicitly says the **2026–27 Heating Equipment Repair and Replacement (HERR) benefit opened October 1, 2026**. The same current page says homeowners must **be 60 or older**, while its **Apply** section mistakenly calls the season **2025–26 opened October 1, 2026** and says **“When applications reopen”** despite the stated opening.

The official **New York City HRA** community-partner notice states that **2026–27 HERR and Clean & Tune opened October 1, 2026** and **eligible homeowners of any age** may participate; it explicitly says the age-60 HERR restriction applied to **2025–26**. The official NYC ACCESS NYC page, updated November 2, independently lists **HERR for eligible homeowners of all ages** and says **apply now**.

A third government page, **Erie County HEAP HERR**, says the program is scheduled to open October 1, 2026, but still states that where there are multiple owners, one must be **age 60+**. Its page displays **Changed 10/28/2026**, making this a separate current public-text conflict, not merely an old cached result.

**Evidence classification:** A reproducible conflict among current government public-facing pages, not proof that any local district is applying an incorrect eligibility rule. A statewide 2026–27 OTDA GIS notice **26DC043 (dated September 9, 2026)** is linked by a third-party Rockland benefit guide, but the official PDF URL could not be fetched through the web reader (502). Its contents are therefore **not independently established here**. Erie County could have a separate operational interpretation or a stale page; ask the responsible program owner to resolve, do not declare its actual decisions unlawful.

HERR requires advance authorization; no reimbursement assumption is warranted.
