## Source-verified finding

The U.S. Department of Agriculture / Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) published a **proposed**, not final, rule titled **“Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program: Reforming Categorical Eligibility”** in the Federal Register publication process for **November 10, 2026**.

- **Primary official source inspected:** Federal Register Office of the Federal Register public-inspection PDF: https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-22936.pdf
- **Official document landing page:** https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-22936 (now directly inspected on November 10 in the postpublication follow-up above).
- **Identifiers:** FR Doc. **2026-22936**, docket **FNA-2026-0661**, RIN **0584-AF10**, 7 CFR part 273. These are directly visible in the official public-inspection PDF.
- **Proposed change:** Restrict TANF-derived SNAP categorical eligibility to households receiving TANF **assistance** as defined in 45 CFR 260.31, rather than automatically treating certain noncash/in-kind TANF benefits or services as a basis for categorical eligibility. Households not categorically eligible would be assessed under ordinary SNAP income and resource rules; this is **not** a proposal to terminate SNAP for everyone receiving TANF, nor does a proposal itself change any household's present eligibility.
- **USDA estimates, not observed outcomes:** At full modeled effect in **FY2029**, approximately **3.2 million households / 6.3 million people** would no longer receive SNAP because their income or resources exceed federal statutory limits. USDA estimates **1.78 million** children would lose *automatic* free-school-meal eligibility; it separately estimates approximately **1.66 million** of them would still receive free meals through an alternative qualification process, **93,000** would move to reduced-price meals and **28,000** would pay full price. It also estimates **5,500** WIC participants would no longer qualify after individual assessment. These are agency model estimates with uncertainty, not verified realized effects.
- **Official source text for estimates:** public-inspection PDF, Regulatory Impact Analysis summary (around PDF text lines 210–222).
- **Participation and privacy:** The official notice accepts comments via Regulations.gov, postal mail or email; commenters should identify **FNA-2026-0661** and the rule title. It warns that comment substance and commenters' identities may be publicly disclosed. Do not include private benefit, medical, financial, immigration, or child-identifying details in a public submission.
- **Deadline:** The prepublication PDF contains a placeholder. **Superseding postpublication verification:** the published Federal Register DATES section confirms **December 10, 2026** as the receipt deadline. Direct Regulations.gov form status remains unverified due to a 403 reader error; no accepted filing is claimed.
