# 2027–28 FAFSA Tagalog-language dependent-support date mismatch — source crosswalk (UNSENT)

**Observed:** 2026-11-12 UTC, public-source review. **Status:** specific official-page indexed-text inconsistency; independent uncached JavaScript-rendered browser verification and qualified Tagalog/FSA editorial review still needed. **No agency report sent, no actual applicant misclassification or harm established.**

## Primary sources and exact discrepancy

1. U.S. Department of Education / Federal Student Aid **2027–28 Tagalog FAFSA guide**, “Student Personal Circumstances”: https://studentaid.gov/apply-for-aid/fafsa/filling-out/other-languages/tagalog-guide-2027-28
   - The same bilingual bullet's **English** asks whether the student has children or other people (excluding spouse) living with and receiving more than half of their support from the student **now and between July 1, 2027 and June 30, 2028**.
   - Its immediately following **Tagalog parenthetical** instead says **“sa pagitan ng Hulyo 1, 2025, at Hunyo 30, 2026”** (between July 1, 2025 and June 30, 2026). The Tagalog dates are two years earlier than the adjacent English dates.
   - The **same Tagalog guide**, later under “Student's Children and Other Dependents” and “Parent Family Size,” correctly gives **Hulyo 1, 2027 ... Hunyo 30, 2028**, confirming internal inconsistency, not a blanket claim that the whole translation is stale.
2. FSA's final **2027–28 FAFSA English PDF** (official static form): https://studentaid.gov/sites/default/files/2027-28-fafsa-form.pdf . Page 1 identifies award period **July 1, 2027–June 30, 2028**. The official form was linked from the Department's current FAFSA Updates/Toolkit: https://financialaidtoolkit.ed.gov/tk/learn/fafsa/updates .
3. Federal Student Aid announcement, **Oct. 6, 2026**, confirms final 2027–28 PDF forms: https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2026-10-06/final-2027-28-fafsa-pdf-form-fafsa-form-incarcerated-applicants-and-fafsa-submission-summary .

## Practical public-safe clarification (for qualified editorial review, NOT distributed)

For the 2027–28 FAFSA, the relevant dependent-support interval in the bilingual guide's English question is **July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2028**, not July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026. Applicants should use the official 2027–28 form and consult Federal Student Aid or their school's financial-aid office if the translated guide is confusing. Do not enter private financial, identity, or family details into third-party correction drafts.
