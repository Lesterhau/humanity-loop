## Why this matters now

NYC HRA says the statewide fixed ABAWD 36-month clock restarted **2026-10-01**, ending **2029-09-30**. A person who previously lost SNAP for ABAWD noncompliance may reapply or be added back, subject to normal eligibility; existing counted noncompliant months reset for this period. HRA says the first possible new reductions/closures are **January 2027** and no separate **state** notices will be sent about the reset. The NYC ABAWD page explicitly tells people to reapply at ACCESS HRA or contact **718-SNAP-NOW (718-762-7669)** if their circumstances/exemption changed. These are *NYC/NY-state program communications*, not a universal national reopening or automatic reinstatement.
