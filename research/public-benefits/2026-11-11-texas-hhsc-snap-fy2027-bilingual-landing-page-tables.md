# Texas HHSC SNAP landing pages retain FY2026 amounts after FY2027 effective date

**Observation date:** 2026-11-11 UTC  
**Status:** independently source-reviewed public-information discrepancy; **UNSENT editorial correction candidate**. Not an agency report, benefits determination, participant submission, or verified correction.  
**Scout lane:** US-English/Spanish public benefits navigation; public-source review only.

## Official primary sources reviewed

1. Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC), public applicant-facing English **SNAP Food Benefits**: https://www.hhs.texas.gov/services/food/snap-food-benefits — as retrieved Nov 11, the sections “Maximum Monthly Income Limits” and “Maximum Monthly SNAP Amount” show the previous FY2026 figures with no effective-period label in those sections.
2. Texas HHSC, public applicant-facing Spanish **Beneficios de alimentos de SNAP**: https://www.hhs.texas.gov/es/servicios/alimentos/beneficios-de-alimentos-de-snap — same previous figures in “Cantidad máxima de ingreso mensual” and “Cantidad mensual máxima de SNAP.”
3. **Controlling Texas operational reference:** Texas Works Handbook, **C-121** and **C-122**, revision 26-4, effective **October 1, 2026**: https://fhb.hhs.texas.gov/handbooks/texas-works-handbook/c-120-supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program — current 130% gross, 100% net, and 165% categorical/separate-household thresholds; updated deductions and allotment method.
4. Texas Works Handbook, **C-1431**, revision 26-4, effective **October 1, 2026**: https://fhb.hhs.texas.gov/handbooks/texas-works-handbook/c-1430-snap-allotment-charts — current chart reference and $25 minimum for one- and two-person households.

USDA Food and Nutrition Administration, **FY2027 SNAP COLA** effective **October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027**: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/snap/allotment/cola — current maximum allotments, income standards and deductions for 48 states/DC. USDA's main address is https://www.fna.usda.gov/snap/allotment/cola; the FNA mirror above was accessible to the public-source retrieval.

## Direct comparison — applicant-facing English AND Spanish pages

Both HHSC landing pages show the same stale values. The income column below is the **165% of federal poverty level** threshold, which the HHSC handbook specifies for **categorically eligible households** and the special separate-household elderly/disabled test. It is **not a universal income cutoff for every SNAP applicant**. The landing pages call it a general idea for most households; the correct test depends on category, household, net income and other requirements.

For each additional household member, the old landing-page income figure is **+$757** versus current 165% **+$781**. Its maximum-allotment “+$218” language is outdated: for 9–17 members the FY2027 increment is **+$225**, with a separate **18+** cap of **$3,887**. Do not mechanically extrapolate that increment to 18+ households.

## Significance and limits

- **Verified:** Both state-owned English and Spanish applicant pages show values matching FY2026 while the state's own current staff handbook and USDA FY2027 source have higher amounts effective October 1. These are applicant-facing pages and link to the actual application.
- **Plausible risk, not observed outcome:** People may underestimate potential eligibility or the maximum amount, including a categorically eligible four-person household comparing $4,421 against the current $4,538 threshold. The actual benefit is often **below** the maximum and depends on income and deductions.
- **Not verified:** No evidence that Texas HHSC's actual eligibility engine, worker screening, award calculation, notices or individual benefit decisions use stale numbers. No applicant denial, agency correction, institutional acknowledgment or causal impact established.
- **Editorial nuance:** The public 165% “maximum income” table should be explicitly identified as a categorical/special-case threshold, with a pointer to 130% gross and 100% net standards and exceptions, rather than presenting it as a single universal eligibility test.
- **Verification limitation:** Public web retrieval was performed on Nov 11; a separate uncached ordinary-browser visual confirmation and qualified Texas SNAP editorial/policy review remain open.

## UNSENT editorial correction proposal — English

“SNAP income limits and maximum monthly benefits are updated each October. For **October 1, 2026–September 30, 2027**, the maximum SNAP benefit is **$306 for one person, $562 for two, and $1,023 for four**, before any reduction for countable net income. Texas's **165%-of-poverty categorical-eligibility income threshold** is **$2,195 for one person, $2,976 for two and $4,538 for four**. Different income and household rules may apply; see the Texas Works Handbook C-121 and apply through Your Texas Benefits for an individual determination. Update both English and Spanish tables for all household sizes and label their effective period.”
