# Michigan SNAP TLFA: separate under-18 applicant-age exception from under-14 dependent-child exception

**Observed:** 2026-11-11 UTC. **Disposition:** source-verified statutory reconciliation of an existing MDHHS public-information conflict; **UNSENT** review-only editorial candidate. This is a distinct follow-up to [the November 10 waiver/flyer audit](2026-11-10-michigan-snap-tlfa-november-waiver-outdated-flyer-faq.md), not a new independent applicant case, agency report, or verified beneficiary outcome.

## New primary-law crosswalk

The **enacted** [Public Law 119-21, §10102(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm) rewrote 7 U.S.C. §2015(o)(3). Its subparagraph **(A)** excepts an **individual who is under 18** (or over 65) from the SNAP ABAWD *time limit*. Its **different** subparagraph **(C)** excepts a **parent or other household member with responsibility for a dependent child under 14**. These are two different eligibility questions. The law also preserves other exceptions in (B), (D), (E), (F) and (G); a child's age alone does not decide every case.

Michigan's [current main MDHHS work-requirements page](https://www.michigan.gov/mdhhs/assistance-programs/food/learn-more/work-requirements) and its [October 30, 2026 announcement](https://www.michigan.gov/mdhhs/inside-mdhhs/newsroom/2026/10/30/federal-work-requirements-for-some-snap-recipients-begin-november-1) correctly use **under 14** for the household-dependent-child exception. The agency's separate [“How does an able-bodied individual meet the work requirements?” page](https://www.michigan.gov/mdhhs/assistance-programs/food/learn-more/work-requirements/how-does-an-able-bodied-individual-meet-the-work-requirements) still presents **“Reside in a household with a child under age 18”** as an exception. This older page's under-18 language is **not** saved by the statute's under-18 **individual age** exception; it applies the age to the child in the household, a different category.

## Narrow illustrative scenarios (not eligibility determinations)

| Hypothetical situation | What this source check establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| SNAP recipient is 17 | §10102(a)(3)(A) provides an age exception from the ABAWD time limit | Other SNAP eligibility and general work rules |
| Adult age 30 is responsible for a dependent child age 13 | §10102(a)(3)(C) supplies the dependent-child exception, subject to facts/household definition | Automatic benefit eligibility |
| Adult age 30 is responsible for a dependent child age 15 | The child-age exception in (C) alone does **not** apply; the older MDHHS page could incorrectly imply otherwise | That the adult necessarily must work or loses SNAP; another exception, area waiver, good cause, case-specific process or other rule may apply |

## Separate geographic and timing caveat

Michigan's October 30 announcement specifies a **November 1, 2026** waiver transition for Alger, Arenac, Iosco, Iron, Luce, Montmorency, Oceana, Roscommon counties and Bay City, while the main page lists continuing waivers for seven other counties and five cities through October 31, 2027. The older linked March flyer and main-page “March 1” FAQ were documented in the prior audit. **Do not conflate the waiver geography, applicant age, child exception and work-compliance pathways.** They are separate questions.

## UNSENT proposed editorial correction

> Please update the separate MDHHS “How does an able-bodied individual meet the work requirements?” page's “child under age 18” household exception. Public Law 119-21 §10102(a) separately provides an under-18 exception for the **individual** and an under-14 exception for a **responsible adult with a dependent child**. MDHHS's October 30 release and current main page already use the latter. Please link to the current main page, date the change, and distinguish the March and November 2026 geographic implementation phases on the linked flyer/FAQ. A qualified Michigan SNAP policy editor should verify all other listed exceptions and current agency operational guidance before publication.
