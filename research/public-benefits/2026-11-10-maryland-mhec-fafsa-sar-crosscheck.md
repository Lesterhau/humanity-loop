# Maryland MHEC FAFSA legacy SAR guidance — independent crosscheck (2026-11-10 UTC)

**Scope:** Humanity Loop issue #60, read-only public education-access verification. **Status:** confirmed inconsistent public terminology; unsent editorial suggestion; no agency report, applicant outcome, correction or outside contribution.

## Direct primary-source observations (checked 2026-11-10 UTC)

1. Maryland Higher Education Commission (MHEC), **Additional Financial Aid Resources**, https://mhec.maryland.gov/preparing/pages/college411/addfinaidreso.aspx — under “Sources of Federal Student Aid,” the public page still tells readers to request a copy of a “SAR” by calling the Federal Student Aid Information Center, and links to legacy federal aid domains. Web reader retrieved the page on Nov 10, with source crawl metadata “yesterday”; exact live origin rendering for every visitor was not independently captured.
2. **MHEC itself acknowledges the terminology change** in its **Important FAFSA Changes** page, https://mhec.maryland.gov/Pages/FAFSA-changes.aspx — in the 2024 FAFSA changes material, item 7 explicitly maps “Student Aid Report (SAR)” to “FAFSA Submission Summary (FSS).” This is unusually strong same-institution corroboration that the separate undated College411 resources page has not been harmonized. The 2024 page is historical, not proof of a current Maryland award deadline.
3. U.S. Department of Education / Federal Student Aid, **Learn About the FAFSA Submission Summary**, https://studentaid.gov/apply-for-aid/fafsa/review-and-correct/fafsa-submission-summary/ — the official article describes access to the processed FAFSA Submission Summary through StudentAid.gov Dashboard → My Activity → processed FAFSA → View FAFSA Submission Summary. The main web app can require JavaScript; the public indexed article text was available.
4. Federal Student Aid, **7 Things To Do After Submitting Your FAFSA**, https://studentaid.gov/articles/things-after-fafsa/ — independent official explanatory article confirms processed forms and the FAFSA Submission Summary are available through the StudentAid.gov account, and distinguishes the summary from an actual aid offer.
5. MHEC's newer **Maryland Higher Education FAFSA Updates** page, https://mhec.maryland.gov/Pages/Maryland-Higher-Education-FAFSA-Updates.aspx, provides a separate newer financial-aid entry point. Existence of a newer page does not establish that the old College411 URL is retired, replaced, or unreachable; it remained publicly retrievable on Nov 10.

## Narrow assessment

**Verified:** Maryland's old public guide uses the legacy “SAR” name without explaining the newer FAFSA Submission Summary; Maryland's own separate FAFSA-change guidance expressly documents the replacement terminology, consistent with current federal student-aid instructions.

**Unverified:** Whether the listed FSA telephone numbers remain useful, whether legacy SAR records can still be requested, the number of affected students, any failed application or lost aid, any active MHEC editorial ticket, or ordinary-browser rendering of the page. This is a documentation/accessibility concern, **not a finding of agency benefit misprocessing**. Do not generalize to all MHEC financial-aid pages.
