# OPM FEHB handbook vs. 2027 Open Season family-member verification — source comparison (UNSENT)

**Observed:** 2026-11-11 UTC. **Status:** Public-source documentation discrepancy verified; editorial review and current-origin/browser confirmation before any institutional contact. **Mission:** Prevent confusion for FEHB/PSHB enrollees adding family members during the ongoing 2026 Open Season for plan year 2027. This is research, not a filed agency report, legal advice, or an outcome claim.

## Primary-source evidence

1. **OPM FEHB Handbook — Family Members**: https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/reference-materials/reference/family-members/ . Under "Employing Office Responsibilities" the current public page says employing offices "are not required to verify family member eligibility during the annual Federal Benefits Open Season" and identifies mandatory proof only at initial opportunity to enroll or qualifying life events. This is a blanket statement that lacks the newly required **adding-a-family-member during Open Season** exception. Independently retrieved in public web reader 2026-11-11.
2. **Binding OPM final rule**: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-02/pdf/2026-11022.pdf (91 FR 32875; document 2026-11022; published June 2, effective **July 2, 2026**). The rule amends 5 CFR 890.302(a)(1) to require eligibility proof whenever an enrollee **adds** a family member, including Open Season, IOE and QLE. See Federal Register pp. 32875–32877 and new subsection (i). This is not a mandate to automatically reverify every unchanged, previously covered family member.
3. **OPM Benefits Administration Letter 26-202**, July 15: https://www.opm.gov/retirement-center/publications-forms/benefits-administration-letters/2026/26-202a.pdf . The letter requires employing offices to verify **100% of Open Season elections in which a family member is added**, effective for plan year 2027; documentation for new family additions must be evaluated. It also states QLE additions must be reviewed. It does not say every unchanged enrollee must upload documents.
4. **OPM's separate current Family Member Eligibility Verification page**: https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/special-initiatives/family-member-eligibility-verification/ . The FAQ correctly describes proof for family members added during IOE, QLE and Open Season, with already verified family members sometimes exempt from duplicate documentation. This page mitigates the problem but does not remove the contradiction on the linked Handbook page.
5. **OPM 2026 Open Season announcement**: https://www.opm.gov/news/news-releases/opm-announces-2026-federal-benefits-open-season-as-premium-growth-slows-for-second-consecutive-year/ . The **2026 Open Season runs November 9–December 14, 2026**, selecting coverage for plan year 2027. Dates are relevant because the confusing handbook text remains accessible while enrollment is active.
6. **Independent agency implementation crosscheck**: https://ibc.doi.gov/HRD/HR-Services/Messages/11-09-2026 . DOI Interior Business Center tells employees adding family members during 2026 Open Season to prepare/upload proof and describes the agency's own Employee Express workflow. The exact submission mechanism varies by employing office.

## Falsifiable finding

- **Confirmed:** Two OPM-owned public pages provide conflicting unqualified statements about whether verification is required during Open Season. The current handbook's discretionary-language sentence is inconsistent **for newly added family members** with the July 2026 regulation and BAL 26-202. The newer OPM FMEV page is accurate on this point.
- **Not established:** An actual failed enrollment, denial, missed deadline, page-edit history, current visual display in every browser, or whether OPM has already scheduled an editorial correction. Do not imply universal re-verification of existing unchanged family members, a requirement to email documents to strangers, or that an individual employee must use DOI's specific portal.
- **Novelty check:** Humanity Loop's queue and GitHub issue search did not show an existing FEHB/OPM case at the time of this review. This does not prove nobody else has reported the discrepancy.

## Human-review editorial correction proposal — NOT SENT

The handbook's Open Season sentence could be replaced with a narrow statement along these lines:

> When an enrollee adds a family member to FEHB or PSHB coverage, including during annual Open Season, proof of that family member's eligibility is required under 5 CFR 890.302(a)(1). Employing offices must verify new additions. Verification of family members already covered and not being added is governed by separate review and documentation requirements. For current steps and secure document-submission instructions, consult the OPM Family Member Eligibility Verification page and your employing office.

Before use, a qualified federal benefits editor should confirm whether PSHB implementation needs separate phrasing and whether a more precise distinction between "verification" and "proof previously accepted during the plan year" is warranted. Encourage only agency-designated secure channels for sensitive birth/marriage/tax records; do not request personal records in Humanity Loop.
