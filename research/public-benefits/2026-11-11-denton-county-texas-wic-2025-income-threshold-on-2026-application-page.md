# Denton County WIC: applicant-facing 2025 income thresholds conflict with current Texas WIC (2026–27)

**Evidence status:** Independently source-reviewed, November 11, 2026 UTC. **Tier-0 public-information comparison only; UNSENT editorial correction candidate.** No applicant decision, agency notification, website correction, outside contributor, or beneficiary impact established.

## Direct primary sources and observed evidence

1. **Denton County Public Health — WIC Eligibility**: https://www.dentoncounty.gov/897/Eligibility (retrieved November 11, 2026 UTC). The page says "Check to see if your income qualifies you to receive WIC benefits below" and lists household gross monthly ceilings of **$2,413 / $3,261 / $4,109 / $4,957 / $5,805 / $6,653** for sizes 1–6. It links to the Texas WIC application and says households receiving Medicaid/SNAP/TANF already meet the income test. The page does not label these ceilings with an effective period.
2. **Texas Health and Human Services — official Texas WIC application/income page**: https://www.texaswic.org/apply (retrieved November 11, 2026 UTC; site footer dated November 5, 2026). Current gross monthly ceilings are **$2,461 / $3,337 / $4,212 / $5,088 / $5,964 / $6,839** for sizes 1–6. Texas WIC says SNAP/Medicaid/TANF participation satisfies the income criterion; applicants still undergo an eligibility appointment.
3. **USDA Food and Nutrition Administration — 2026–27 WIC guidelines**, published April 29, 2026: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/wic/fr-042926 . The 185%-of-poverty monthly values for contiguous states match the Texas WIC page, effective **July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027**. USDA allows state agencies to coordinate earlier with Medicaid but requires implementation no later than July 1.
4. **USDA FNA — 2025–26 historical WIC guidelines**, published March 10, 2025: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/wic/fr-031025 . The Denton County figures match the previous income period, **July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026**.
5. **Denton County WIC program landing page**: https://www.dentoncounty.gov/873/Women-Infants-Children-WIC (retrieved November 11, 2026 UTC). It directs visitors to the county's eligibility requirements, so the outdated table is in an applicant-facing path, not an isolated archive.
