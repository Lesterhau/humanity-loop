## Public-service distinction

Ohio residents at risk of losing electricity or natural gas have **two different routes** that must not be conflated:

1. **Special Reconnect Order (SRO):** The Ohio Consumers' Counsel (OCC), a state agency, says this is available **October 12, 2026–April 16, 2027** for residential customers of **PUCO-regulated** electric or natural-gas utilities. **No income test** applies to SRO. A customer can generally maintain/reconnect covered service by paying **$175** rather than the entire overdue amount; this is **not cancellation of the remaining debt**. If disconnected, up to **$36 of reconnection fees** may be due up front, with excess fees deferred. The utility should be contacted directly. SRO does **not** establish the same rights for municipal utilities or cooperatives.
2. **Winter Crisis Program (WCP):** OCC describes a **November 2, 2026–March 31, 2027** assistance period for qualifying households with a disconnection, threatened disconnection, transfer/new-service need, PIPP default or very low bulk fuel. This program **does** have income and other eligibility rules. For 1–7 people OCC lists **175% of federal income guidelines**; for 8+ people it lists **60% state median income**. An eligible applicant may receive help toward the SRO's $175, but approval and benefit amounts are not automatic. An **appointment with a local Energy Assistance Provider/Community Action Agency (EAP/CAA)** is required to complete the WCP application, including remote or in-person appointments.
3. **30-day disconnection protection:** OCC states that when a WCP appointment is made with a local EAP/CAA, PUCO-regulated utilities must delay disconnection for **30 days after the appointment**, with a **once-per-year** limitation. This is not a universal 30-day grace period for every utility/customer, nor a guarantee of permanent service.

## Applicant-facing draft (NOT distributed)

> **Ohio heating or electricity at risk?** If your provider is a PUCO-regulated electric or natural-gas utility, Ohio's 2026–27 Special Reconnect Order may let you avoid a shutoff or reconnect by paying $175 even if you owe more, with **no income requirement**. You still owe the remaining balance, and a reconnection fee may apply. Contact your utility and ask specifically about the **Special Reconnect Order**. If your household has limited income, ask a local Energy Assistance Provider about **Winter Crisis Program** assistance and the 30-day disconnection hold linked to a qualifying appointment. Check the official [OCC SRO guide](https://www.occ.ohio.gov/factsheet/special-reconnect-order-sro) and [OCC WCP guide](https://occ.ohio.gov/factsheet/heap-winter-crisis-program-wcp); eligibility and utility coverage must be confirmed.
