# U.S. Virgin Islands SNAP: FY2026 chart still linked during FY2027 — public information-access review

**Research completed:** 2026-11-09 UTC  
**Lane:** U.S. public-benefits information access; one-off Tier-0 external source comparison (not a seventh Scout Mesh cell)  
**Decision:** `verified_editorial_update_candidate` — publicly accessible, expired-period chart linked from a live agency page. **Not** a finding of incorrect benefit administration or an actual applicant denial.  
**Responsible publisher:** U.S. Virgin Islands Department of Human Services (VIDHS), Division of Family Assistance.  
**Review-only:** No agency outreach, form submission, eligibility determination, live benefit calculation, or public correction has been undertaken.

## Precise observed condition

On November 9, 2026, the live [VIDHS Family Assistance page](https://dhs.vi.gov/family-assistance-programs/) directly displayed the link label **“SNAP Income Eligibility Limits Chart – FY 2026 (Click here)”** in its SNAP Forms section. Following that link opened an official VIDHS [one-page chart PDF](https://dhs.vi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/DFA_FY-2026-COLA-ADJUSTMENTS-AND-DEDUCTIONS-TABLE.pdf). The PDF explicitly dates itself **October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026** (top of page 1). It contains 2026 SNAP income screening amounts, deductions and maximum allotments. Thus the linked chart is a clearly labeled *prior-fiscal-year* document, not a false label on current FY2027 figures.

The [USDA Food and Nutrition Administration's current SNAP COLA tables](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/snap/allotment/cola) explicitly cover **October 1, 2026–September 30, 2027**. These official federal tables include the Virgin Islands in the common income-standard column and provide a separate Virgin Islands maximum-allotment column. The current federal figures differ from those in the linked VIDHS PDF. The USDA FY2027 policy notice was published in August 2026; the USDA live tables were directly retrieved during this review.

**Important local eligibility caveat:** The [VIDHS October 1, 2025 press release](https://dhs.vi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/VIDHS-Press-Release-SNAP-COLA-and-Simplified-Reporting-October-2025.pdf) explicitly says the territory adopted a **175% of federal poverty** income limit for categorically eligible (CATEL) households, while 130% remains relevant to simplified reporting. The linked chart itself also contains a 175% column. The crosswalk's federal 130% values are *not* a complete statement of current Virgin Islands applicant eligibility. Do not advise an applicant that crossing 130% automatically disqualifies them. Eligibility depends on categorical status, household composition, deductions, other rules and agency determination.

## Why this matters, and what it does not prove

The agency's current navigation offers a downloadable reference for an already ended fiscal year without a neighboring FY2027 chart link in that particular SNAP Forms section. An applicant using it as current guidance could underestimate some 2027 maximum allotments or screening figures. This is a **plausible information-access risk**, not evidence that the territory is processing claims using the old amounts, that no FY2027 document exists anywhere on the agency site, that any individual lost benefits, or that an agency failed a legal duty. The FY2026 chart is valid historical information for its stated period.

The live VIDHS page also includes an old Benefits.gov attribution in a separate paragraph. That is **not** the finding of this investigation and is not counted as another defect; Benefits.gov may redirect to USA.gov. This audit is strictly about the FY2026-vs-FY2027 chart placement and values.

## Bounded human-review editorial suggestion — NOT SENT

> On your current Family Assistance / SNAP page, the Forms section links a “SNAP Income Eligibility Limits Chart – FY 2026” PDF dated October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026. USDA has published FY2027 SNAP income, allotment and deduction tables effective October 1, 2026. For clarity, please consider placing a current, territory-reviewed FY2027 chart in the SNAP section and retaining the FY2026 chart under an explicitly labeled prior-year archive. Please preserve the territory's distinct categorical-eligibility (175%) and simplified-reporting (130%) explanations; the national 130% screening table alone is not a substitute for territory-specific policy. This is a factual website-reference suggestion, not a claim about how applications are adjudicated.

No outreach is authorized merely by creating this draft. Human/program review should check the applicable 2026–27 territorial CATEL rules and all benefit columns before proposing a replacement PDF or contact.

2. Follow the link to the official PDF and verify the effective dates printed at the top and its 1-, 2- and 4-person figures.
3. Independently open [USDA FY2027 SNAP COLA tables](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/snap/allotment/cola) and verify the October 2026–September 2027 period, Virgin Islands columns and the crosswalk above.
4. Search for a newer VIDHS chart or a link explicitly indicating that the FY2026 document is archival. A new current chart in the same navigation would narrow or resolve the finding.
5. **Falsify the active-reference concern** if ordinary visitors are served a newer FY2027 PDF despite the old link label, or if the page is intentionally an FY2026 historical archive with clear FY2027 navigation. If confirmed, obtain human editorial approval before any agency contact.
