# Michigan SNAP TLFA: April 2026 BEM 620 confirms under-14 child rule while public FAQ still says under 18

**Observation:** 2026-11-12 UTC. **Disposition:** independently source-reviewed, public-safe, **UNSENT** editorial/version-parity follow-up to [Nov 11 statutory crosswalk](2026-11-11-michigan-snap-under18-vs-under14-statutory-crosswalk.md). No agency contact, individual case access, or verified denial/correction.

## New evidence not in the earlier statutory comparison

The MDHHS **Bridges Eligibility Manual, BEM 620**, in the official [compiled mobile policy PDF](https://mdhhs-pres-prod.michigan.gov/olmweb/ex/BP/Mobile/BEM/BEM%20Mobile.pdf), has an **April 1, 2026** revision, **BPB 2026-008**, around printed BEM 620 pages 1–3 (PDF text lines 31254–31321). This is an actual state policy-manual source, not merely a news release:

- Page 1: TLFA applies to FAP individuals **ages 18–64 without dependents under 14**, unless deferred.
- Page 3: a FAP group containing an **individual under age 14** is deferred, even if that child is disqualified or otherwise ineligible, referring to BEM 212.
- Page 2–3: MDHHS specialists must explain the rules, screen for deferrals, and **re-screen when an individual becomes subject to TLFA before applying a countable month**.

A separate official [single-item BEM 620 PDF](https://mdhhs-pres-prod.michigan.gov/olmweb/exf/BP/Public/BEM/620.pdf) retrieved in the public reader carries **BPB 2025-031, December 1, 2025**, and already says **under 14** in its FAP-group-child deferral (page 3), although its December 2025 waiver-location list is historical. An older [alternate single-item URL](https://mdhhs-pres-prod.michigan.gov/olmweb/EX/BP/Public/BEM/620.pdf) returns **BPB 2025-023, October 1, 2025**, with the **under-18** group-child deferral and 18–54 TLFA age range. These three differently versioned URLs demonstrate why one search hit or cached policy PDF cannot be called the current operative rule without checking the revision.

The **separate public MDHHS help page** [“How does an able-bodied individual meet the work requirements?”](https://www.michigan.gov/mdhhs/assistance-programs/food/learn-more/work-requirements/how-does-an-able-bodied-individual-meet-the-work-requirements) still says a person may be exempt for residing in a household with a child **under age 18** (reader observed Nov 12; wording split across bullets). The [MDHHS October 30, 2026 announcement](https://www.michigan.gov/mdhhs/inside-mdhhs/newsroom/2026/10/30/federal-work-requirements-for-some-snap-recipients-begin-november-1) instead says **under 14**, as does the April manual. [Public Law 119-21 §10102(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm) distinguishes the **applicant under 18** exception from the **responsible household member/dependent child under 14** exception.

## Implication and boundaries

**The newer manual supports a narrower, more defensible conclusion:** this is a stale **public help-page wording** conflict against federal law, a state manual, and an agency news release. It is **not evidence that the Bridges eligibility engine or caseworkers use the old age threshold**. The manual is a published policy document, not proof that software or individual determinations comply.

The April manual's December 2025 **waiver-location list is historical for November 2026**. The October 30 MDHHS announcement says **Alger, Arenac, Iosco, Iron, Luce, Montmorency, Oceana, Roscommon counties and Bay City** lost their previous waiver on **Nov 1, 2026**; other specified locations retain waivers. Do not use the older PDF's location list as live waiver status. The exact group-composition/parental-responsibility facts, other exceptions, notices, and local waivers must be evaluated case by case.

## Narrow, unsent editorial recommendation

A qualified MDHHS SNAP policy/web editor should update or retire the standalone under-18 help-page bullet, link to current BEM 620 and the latest work-requirements landing page, and ensure readers can distinguish an **individual's age** from a **dependent child's age**. Separately check the published manual's waiver-location section against the October 30 notice and whether a newer post-November BEM 620 bulletin exists. Do not assert an operational screening defect without case or system evidence.
