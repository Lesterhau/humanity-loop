# Butte County, California WIC: applicant-facing income chart still shows expired 2025–26 limits

**Research observation:** 2026-11-11 UTC. **Status:** verified discrepancy in publicly retrievable government-page text; independent uncached ordinary-browser capture and county editorial confirmation pending. **Action:** public-safe evidence only; UNSENT correction draft. **Scope:** Butte County WIC applicant guidance, not California WIC eligibility administration or any individual eligibility decision.

## Primary-source comparison

1. **Butte County Public Health, “Are You Eligible?”** https://www.buttecounty.net/1274/Are-You-Eligible — public web retrieval on Nov 11 shows an income table explicitly marked **“effective April 1, 2025 - June 30, 2026”**, with 2025–26 values. The county page also links to the state's WIC assessment. Direct ordinary-browser uncached rendering was **not** separately established; public-web retrieval metadata reported a crawl approximately two weeks old.
2. **California Department of Public Health, California WIC “Am I Eligible?”** https://myfamily.wic.ca.gov/Home/AmIEligible — public web retrieval on Nov 11 (indexed/crawled today) displays **“185% Federal Poverty Level, Effective May 1, 2026 - June 30, 2027”** and higher values. CDPH explicitly states only local WIC staff can confirm eligibility; Medi-Cal, CalFresh, CalWORKs or FDPIR participation may confer an income-eligibility route.
3. **USDA Food and Nutrition Administration, WIC Income Eligibility Guidelines 2026–2027** https://www.fna.usda.gov/wic/fr-042926 — USDA's official indexed notice dated April 29, 2026, states federal guidelines effective July 1, 2026 and confirms the same 2026–27 maximum income values for the contiguous states. A separate direct web open returned HTTP 403; use the California state implementation as the controlling applicant-facing comparator, not an inference that USDA's page was directly retrievable in every browser.

## Why this is actionable, and limits

An applicant relying solely on Butte County's chart may incorrectly conclude that gross income above the **expired** threshold but at or below the **current** California threshold makes them ineligible. The page's existing link to the state eligibility assessment reduces, but does not eliminate, this information-access risk. We have **no evidence** of a rejected application, incorrect clinic screening, missed benefits, or that the county knowingly retained the chart. WIC eligibility also involves residency, qualifying life stage, nutritional assessment, and possible adjunctive eligibility; an income figure alone does not guarantee benefits. Do not advise anyone to omit information or assume a determination.
