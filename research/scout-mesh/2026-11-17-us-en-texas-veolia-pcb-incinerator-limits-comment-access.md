## Official evidence

EPA Region 6 public notice (published and last updated October 26, 2026): https://www.epa.gov/tx/united-states-environmental-protection-agency-region-6-dallas-texas-proposed-modification

EPA states that Veolia ES Technical Solutions, LLC in Jefferson County, Texas requested June 30, 2026 changes to certain PCB incinerator operating limits established after a 2025 trial burn. Requests for a public hearing may be made during the comment period; EPA decides whether to hold one. EPA's named contact is Dr. Sunita Baniya (contact details on official notice).
