## Findings
EPA Region 9 requests comments through **November 18, 2026** on proposed modification of nonhazardous Class I UIC permit **R9UIC-CA1-FY16-1**, for San Joaquin Refining Company's two wastewater injection wells WD-1 and WD-2 in Bakersfield, California. EPA notice and fact sheet agree on deadline. The change would allow a step-rate-test fracture gradient measured at either well to establish maximum allowable surface injection pressure for both. EPA reserves discretion to require a new WD-1 test if subsequent monitoring/test data indicate material formation-response or integrity differences; the draft specifies written notice and a 30-day plan-submission requirement if ordered. Other conditions remain unchanged.

The EPA fact sheet reports WD-1's November 2020 test did not reach formation-parting pressure because of friction and wellhead pressure constraints; WD-2's June 2024 test reached a 0.72 psi/ft fracture gradient. EPA provisionally allowed WD-2 data for WD-1 in March 2025 and proposes continuing that practice based on proximity and similar injection intervals. EPA preliminarily concludes the change protects underground sources of drinking water. This is an agency technical determination, not independent proof of absence of risk or contamination.
