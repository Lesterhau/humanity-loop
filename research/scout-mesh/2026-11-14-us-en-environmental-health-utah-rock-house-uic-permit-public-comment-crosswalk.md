## Verified facts and why they matter

- **Public input due Monday, November 16, 2026.** EPA says *anyone* may email comments or request a public hearing, with issues to be raised, using subject **Draft Permit UT22475-13000** to **gallant.william@epa.gov**. EPA says a hearing will occur if significant public interest warrants one, with 30-day notice. EPA's notice does not state an exact submission cutoff hour/time zone; **do not invent one**. Extensions, if any, are to appear on that notice page. No hearing is confirmed by the notice.
- **Proposed, not operating authorization.** This is a draft UIC Class II permit to convert an existing gas well to disposal of produced water from nearby oil/gas operations in Uintah County on the Uinta and Ouray Indian Reservation. EPA's permit says actual injection additionally requires written authorization to inject (ATI) after pre-injection tests, mechanical integrity, and corrective action. The notice is not evidence injection has commenced or that tribal consultation has/has not occurred.
- **Proposed depth/controls.** The authorized zone is part of the Mesaverde Group at estimated 6,429–7,242 feet; Attachment II proposes initial **1,655 psi** maximum allowable injection pressure (MAIP), subject to step-rate testing and written EPA approval of later changes. The review radius is **0.5 mile**. There is **no maximum cumulative injection volume limit** in Attachment II, but pressure, fluid-source, monitoring and non-endangerment requirements remain. Do not describe this as unlimited pressure or unregulated injection.
- **Five existing wells reviewed in the 0.5-mile area.** Statement of Basis printed p. 11 identifies four plugged-and-abandoned wells and one shut-in well. Cement bond logs (CBLs) were not available for two listed wells. EPA explains its basis for treating plugged well 7-32-10-23 as adequately sealed despite no CBL; absence of a CBL alone does **not** establish leakage.
- **Specific safeguard before injection.** The shut-in **Rock House 6D-32**, 1,463 feet from the proposed injector, lacks a CBL. Draft permit Attachment VII requires CBL/other EPA-approved testing and a report. If adequate, EPA must give written confirmation no further action is needed **before ATI**; if inadequate, a plugging/abandonment or other remediation plan must be approved and implemented **before ATI**. This is a *proposed enforceable prerequisite*, not evidence that testing is already complete or that the well is leaking.
- **Seismic monitoring.** Draft permit Section B.11 requires USGS earthquake alerts within 20 miles and immediate shutdown/report within 24 hours for reported events within 2 miles, with EPA approval before resumption. Annual reporting covers other events within 20 miles. This is not proof earthquakes are caused by the project.

## Plain-language, neutral participation card — qualified review required before external distribution

**EPA is taking public comments through November 16 on a proposed oil-and-gas wastewater injection permit, UT22475-13000, for the Rock House 3-32-10-23 well in Uintah County, Utah.** The draft allows no fixed cumulative injection-volume cap but proposes pressure and monitoring limits.
