# Penobscot Indian Nation North Loop Road project — Section 401 public-participation source check

**Observed:** 2026-11-17 UTC. **Cell:** US-English environmental/civic access cross-cutting scout. **Status:** independently source-reviewed public information; no agency contact, filing, impact or formal private Scout completion.

## Official source and confirmed facts

EPA Region 1 public notice: https://www.epa.gov/me/epas-clean-water-act-cwa-section-401-water-quality-certification-behalf-penobscot-indian-nation
EPA public-notice index (corroborating Nov 28 deadline): https://www.epa.gov/publicnotices/notices-search?f%5B0%5D=location%3ATribal+Lands

EPA reports that on **October 8, 2026**, the **Penobscot Indian Nation** requested Clean Water Act Section 401 water-quality certification for its **North Loop Road Bridge Replacement & Dam Removal Project** in Maine, associated with a US Army Corps of Engineers nationwide permit application. EPA Region 1 is the Section 401 certifying authority for this request; the applicant is the Nation, not an alleged violator. EPA says its possible decisions include certification, certification with conditions, denial or express waiver.

The project description includes removal of a manmade earthen dam, stream realignment, replacement of an undersized stream crossing, and temporary cofferdams. EPA estimates **approximately 264 square feet of temporary impact**; this is a project estimate, not verified ecological harm. The notice says the application itself is **available on request** through EPA contact Haley Miller; this review did not obtain or evaluate it.

**Public comments:** EPA instructs commenters to email **r1cwa401@epa.gov by November 28, 2026, 5 p.m. EST**. People unable to email or requiring other assistance are directed to Haley Miller (617-918-1169). EPA says not to submit confidential business information; audio/video comments require an accompanying written comment, which is treated as the official comment. The notice does not establish that any comment has been received or that the certification has been granted.
