## Verified observations

- CPSC warning **27-080** specifically concerns **certain discontinued Qing Song full-face snorkel masks** sold on **Amazon.com and TikTok.com between January 2019 and August 2026**. The notice does not provide a total unit count.
- The affected masks cover the eyes, nose and mouth with a face screen, have a snorkel tube centered at the top, come in several colors, and are identified by a **distinctive shark-fin-shaped feature on the snorkel tube**. CPSC's official warning includes an image of a child-sized mask and a close-up captioned “Shark Fin” feature. Identification should use those official photos rather than a generic full-face-mask silhouette.
- CPSC warns that these masks can cause labored breathing, loss of consciousness, fluid in the lungs and increased carbon dioxide, creating a drowning hazard.
- CPSC reports **eight incidents** involving acute breathing difficulty, dizziness or loss of consciousness, **one requiring hospitalization**. The notice does **not** attribute any death to this particular Qing Song product.
- Seller named by CPSC: **Shenzhen Songxin Silicone Products Co., Ltd.** The seller **has not agreed to an acceptable recall**. Thus this is a **federal product safety warning, not a cooperative recall with an announced refund or replacement**.
- CPSC urges owners to **stop using the affected masks immediately, dispose of them, and not sell or give them away**. A safety incident can be reported via SaferProducts.gov.
- A separate CPSC warning for **OUSPT** masks has different identification, sales and incident information. Do not combine its units, reports or alleged fatality with Qing Song.
