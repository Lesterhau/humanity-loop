## Verified official-source chronology

1. **October 15, 2026 — narrower company recall, posted by FDA:** Family Farmstead Dairy recalled **Raw Mild Cheddar Cheese**, lot/best-by **7/22/27**, after Maryland sampling detected Shiga toxin-producing *E. coli* O26:H11 and O45:H2. FDA-hosted announcement lists NY, PA, NJ, CT, MD, VA and MA, with possible downstream distribution elsewhere. It explicitly stated no reported associated illnesses *at that time*. Source: [FDA Oct 15 company announcement](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/family-farmstead-dairy-announces-recall-raw-mild-cheddar-cheese-due-potential-e-coli-o26h11-and), accessed via direct FDA web-reader Nov 12.
2. **November 10, 2026 — broader company recall, posted by FDA:** Family Farmstead Dairy recalled **all identified Raw Mild Cheddar and Raw Sharp Cheddar, 8-oz plastic-wrapped packages, with lot/best-by dates 10/16/27 and earlier**, citing **potential *Listeria monocytogenes* contamination**. FDA-hosted company announcement lists retail distribution in **NY, NJ, VT, NH, MA, PA, RI, CT, DE, VA, WV and TN**. The announcement expressly says no other Family Farmstead products are included unless separately identified. Source: [FDA Nov 10 expanded company announcement](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/family-farmstead-dairy-announces-recall-all-cheddar-cheese-products-because-possible-health-risk), accessed directly Nov 12.
3. **Public-health context:** [CDC Listeria and raw-dairy information](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/causes/dairy.html) explains heightened concern for people at risk, including pregnant people and those with weakened immunity. This is general prevention information, not evidence of illness from these specific lots.
4. **Critical deduplication:** CDC's separate [September 25 E. coli outbreak notice](https://www.cdc.gov/ecoli/outbreaks/raw-milk-cheese-09-26/index.html) concerns **Sierra Nevada Cheese Company / Graziers** products, **not Family Farmstead**. Do not transfer its outbreak counts, ages, hospitalizations, recall scope or causal conclusions to Family Farmstead.

## Material consumer-information finding

A consumer or retailer who checked only the **October 15** Family Farmstead notice could wrongly assume the recall covers just **one mild-cheddar lot**. The newer **November 10** notice is broader: it includes **raw sharp cheddar** and the specified packages through **October 16, 2027**. These are **two successive company notices**, not proof FDA's earlier announcement was erroneous when issued. The newer notice should control the product-scope summary until superseded. Long-dated product may still be held in a household or retail inventory; no stock or actual exposure was independently inspected.
