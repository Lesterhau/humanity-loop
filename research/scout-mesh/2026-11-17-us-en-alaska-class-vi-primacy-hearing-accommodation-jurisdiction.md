## Verified facts
EPA proposes, but has NOT finalized, approval of Alaska's request to administer Class VI CO2 geologic-sequestration injection well permitting. If approved, AOGCC would issue/enforce these permits, EXCEPT in Indian country and areas of exclusive federal jurisdiction. Docket EPA-HQ-OW-2026-4588 accepts comments received by December 14, 2026. EPA lists a virtual public hearing December 10, 2026, 3–6 p.m. Alaska Standard Time; the EPA page links hearing registration. The Federal Register directs those seeking interpretation or accessibility accommodations to preregister and describe needs at least one week before the hearing (approximately December 3). Federal Register notice says comments may be posted publicly including personal information. EPA describes technical well safeguards and continuing EPA oversight; proposed transfer of permitting authority does not itself authorize any particular well.

## Distinct civic-access observation
The accommodation request lead time is earlier than the hearing and comment deadlines, and the jurisdiction exception matters for Alaska Native communities. A short neutral public-access explainer could make those distinctions explicit without implying that state primacy extends to Indian country, that EPA approved any injection well, or that the proposed rule is already final.
