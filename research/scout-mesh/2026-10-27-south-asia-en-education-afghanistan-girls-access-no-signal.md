## Dated primary-source observations

1. **UNICEF, 14 August 2026:** More than **2.6 million girls** have been denied secondary education since 2021. The restriction on girls' formal education beyond Grade 6 dates to September 2021; women's university access was suspended in December 2022. UNICEF reports 2025 emergency education support to **more than 3.7 million** public-school children and community-based learning for **442,000** children, **66% girls**. These 2025 participation counts are not proof of secondary-school reopening or current October enrollment. UNICEF estimates up to **20,000 female teachers** and **5,400 female health workers** at risk of loss by 2030, a projection rather than a measured current loss. Primary: https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/more-26-million-girls-afghanistan-have-been-denied-secondary-education-2021 .
2. **UNICEF Afghanistan, 11 October 2026:** Its International Day of the Girl statement reiterated that adolescent girls remain excluded from schooling beyond Grade 6, called for the restriction to be lifted, and described existing collaboration with local communities and partners on education, life skills and child protection. Its **28.7%** child-marriage figure refers to women aged 20–24 married before 18 in the **2022–23** Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey; it is **not** a 2026 marriage incidence statistic. Primary: https://www.unicef.org/afghanistan/press-releases/end-child-marriage-invest-girls-protect-their-futures-unicef-afghanistan .
3. **UNICEF Afghanistan, 13 May 2025 (historical implementation example):** UNICEF/EU-funded community-based learning in Herat included a 28-child Grade 3 class and supplied teachers and materials. UNICEF described multiple established donor partners and **more than 15,000** supported community-based education classes. These 2025 implementation examples demonstrate an existing partner channel; they do **not** prove all girls have access, and primary learning is not a lawful substitute for reopening secondary education. Primary: https://www.unicef.org/afghanistan/stories/keeping-hope-alive-afghanistans-community-classes .
