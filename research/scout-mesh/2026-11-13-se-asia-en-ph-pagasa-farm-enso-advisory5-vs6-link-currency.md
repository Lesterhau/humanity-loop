# Philippines PAGASA: farm-weather ENSO link points to October Advisory 5 despite November Advisory 6

**Observed:** 2026-11-13 ~01:36 UTC (09:36 Philippine Standard Time), public read-only source review.  
**Cell:** se-asia-id-civic (public information / agriculture / climate).  
**Decision:** verified public-information currency / navigation candidate; **not** a demonstrated meteorological forecast error, agency contact, or beneficiary outcome.  
**Distribution:** UNSENT, human/editorial review required.

## Exact primary-source evidence

1. **Current PAGASA Farm Weather Forecast**, issued **07:00 PHT 13 Nov 2026**, valid to **07:00 PHT 14 Nov**: https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/agri-weather . The page's ENSO block says **“Updated as of 04 November 2026”**, describes a *very strong El Niño*, but its explicit PDF URL is https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/climate/monthlyclimateassessment/EN_Advisory_5.pdf .
2. **That linked PDF is Advisory No. 5**, signed and **issued 07 October 2026**. It describes a *strong to very strong* El Niño, **September 2026 assessment** and **October 2026 outlook**, including geographic dry-spell forecasts specific to October. Source: same PDF, pp. 1–2.
3. **PAGASA's official ENSO Advisories index**: https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/el-nino-la-nina/advisories . It separately lists **Advisory No. 6 issued 03 November 2026** (and Advisory No. 5 issued 07 October).
4. **Newer Advisory No. 6**, dated **03 November 2026**, official full PDF: https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/climate/elninolanina/ENSO_Advisory6_OS.pdf . It explicitly describes a **very strong** El Niño, **October 2026 assessment** and **November 2026 outlook**. The November outlook forecasts widespread below-normal rainfall except near-normal in Davao del Norte, Davao City and Agusan del Norte; by end-November meteorological drought is forecast in **Albay, Catanduanes, Capiz, Negros Oriental, Samar and Siquijor** (p. 2). This is a *forecast*, not observed November drought.
5. **Official simplified Advisory No. 6**: https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/climate/elninolanina/11052026_MCAO_EN_Advisory_6.pdf . It also carries **03 November 2026** issuance and November outlook.

## What is actually different

| Element | Farm-weather-linked No. 5 | Newer official No. 6 |
|---|---|---|
| Issue date | 07 Oct 2026 | 03 Nov 2026 |
| ENSO wording | strong to very strong | very strong |
| Assessment month | September | October |
| Forecast/outlook month | October | November |
| Access path | Direct link in live 13 Nov Farm Weather ENSO block | Official ENSO Advisories index, available separately |

**Narrow finding:** The farm-weather page's ENSO block has current **04 Nov** wording but directs users to a PDF containing the **October** outlook, even though a newer **November** outlook is already publicly available on PAGASA's official advisories page. The farm-weather daily weather forecast itself is current; this does **not** show its day-ahead forecast is stale. The older PDF is an authentic archival publication and may remain intentionally accessible. The link target / missing issue-date label is the candidate improvement.

**Public-interest significance:** A farmer or local communicator following the direct farm-weather ENSO PDF link may accidentally treat October regional drought forecasts as the latest November outlook. A current ENSO state does not rule out local heavy rain or flooding: Advisory 6 expressly preserves tropical-cyclone/rain-bearing-system exceptions, and the Nov 13 farm forecast still lists scattered rain/thunderstorms in multiple regions.
