## Verified facts and technical distinction
- Permit **R9UIC-CA1-FY16-1** concerns two Class I non-hazardous wastewater injection wells, **WD-1** and **WD-2**, at San Joaquin Refining's Bakersfield facility. EPA proposes allowing a step-rate-test fracture gradient from **either** well to establish the maximum allowable surface injection pressure (MASIP) for **both** wells.
- EPA's fact sheet says the wells are ~40 feet apart at surface and ~337 feet apart at bottom-hole locations, with similar injection intervals. WD-1's November 2020 SRT did not reach formation parting pressure because of test pressure/friction constraints; the highest gradient attained was **0.65 psi/ft**. WD-2's June 2024 SRT reached **0.72 psi/ft**. These figures are different measurements and do not by themselves establish unsafe pressure or a legal violation.
- EPA issued a **temporary March 10, 2025** determination allowing WD-2's gradient for WD-1 pending a new WD-1 SRT. Because the WD-1 test constraints remained unresolved, the operator requested the present permit modification. EPA preliminarily finds the proposed change protective of underground drinking-water sources.
- Draft text reserves EPA's ability to require a new WD-1 SRT if monitoring/test data reveal material differences in formation response or integrity; after written notice, the operator must submit an SRT plan within **30 days** and test on the approved schedule. All other permit conditions remain unchanged.
- **Public comments due November 18, 2026**. EPA explicitly permits email to **R9UICPermits@epa.gov**, copying **rumrill.nancy@epa.gov**. Accommodation or language assistance is available through the EPA contact on the notice. No exact closing clock hour was identified on the reviewed notice. A public hearing may be requested in writing, identifying issues; none was scheduled in the fact sheet.
