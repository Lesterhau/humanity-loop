# CPSC recall 27-082 — Allstar Sharper Image tabletop fire pits (public-source safety cross-check)

**Observation:** 2026-11-15 UTC. **Lane:** US English consumer safety; read-only official-source investigation, UNSENT editorial draft. **Status:** verified CPSC notice; no independent retailer inventory or refund-portal test; no consumer contact or outcome.

## Primary source and exact scope

US Consumer Product Safety Commission, published November 12, 2026: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Allstar-Recalls-Sharper-Image-Tabletop-and-Infinity-Fire-Pits-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Burn-and-Fire-Hazards

- Recall number **27-082**, about **265,000** outdoor **Sharper Image-branded Tabletop Fire Pits and Tabletop Infinity Fire Pits**, importer Allstar Marketing Group LLC.
- The official notice identifies a **77 logo** on the front and a lot-code sticker on the base, including **KS-CY or KS-FC** and a YYYY-MM-DD date code. Do not imply every product bearing the Sharper Image brand is affected; match the CPSC product photographs/description and consult the recall operator.
- Hazard: ignited flammable liquid may escape or cause **flame jetting** from fuel containers, risking severe burns/fire.
- **Four reported incidents of ignited fuel escaping; four injuries, including third- and fourth-degree burns.** These are CPSC's reported figures as of the notice, not a forecast of future cases.
- Sold at multiple national retailers and online January 2023–June 2026, approximately $30–$50.

## Bounded, unsent consumer-safety wording for qualified editorial review

CPSC recalled certain Sharper Image tabletop fire pits and Infinity tabletop fire pits (recall 27-082) on November 12, 2026, because burning fuel can escape or jet from the container. Stop using affected units immediately. Consult CPSC's official photographs and identifying lot-code details. CPSC says Allstar offers a full refund through its recall process. Do not handle or dispose of remaining fuel casually: follow the official recall instructions and local hazardous-waste requirements. The company asks consumers to register, mark the product and provide three photographs before disposing of the unit. See the official CPSC notice for current contact and instructions.
