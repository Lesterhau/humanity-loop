Observed: 2026-11-16 UTC. Scope: public primary-source review only; no environmental violation or actual discharge increase inferred.

## Official evidence
- EPA public notice (published October 21, 2026): https://www.epa.gov/me/draft-permit-modification-city-brewer-water-pollution-control-facility-brewer-me-me0100072
- Maine DEP draft modification and supporting permit (October 21, 2026): https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-10/draftme0100072permitmod-2026.pdf

The Maine DEP's proposed modification to MEPDES ME0100072 would increase **authorized transported waste** from **138,000 to 156,000 gallons/day** (+18,000; approximately +13.0%), with landfill leachate reserved capacity rising from **60,000 to 78,000 gallons/day** (+18,000; +30.0%). The remaining transported-waste allowance is unchanged at 78,000 gallons/day by subtraction. The underlying December 2, 2024 permit authorized a monthly average **5.19 million gallons/day** of secondary-treated discharge to the Class B Penobscot River. The proposed action expressly retains other unmodified permit conditions. The order is labeled **PROPOSED MODIFICATION** and has a blank signature date; its 'grants'/'approves' draft language does **not** prove a final effective authorization.

The official transmittal says comments must be **received by close of business Friday, November 20, 2026**. It specifies physical written comments to Laura Crossley, Maine DEP, Bureau of Water Quality, Division of Water Quality Management, 17 State House Station, Augusta, ME 04333-0017. Bekah Farmer's email and phone are given for questions, but the letter does **not explicitly confirm emailed comments as an accepted filing method**. Exact close-of-business hour and any subsequent extension were not independently verified.
