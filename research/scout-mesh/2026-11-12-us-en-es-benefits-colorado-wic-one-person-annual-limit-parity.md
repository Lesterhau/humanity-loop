**Observed:** 2026-11-12 UTC (public-source web reader, not an uncached human browser)  
**Scout lane:** US / English + Spanish / public-benefits access  
**Disposition:** verified public-page numerical mismatch; proposed correction **UNSENT**; not evidence of incorrect eligibility decisions or participant harm.

## Official sources checked

1. Colorado WIC English applicant income chart, headed “2026-2027 Income Eligibility Guidelines,” effective July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027: https://www.coloradowic.gov/2026-2027-income-eligibility-guidelines . Direct web-reader retrieval on Nov 12 displayed household size **1, YEARLY $29,536**, MONTHLY $2,461, TWICE-MONTHLY $1,231, BI-WEEKLY $1,136, WEEKLY $568. Other listed household-size rows 2–8 match USDA's corresponding 185%-of-poverty figures in the checked comparison.
2. Colorado WIC Spanish applicant income chart, headed “2026-2027 Pautas de Ingresos de WIC,” same effective dates: https://www.coloradowic.gov/es/2026-2027-pautas-de-ingresos-de-wic . Web-reader retrieval on Nov 12 (underlying indexed page marked crawled last month) displayed household size **1, Annales $29,536**, and the same other frequency values. This is a second language presentation of the *same* one-cell mismatch, not a separate underlying policy defect.
3. USDA Food and Nutrition Administration, *WIC Income Eligibility Guidelines 2026-2027*, published Apr 29, effective July 1, 2026: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/wic/fr-042926 . Direct web-reader retrieval on Nov 12 displayed the 48-state/DC/territories **185% maximum** for household size **1** as **annual $29,526**, monthly $2,461, twice-monthly $1,231, biweekly $1,136, weekly $568. The notice explains states may align WIC income guidelines with qualifying state/local health-care guidelines but cannot exceed the reduced-price-meal (185%) ceiling.
