## INTEND / narrow question

Check whether Indonesia's 2026 digital identity (Identitas Kependudukan Digital, IKD) requirement for **self-service Anjungan Dukcapil Mandiri (ADM) document-printing kiosks** is being inaccurately conflated with a requirement to possess a smartphone for **all** civil-registration services. Check official Indonesian-language primary sources for explicit non-smartphone alternatives before proposing any intervention. Do not submit registrations, test identity/biometrics, obtain citizen records or contact agencies.

## Source-verified evidence

1. **Yogyakarta city civil-registration agency, 28 April 2026:** Its official article states that **self-service ADM printing** requires IKD starting in 2026 and reports **18.64% IKD activation** in the city as of April. Crucially, it also explicitly says physical KTP remains valid, and residents who cannot activate IKD because they lack a suitable device or cannot operate one are **excepted** and may use in-person Disdukcapil services. The city describes 169 local GISA cadres as additional help. This is an agency account of policy and accommodation, **not** independent evidence that every exception works in practice. https://dindukcapil.jogjakota.go.id/detail/index/48179
2. **Landak regency civil-registration agency relaying national Dukcapil data, 16 September 2026:** Reported 22.48 million IKD activations as of September 14, representing **23.76% of the approximately 94.65 million** people with a smartphone and completed dynamic KTP-el recording. This denominator differs from the entire KTP-eligible population. The agency also describes outreach and physical KTP issuance, including 22 million physical-card blanks procured in 2026. Figures are attributed government statistics, not audited user-access outcomes. https://disdukcapil.landakkab.go.id/2026/09/16/dukcapil-kemendagri-perkuat-layanan-kependudukan-digital-dan-integrasi-data-nasional/
3. **Kutai Timur, Sukamaju village government, 13 October 2026:** Its local digital social-protection registration notice includes a distinct assisted enrollment route via official agents for people without smartphones and older residents. This is a **different service**, included only as counterevidence to a blanket claim that Indonesian digital public services universally require smartphone ownership. https://desasukamaju.kutaitimurkab.go.id/berita/pelaksanaan-program-piloting-digitalisasi-perlindungan-sosial
4. **Banyuwangi civil-registration agency, 22 January 2026:** Describes welfare registration via KTP-el/family card and field agents for people without phones, another program-specific accommodation. It does not prove Yogyakarta kiosk usability or national coverage. https://disdukcapil.banyuwangikab.go.id/berita/pemanfaatan-ikd-untuk-digitalisasi-bansos

## Novelty, existing solutions and uncertainty

**Observed distinction:** A mandatory credential for a *particular self-service kiosk workflow* does not establish a mandatory credential for *all* civil-registration access. Yogyakarta's own official article explicitly describes exceptions and human-assisted channels. ## 10th-Man / falsification

The government may have adequate offline routes, or those routes may be inaccessible in particular locations; neither outcome is proven by these announcements. A claim that physical IDs are invalid or non-smartphone residents cannot access civil registration would **contradict the official cited Yogyakarta policy**. A future independently documented refused counter service, an inaccessible GISA route, or credible service-performance data could justify a narrower investigation; a general anti-digitization campaign would not.
