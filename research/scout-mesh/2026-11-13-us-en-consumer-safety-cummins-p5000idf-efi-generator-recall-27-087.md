## Primary sources actually checked

1. **US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)**, [recall 27-087](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Cummins-Recalls-Portable-Generators-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards), recall date **2026-11-12**. Direct web-reader open succeeded on 2026-11-13. CPSC reports about **2,960** affected Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel portable generators. Improperly installed fuel-system vent valves may prevent gasoline vapors escaping, build pressure, discharge fuel or eject the fuel cap, creating fire/burn risk. The firm reports **11** fuel-discharge/cap-ejection incidents, including **one person splashed in the face with fuel**; no medical attention reported. CPSC directs consumers to **stop using recalled generators** and arrange a **free authorized repair**. Retail dates May 2025–August 2026. 
2. **Cummins manufacturer**, [Safety Campaign #7308](https://www.cummins.com/en-na/na/generators/portable-generators/safety-recalls/campaigns/7308), official-domain search-index text crawled **2026-11-13**. This manufacturer page specifies the precise recalled combination **model P5000iDF EFI + part number A075C500** (both on side label); identifies an EVAP valve inspection/reinstallation, tank inspection and fuel-hose routing change. It directs consumers to arrange no-charge repair through an authorized service location and **call before transporting** to confirm the location can perform Campaign 7308. **Direct open returned an internal error**, so the manufacturer's current uncached browser presentation and images were not independently confirmed. The CPSC page directly links this same campaign URL.
3. **Cummins manufacturer recall index**, [Portable Generator Product Safety Recalls](https://www.cummins.com/en-na/na/generators/portable-generators/safety-recalls), official-domain indexed text reviewed 2026-11-13, identifies Campaign 7308 and 1-800-286-6467 as recall contact.

## Narrow, source-grounded safety implications

- **Identification is model AND part number, not all Cummins portable generators.** Match both **P5000iDF EFI** and **A075C500** against the generator's side label; verify against the official campaign or Cummins rather than assuming every visually similar unit is recalled.
- **Stop using an affected generator now.** The authorized remedy is a **free service repair**, not an announced refund or consumer do-it-yourself modification.
- **Transport qualification matters.** The manufacturer campaign's indexed text instructs owners to let the generator cool, keep it upright/secured, and **not transport or further handle a generator with an abnormally swollen/bulged fuel tank, gasoline smell, or visible fuel leak**. Keep it away from flames/sparks and contact Cummins for specific guidance. These transport instructions are from the manufacturer page's official-domain indexed text; they are not separately stated in the CPSC excerpt. Do not direct owners to attempt repairs or move a leaking unit.
- **No verified injuries requiring medical attention** is not evidence the hazard is harmless. No repair completion, owner notification, consumer adoption, or injury prevention is established by this research.
