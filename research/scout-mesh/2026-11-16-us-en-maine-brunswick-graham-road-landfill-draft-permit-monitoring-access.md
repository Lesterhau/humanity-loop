Observed: 2026-11-16 UTC. Status: UNSENT research/evidence; no agency contact or public filing.

## Primary sources
- EPA notice (published 2026-10-19): https://www.epa.gov/me/draft-permit-town-brunswick-graham-road-landfill-brunswick-me-me0102113
- Maine DEP proposed renewal and fact sheet (57-page EPA-hosted PDF): https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-10/draftme0102113permit-2026.pdf

## Verified findings
1. Maine DEP's October 19 transmittal states comments must be **received by close of business Thursday, November 19, 2026** (PDF p.1); the EPA landing page lists the same public-comment period. The transmittal requests written comments sent to Maine DEP, Bureau of Water Quality, 17 State House Station, Augusta ME 04333-0017. It does **not expressly establish an email comment submission channel or exact cutoff hour**. Verify before filing.
2. The proposed renewal carries forward authorization for up to **0.30 million gallons/day monthly average** of treated landfill leachate and stormwater to the **Class B Androscoggin River** from outfall 001A (PDF pp.3, 5). This is a proposed permit, not proof of new or increased discharge.
3. Maine DEP proposes reduced E. coli limits: **64 per 100 mL monthly geometric mean** and **236 per 100 mL daily maximum**, with seasonal monitoring **April 15–October 31** (PDF pp.3, 7, 9). Units may be MPN or CFU per 100 mL as specified. Mercury interim limitations are incorporated; the table lists **4.5 ng/L monthly average, 6.8 ng/L daily maximum**, monitored annually (PDF p.7).
4. Monitoring footnote (PDF p.9) says discharges resulting from weather events **may occur without being monitored**; ordinary monitoring is conditioned on discharge on normal monitoring days. This is a permit-text observation, **not evidence of unlawful discharge, missed required monitoring, contamination, or public harm**.
