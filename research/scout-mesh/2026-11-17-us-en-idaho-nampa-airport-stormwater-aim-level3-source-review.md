## Specific findings
EPA alleges that Nampa Municipal Airport failed to implement corrective action following eight stormwater visual assessments between October 22, 2022 and April 3, 2024 (agreement ¶3.29, printed p.6). EPA further alleges TSS AIM Level 1 triggers in 2022 Q2 and 2023 Q4; E. coli AIM Level 2 in 2023 Q2; TSS AIM Level 2 in 2024 Q2; and an E. coli AIM Level 3 trigger in 2024 Q3 after a benchmark exceedance greater than fourfold (¶¶3.32–3.38, printed pp.7–8). The agreement alleges missing associated corrective actions and annual-report documentation. **Benchmark exceedance is not itself proof of an ambient water-quality standard violation or human exposure.**

Critical temporal qualification: EPA's proposed agreement states the airport disconnected its remaining outfall and ceased discharge to Mason Creek on **December 18, 2025**, submitted permit termination notice January 22, 2026, and IDEQ acknowledged termination January 26 (¶3.24, printed p.5). Therefore do not characterize the historical discharges as continuing in November 2026 without independent evidence.

EPA's proposed penalty is **$21,538.77**; public comments due **December 10, 2026**, docket **CWA-10-2026-0279**. The respondent neither admits nor denies the specific factual allegations (¶4.2). Proposed consent agreement is not evidence that a final order has become effective, payment was made, or environmental remediation verified.
