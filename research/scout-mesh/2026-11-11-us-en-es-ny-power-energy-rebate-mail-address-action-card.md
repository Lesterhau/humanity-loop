## What the New York Tax Department says

The New York State Department of Taxation and Finance's [POWER check guidance](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/energy-rebate-check.htm), marked **updated October 20, 2026**, says eligible 2024 full-year New York resident income-tax filers receive an automatic, **mailed paper check** in fall 2026. Mailings extend over several weeks; the department does **not** provide an individual delivery schedule. POWER is distinct from the 2025 inflation-refund check.

To qualify, a person must have **timely filed 2024 NY resident Form IT-201**, not been claimed as another taxpayer's dependent for 2024, and meet the applicable income limit. A timely return was filed by April 15, 2025, or October 15, 2025 under a valid extension. **Part-year residents and people who did not file the qualifying 2024 resident return do not qualify under the published criteria.**

| 2024 filing status | NY adjusted gross income (IT-201 line 33) | State-published one-time check |
|---|---:|---:|
| Single, head of household, married filing separately | $150,000 or less | $100 |
| Married filing jointly or qualifying surviving spouse | $150,000 or less | $200 |
| Married filing jointly or qualifying surviving spouse | More than $150,000, through $300,000 | $150 |

Do not extrapolate a payment to filing statuses or income bands not listed by the state.

## One useful action for a household

**No application is needed.** No utility-bill copies are required. The department mails checks to the **most recent mailing address on file**, which can be newer than the 2024 return address. If the address is outdated, use the state's official [address-change instructions](https://www.tax.ny.gov/help/contact/address.htm) and select **Individual change of address** inside the NY Tax Online Services account. Do not enter tax details into an unsolicited link.

The department's [official mail and fraud guidance](https://www.tax.ny.gov/help/letters/) lists POWER checks as currently mailed and directs people to report suspicious contacts impersonating tax officials. A request to **pay an application or release fee** is inconsistent with the state's automatic-payment description. The state says checks are **not** direct-deposited even if the filer normally receives tax refunds by direct deposit. This resource is not an eligibility determination, payment tracker, or substitute for official advice.

## Resumen en español — traducción informativa pendiente de revisión

El Departamento de Impuestos de Nueva York indica que el cheque de reembolso energético **POWER de 2026 se envía automáticamente por correo postal** a quienes cumplan los requisitos; **no hay que solicitarlo ni presentar facturas de servicios públicos**. La elegibilidad se basa en la declaración de impuestos de residente del estado de Nueva York correspondiente a **2024**, presentada a tiempo, los ingresos y la condición de dependiente. Los montos publicados son **$100 o $200** para los grupos indicados arriba, o **$150** para ciertas declaraciones conjuntas. El cheque se enviará a la dirección postal más reciente registrada ante el Departamento. Si cambió de domicilio, consulte directamente las instrucciones oficiales en https://www.tax.ny.gov/help/contact/address.htm. La información completa y los requisitos exactos están en https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/energy-rebate-check.htm.
