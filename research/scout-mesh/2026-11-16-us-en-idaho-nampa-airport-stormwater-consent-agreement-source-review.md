# Nampa Municipal Airport stormwater settlement — primary-source public participation review
Observed 2026-11-16 UTC. Status: UNSENT, public-safe Tier-0 source review; not an accepted contribution or finding of ongoing contamination.

## Primary sources
- EPA notice (published 2026-11-10): https://www.epa.gov/publicnotices/proposed-settlement-nampa-municipal-airport-clean-water-act-violations-idaho
- EPA proposed 12-page consent agreement, docket CWA-10-2026-0279: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-11/cwa-10-2026-0279-nampa.pdf

## Verified crosswalk
1. EPA notice invites written comments by **2026-12-10**, directed by email to **R10_RHC@epa.gov**, referencing **CWA-10-2026-0279**. An alternative telephone contact is supplied if email is not possible. Exact time cutoff and later extensions were not independently verified.
2. The EPA *alleges* stormwater permit violations, including missed corrective actions and annual-report documentation for total suspended solids (TSS) and E. coli. The agreement states that the City of Nampa neither admits nor denies the specific factual allegations (agreement §4.2, PDF p.8).
3. A consequential temporal distinction: agreement §3.24 (PDF p.5) says the airport **disconnected its remaining outfall and ceased discharging to Mason Creek on 2025-12-18**; Idaho DEQ acknowledged termination of MSGP coverage on **2026-01-26**. Thus the proposed settlement is not proof of ongoing discharges or ongoing pollution in November 2026.
4. Agreement §§3.29–3.38 (PDF pp.6–8) identify eight visual-assessment corrective-action failures between 2022-10-22 and 2024-04-03, TSS AIM Level 1 and 2 triggers, E. coli AIM Level 2 and 3 triggers, and an alleged E. coli benchmark exceedance of more than fourfold in 2024 Q3. A benchmark exceedance is not, by itself, proof of a receiving-water health impact.
5. The proposed penalty is **$21,538.77** (§4.3); payment is due 30 days after a Final Order filing (§4.4), not automatically from the notice date. The notice does not establish that a final order has been entered or a penalty paid.

## Public-interest utility and next unchecked action
For accurate civic explanation, distinguish alleged historical permit/reporting failures from current discharge status; avoid implying that airport runoff is presently reaching Mason Creek. Qualified environmental/legal review should assess the complete final-order status, current drainage controls, and any independent water-quality evidence before any externally published statement or separately authorized contact.
