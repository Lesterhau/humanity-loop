## Independently checked evidence
1. **PJM, 2026-08-11:** On July 22, a normally cleared 230-kV line fault in northern Virginia was followed by an unexpected transfer of about **3,800 MW** of computational/data-center load to backup generation, in two waves (approximately 2,970 MW then 1,099 MW). PJM reports transient voltage/frequency excursions and an operator response; **no significant unresolved grid-reliability impact** was reported. PJM also described approximately 1,500 MW transfer events in 2024 and 2025. Primary account: https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-dominion-review-large-load-transfer-event/
2. **PJM, 2026-09-10:** PJM proposed new large-computational-load voltage/frequency **ride-through** requirements, including existing-facility transition planning; it expected to file solutions with FERC in November. This is an **existing response**, not a missing intervention invented by Humanity Loop: https://insidelines.pjm.com/reliability-standards-to-manage-large-load-disconnection-events-proposed-by-pjm/
3. **PJM, 2026-09-08 technical presentation:** Specific voltage/frequency envelopes, post-fault recovery requirements, ERCOT/MISO/NERC comparison and staged 2026–27 review are already publicly proposed; these are proposals, not verified enforceable/implemented requirements. https://www.pjm.com/-/media/DotCom/committees-groups/committees/pc/2026/20260908/20260908-item-05---1-large-load-ride-through---presentation.pdf
4. **IEA 4E/EDNA, 2026-07-01:** Separately assesses market-serving, grid-serving and system-serving data-center flexibility and operational/economic constraints. Demand flexibility is not interchangeable with safe fault ride-through. https://www.iea-4e.org/edna/publications/data-centres-and-flexibility/
5. **Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, September 2026:** Its large-load literature review already categorizes reliability, tariffs, flexibility and cost impacts, reinforcing the duplication check. https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/publications/2026-large-load-literature-review

## Interpretation and falsification
**Emerging issue:** Rapid, correlated load disconnections during otherwise manageable disturbances can create a grid-stability externality even where data centers seek to protect their own operations. The July event and 2024–25 comparators are documented, but **frequency, transfer causes across all facilities, actual outage probabilities, international generality, and post-proposal implementation are not established** by this review. Do not extrapolate the 3,800 MW figure to all grids or imply a blackout occurred.
