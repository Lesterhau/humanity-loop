# India NMMSS 2026–27: source-verified deadline and applicant/verification action card

**Observed:** 2026-11-11 UTC. **Scout cell:** `south-asia-en-education`. **Type:** time-sensitive education-access resource / reusable public-source action card. **Status:** official-source-checked, human-review candidate; not an agency publication, an application, or evidence of a student receiving funds.

## The useful action (as of 11 November 2026)

For the **National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS)**, the Indian Ministry of Education's **31 October 2026** notice extends the AY 2026–27 deadlines to:

| Responsibility | Latest stated deadline | Who should act |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submit fresh/renewal application on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) | **15 November 2026** | Eligible student/guardian; use the official portal |
| Institute Nodal Officer (L1) verifies submitted application | **30 November 2026** | School/institute |
| District Nodal Officer (L2) verifies | **15 December 2026** | District authority |

**Action for students/guardians:** Check eligibility and the specific scheme entry on the official [NSP scheme listing](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships); complete required [One-Time Registration (OTR)](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students) and any application well before **15 November**; retain the submission acknowledgement; check with the school that L1 verification occurs by **30 November**. Never send Aadhaar, passwords, bank details or student records to Humanity Loop. An application submitted is not an approved scholarship.

**Action for schools:** Check pending NMMSS applications and verify through the authorized institute workflow by **30 November**, without asking students to use unofficial intermediaries. The district-level deadline is a separate subsequent step; it does not extend the student's application window.

**If the NSP login or scheme entry shows a different status:** Trust the authenticated scheme-specific workflow for your own application and request clarification from the relevant official NSP/institute nodal channel; do **not** assume a press release guarantees that a form is currently accepting submissions. This research did not log into any account or test actual form submission.

## Primary-source evidence and supersession

1. **Controlling later ministry notice:** Government of India Press Information Bureau (PIB), Ministry of Education, [31 October 2026, release 2330936](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2330936&lang=1&reg=3): explicitly gives **15 Nov / 30 Nov / 15 Dec** for student/L1/L2 respectively. It reports **90,483 fresh and 199,531 renewal applications** as of 30 October, but those counts are ministry-reported, not independently audited by Humanity Loop.
2. **Earlier superseded notice:** PIB Ministry of Education [1 October 2026, release 2317657](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2317657&lang=2&reg=48): listed **31 Oct / 15 Nov / 30 Nov**. The 31 October notice is later and expressly extends the student deadline; do not reuse the older dates.
3. **Official scheme-specific public listing:** [NSP All Scholarships](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships), observed 11 November: NMMSS row lists **15-11-2026** student application, **30-11-2026** institute verification, **15-12-2026** DNO/SNO/MNO verification. This corroborates the later ministry notice.
4. **Official student entry:** [NSP Students](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students), observed 11 November: OTR required for applications, including renewals. The same page carries a *different* PM-USP CSSS renewal deadline announcement, which is a **separate scheme** and has an unresolved conflict with its scheme-specific listing. Do not import PM-USP dates into NMMSS.

## Scope and eligibility guardrails

The ministry describes NMMSS as **₹12,000 per year** for eligible pupils in government, government-aided and local-body schools; **one lakh fresh scholarships annually** for selected Class IX students, with renewal through Classes X–XII subject to conditions. For the selection examination, the ministry cites **at least 55% in Class VII** (five percentage points' relaxation for SC/ST) and **parental income not above ₹3.50 lakh per year**. These are summary conditions, not a complete eligibility determination; students should use the official scheme specifications and state/UT selection rules. No claim is made that every student is eligible or that the application will be approved.

## Existing solutions / why this artifact is limited

The **official PIB notice and NSP portal already provide the answer**. Humanity Loop is not creating a competing scholarship service. The marginal utility of this small public-safe card is (a) putting student, school and district deadlines on one page, (b) explicitly showing the October 1 notice was superseded on October 31, and (c) separating this NMMSS deadline from the previously documented **PM-USP CSSS** date conflict. This is an ephemeral signpost, not a replacement for the official application or a new detector.
