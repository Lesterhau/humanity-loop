[Senego, 6 Oct](https://senego.com/seneau-redemarrage-partiel-des-ouvrages-et-amelioration-progressive-de-la-desserte-a-dakar_2009799.html).

This concerns broader flood/sanitation resilience; **do not conflate it with the potable-water pump outage or a finding against SEN'EAU**. [Government of Senegal, 13 Oct](https://www.primature.sn/publications/conseil-des-ministres/conseil-des-ministres-du-13-octobre-2026).
5. **21 October operator publication:** SEN'EAU states that a **30 September** tripartite agreement with SONES and the Dakar Youth Olympic Games organizing committee formalized two years of collaboration on safe, continuous water supply for the event. This is evidence of **existing planning and coordination**, not an independently verified operational performance test. [SEN'EAU, published 21 Oct](https://www.seneau.sn/joj-dakar-2026-le-cojoj-seneau-et-sones-signent-une-convention-tripartite-pour-securiser-lalimentation-en-eau-potable/).
6. **31 October–13 November:** IOC confirms the scheduled Youth Olympic Games window. The event is a time-bound test of resilience, but **no evidence here establishes that it will worsen service for residents**. [IOC media kit](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/media/dakar-2026-media-kit).
