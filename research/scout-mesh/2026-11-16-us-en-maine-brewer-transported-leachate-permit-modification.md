# Maine Brewer wastewater transported-waste modification — primary-source civic-access check

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-16 UTC. **Status:** UNSENT, human-review-only Tier-0 source crosswalk. **Scope:** distinct from Fort Fairfield ME0100226.

## Official source and finding
Maine DEP's October 21, 2026 proposed modification to City of Brewer's MEPDES permit **ME0100072** / WDL **W002679-5M-L-M** requests an increase in authorized **transported waste** from **138,000 to 156,000 gallons/day**, with the portion reserved for **landfill leachate** increasing **60,000 to 78,000 gallons/day**. The non-leachate transported-waste allowance remains **78,000 gallons/day** by subtraction. Thus the 18,000-gpd increase is entirely allocated to the leachate reservation; it is not a proposed increase to the facility's separately authorized 5.19-MGD monthly-average secondary-treated discharge. The proposed modification retains other December 2024 permit conditions. The document's heading 'MODIFICATION GRANTED' and unsigned proposed order should not be mistaken for an already-effective final approval; the signature/date is blank.

**Public input:** Maine DEP's transmittal states written comments must be **received by close of business Friday, November 20, 2026**, and specifies physical written comments to Laura Crossley, Maine DEP Bureau of Water Quality, Division of Water Quality Management, 17 State House Station, Augusta ME 04333-0017. It lists a staff email for questions, **not an expressly authorized email filing method**. Exact close-of-business hour, later revisions and actual submission receipt are unverified.
