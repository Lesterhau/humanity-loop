# New York State Regular HEAP public service page: 2026–27 season-year and benefit-table mismatch

**Review date:** 2026-11-12 UTC. **Scope:** Public official pages only; English, New York State, heating-assistance information. **Status:** Verified official-public-text inconsistency; **UNSENT** editorial correction proposal, not institutional outreach or a beneficiary outcome.

## Source-to-source observations

1. **State of New York main public service page:** https://www.ny.gov/services/apply-heating-assistance-heap — live official-domain web-reader fetch on 2026-11-12 (lines 115–117) says: “The **2025-2026** Regular HEAP benefit is scheduled to open **November 2, 2026**.” That year label is inconsistent with the opening date and current season. Its Regular Benefit Amounts section (lines 139–149) is explicitly labeled **2025-2026**, and lists **$900** for oil/kerosene/propane and **$635** for wood/pellets/coal/corn. The **same page** (lines 164–183) correctly labels the income limits **2026-2027**, including household size 1 **$3,563** and size 4 **$6,852**. Its Emergency section (line 225) correctly says 2026–27 Emergency HEAP is **scheduled** to open January 4, 2027; the following benefit table is explicitly 2025–26 and should not be presented as a current-season quote.

2. **Onondaga County Department of Social Services:** https://onondaga.gov/dss/heap/ — official county source, web-reader crawled November 11, states **2026–27 Regular HEAP opened Monday November 2, 2026** and gives the correct non-NYC application route (myBenefits or local DSS).

3. **NYC ACCESS NYC:** https://access.nyc.gov/programs/home-energy-assistance-program-heap/ — official city source, last updated November 2, web-reader checked November 12; states **2026–27 Regular HEAP opened November 2, apply now**, and **2026–27 Emergency HEAP scheduled January 4, 2027**. This is a city implementation confirmation, not proof of every county's processing.

4. **Erie County HEAP Regular Benefits:** https://www3.erie.gov/heap/regular-benefits — official county source, web-reader crawled November 10; explicitly labels **anticipated 2026–27** Regular HEAP benefit amounts **$990 + add-ons** for oil/kerosene/propane and **$700 + add-ons** for wood/pellets/coal/corn, with electric/natural-gas **$400 + add-ons**. The county uses “anticipated,” so these values should be confirmed against current OTDA desk guide before any definitive statewide benefit-amount correction.

5. **New York State Senate public notice:** https://www.nysenate.gov/newsroom/articles/2026/cordell-cleare/important-home-energy-assistance-program-heap-opens-nov-2 — state legislative official site, October 2026, repeats the 2026–27 opening and $990/$700 delivered-fuel figures. This corroborates the county amounts, but is not the underlying OTDA administrative benefit schedule.

## Narrow finding

The state service page appears **partially updated**: current 2026–27 income limits and emergency-opening date coexist with a **wrong 2025–26 year label attached to November 2, 2026**, and a **prior-year Regular HEAP benefit table**. The latter is explicitly historical, so do **not** call it a mathematically wrong 2026–27 table; the public-facing defect is **current-season omission/ambiguity** in the main application guidance. The original page's 2025–26 opening sentence is independently inconsistent with the already-open 2026–27 season.

The live reader and indexed snippets differed: older search results still displayed “2025–26 closed April 10, 2026,” while the current page fetch displayed “2025–26 scheduled to open November 2, 2026.” **Use the current direct-page wording** as the evidence; an uncached ordinary browser has not been independently tested.
