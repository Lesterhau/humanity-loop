## Exact original sources and independently compared claims

1. **NJ DHS SNAP Navigator Program, official:** https://www.nj.gov/humanservices/njsnap/apply/help/ — the section “Get Help Applying” currently displays gross monthly income examples of **$2,413 for a one-person household** and **$4,109 for a three-person household**, explicitly identifying them as **October 2025–September 2026** figures. The public reader's fresh 2026-11-10 retrieval reproduced the wording; the earlier cached result was not the sole basis.
2. **NJ DHS Who Is Eligible for SNAP?, official:** https://nj.gov/humanservices/njsnap/apply/eligibility/ — the current 185%-of-FPL state eligibility table is explicitly labeled **October 2026–September 2027** and shows **$2,461 for one person** and **$4,212 for three people**. The same-day public reader reproduced both rows. It also states that households with a senior or disabled member may qualify above the displayed limits, and that meeting an income limit does not itself guarantee eligibility.
3. **NJ DHS student SNAP FAQ, official:** https://nj.gov/humanservices/njsnap/apply/eligibility/collegefaq.shtml — a separate secondary editorial inconsistency: its table displays the FY2027 values ($2,461 / $4,212) while its caption still says **October 2025–September 2026**. The FAQ shows “updated 10/6/26”; that label is not a verified last-content-change timestamp.
4. **USDA FY2027 SNAP COLA reference:** https://www.fna.usda.gov/snap/allotment/cola — confirms FY2027 SNAP standards became effective October 1, 2026, although NJ's 185%-of-FPL categorical-eligibility figures should be taken directly from the **state's current published table**, not confused with USDA's default 130% gross screen.
