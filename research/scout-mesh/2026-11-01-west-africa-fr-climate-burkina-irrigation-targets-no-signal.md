## Original evidence and chronology

1. **9 October 2026 — Burkina Faso government information service (SIG), dry-season 2026–27 plan.** The government described ten special operations, an estimated **254.79 billion CFA francs** in total, **62,500 hectares of earthworks** and **50,000 hectares to be equipped for irrigation**, with a stated goal of increasing irrigated area from under 30,000 to 80,000 hectares by **31 October 2027**. The same article listed 750 free boreholes and 1,000 lowland plots as planned, not delivered. [Primary source](https://gouvernement.gov.bf/actualites/campagne-seche-2026-2027-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-lirrigation-pour-accroitre-la-production-agricole/).
2. **21 October 2026 — government announcement of project launch on 20 October.** The later SIG report describes earthworks and leveling over **60,000 hectares** during November 2026–April 2027, while retaining **50,000 hectares of completed irrigation perimeters** by **31 October 2027** and the same 254.79 billion CFA franc budget. [Primary source](https://gouvernement.gov.bf/actualites/offensive-agropastorale-le-premier-ministre-lance-lamenagement-de-50-000-hectares-irrigues/).
3. **21 October 2026 — APAnews.** The independent news agency also repeats **60,000 hectares** of preparatory earthworks and 50,000 hectares of irrigation. This supports the later figure's public circulation but does **not** independently audit the government's plan or spending. [Contemporaneous secondary report](https://fr.apanews.net/news/burkina-plus-de-254-milliards-pour-irriguer-50-000-hectares/).
4. **October 2026 — FEWS NET special report.** Regional rainfall deficits and dry spells affected parts of the Sahel in 2026, with Burkina Faso described as experiencing moderate effects relative to Chad and other zones. This independently establishes a climate/food-security context, **not** the irrigation programme's effectiveness, environmental sustainability, or hectares delivered. [FEWS NET regional context](https://fews.net/global/special-report/october-2026).

## Evaluation

- **Exact apparent mismatch:** earlier preparatory earthworks target 62,500 ha versus later 60,000 ha, a difference of **2,500 ha (4% of the earlier earthworks figure)**. The final irrigated-area goal of 50,000 ha is **unchanged**. The articles are 12 days apart and could describe a genuine revision, different phases, or rounding. Neither establishes that the other was erroneous; the definitive project document and current contractor plan were not reviewed.
- **Existing solution / duplication:** A government-funded, publicly announced irrigation programme already exists; multiple channels cover its rollout. No request from affected producers, independent technical assessment, or consented implementation partner has been identified. Earlier Humanity Loop West Africa scans addressed Burkina WASH/VSLA and regional El Niño risk, not this precise programme-target version difference.
- **STEEP+ / cross-impact:** Environmental and social dimensions include water withdrawals, aquifer recharge, local land use and access, drought resilience, farm income and agricultural labor. Economic and governance dimensions include budget execution, water accounting and independent evaluation. **None of these effects were measured in this review.** Large-scale irrigation may improve dry-season production but can also shift water/land burdens; do not presume net benefit or harm.
- **Weak signal / TIPPO:** A rapid shift in preparatory-earthworks figures during a large, drought-responsive investment is a potential *monitoring question*, not evidence of a new intervention or systemic failure. **H1:** rain-dependent cultivation; **H2:** irrigation expansion and maintenance; **H3:** independently monitored, water-budgeted climate-resilient food systems. The transition is aspirational, not demonstrated.
- **Futures Triangle:** push = rainfall variability and food-security goals; pull = stated irrigation/production targets; weights = cost, maintenance, water availability, land rights and uncertain implementation. All weights are analytical questions, not verified programme failures.
- **Falsification / promotion test:** Obtain an authoritative dated implementation plan explicitly reconciling 60,000 versus 62,500 ha, or independent credible evidence that one active public figure is materially misleading for an ongoing application/procurement decision. Verify a specific unaddressed consequence or a consenting partner's concrete request before escalating. Recheck only on substantive new programme documents or at a proportionate later review; no hourly reread.
