## Essential alternative explanation / falsification

The city expressly requests a **2025 tax return**, and its 2026–27 form uses the **2025–26** CDE chart. This may be an intentional policy of matching prior tax-year income to the corresponding year's meal thresholds. Older El Cerrito forms also used prior-year tax returns and income figures. No governing city policy document establishing which school year's CDE scale must be used for 2026–27 childcare discounts has been independently confirmed. Thus the mismatch is real but the conclusion “city is applying the wrong threshold” is **NOT verified**.

Also, applicants with qualifying proof of Food Stamps, CalWORKS, TANF or WIC have an alternative documentation path under the city form. Program availability, income counting, discount review and appeal practices are unverified.

## Proposed narrow editorial clarification (UNSENT; owner approval required for any contact)

> The City's 2026–27 childcare discount application lists income figures matching CDE's 2025–26 free/reduced-price meal guidelines, while CDE has published higher 2026–27 scales. The form also requests a 2025 federal income tax return, so this may be intentional prior-year matching. Could the Recreation Department clarify whether the 2025–26 scales are deliberately used for 2026–27 discounts, and, if so, label the chart's effective year and policy basis? If current-year CDE scales are intended, the four-person annual free/reduced thresholds would be $42,900/$61,050 rather than $41,795/$59,478. We are requesting clarification, not asserting that any application was denied incorrectly.
