## Source-backed observations (checked 2026-10-23)
1. **Makassar City government, 2026-10-22:** The municipal digital services office (Diskominfo) publicly reported receiving a **draft** set of disability-access recommendations from **SAFEnet** concerning its **Lontara+** public-service app. The city quotes SAFEnet identifying screen-reader support, Deaf-accessible information, motor-accessible navigation, simpler menus/content, privacy/transparency, and meaningful disability participation as areas for improvement. City official Muhammad Roem **welcomed** the recommendations; this is **not** proof the recommendations were adopted or implemented. [Municipal announcement](https://makassarkota.go.id/berita/mkskota-38217/muhammad-roem-sambut-rekomendasi-safenet-untuk-penguatan-aksesibilitas-lontara).
2. **ANTARA regional news, 2026-10-23:** At a public press event, the disability civil-society coalition and SAFEnet advocated participation of disability organizations as partners, not just end users. The report attributes to SAFEnet researcher Ni Putu Chandra a finding that disability groups had not been involved in planning/development/evaluation. **That is the researchers' reported finding, not an independently replicated Humanity Loop audit.** [ANTARA report](https://makassar.antaranews.com/berita/641099/kmsp-disabilitas-rekomendasikan-aplikasi-lontara-akomodir-difabel).
3. **Makassar City, 2026-08-17:** Lontara+ added civil-registration, property-tax and local mobility functions. That increases the stakes of accessibility because public-service access is involved, but does not establish a particular person's exclusion. [City launch](https://makassarkota.go.id/berita/mkskota-37482/hut-ke-81-ri-makassar-launching-tiga-fitur-terbaru-app-lontara-komitmen-pelayana).
4. **Indonesia Ministry of Communication and Digital, 2026-07-16:** National officials publicly stated that government digital services must be accessible to citizens with disabilities and welcomed disability commission evaluations. This confirms the issue has institutional ownership and is not a policy vacuum. [Ministry statement](https://www.komdigi.go.id/berita/siaran-pers/detail/wamen-nezar-patria-layanan-digital-pemerintah-harus-dapat-diakses-semua-warga-negara).
5. **Existing solutions / duplication check:** SAFEnet and disability-led partners have already conducted consultations and presented recommendations; city government has received them. A fresh independent app, redundant audit or uninvited public complaint would duplicate current work. No public proof of an implementation timetable, accepted remediation checklist or post-fix user acceptance test was established by this bounded search; **absence from searched releases does not prove these do not exist elsewhere**.
