## What primary sources actually establish

1. **PAHO, October 21, 2026 (Spanish):** Childhood lead exposure remains an important preventable threat in Latin America and the Caribbean. PAHO reports that *among children evaluated in available studies*, about 30% had blood lead above 5 µg/dL and 6.78% above 10 µg/dL; PAHO explicitly warns that surveillance and screening data are incomplete. **These are figures for evaluated children, not population-representative prevalence estimates.** PAHO is already working with countries on toxicology centers, laboratory capacity, biomonitoring and exposure surveillance.  
   Source: https://www.paho.org/es/noticias/21-10-2026-exposicion-infantil-al-plomo-sigue-siendo-amenaza-prevenible-para-salud
2. **WHO, May 12 and October 15, 2026:** The PREVENT lead-poisoning technical package has a published draft overview and a full 2027 release plan. It explicitly calls for source prioritization, exposure measurement, responses, regulatory enforcement, and tracking. WHO says it is building blood-lead testing capacity in Brazil and other countries with partners. This is an **existing solution and consultation process**, not an unmet request for Humanity Loop to build a parallel app.  
   Sources: https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/technical-package-on-lead-poisoning-prevention--overview ; https://www.who.int/news/item/15-10-2026-countries-move-to-accelerate-action-against-lead-poisoning
3. **Pure Earth, September 29 / October 20, 2026 (Spanish):** A regional webinar with representatives from Colombia, Mexico, Peru and Brazil specifically shares prevention implementation and partnerships. Another existing actor already convenes relevant regional experts.  
   Source: https://www.pureearth.org/america-latina-unida-frente-al-plomo/

## Novelty / duplication / counterevidence
