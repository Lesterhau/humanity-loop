# Oregon WIC — 2026–27 three-person annual income table discrepancy

**Observation date:** 2026-11-12 UTC  
**Scout lane:** US-EN benefits/service-access, source-reviewed  
**Disposition:** verified conflict in publicly retrieved official text; **unsent** correction candidate, not verified applicant harm, incorrect case adjudication, or institutional correction.

## Exact official-source crosswalk

1. [Oregon Health Authority — WIC Eligibility guidelines](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/PH/HEALTHYPEOPLEFAMILIES/WIC/Pages/income.aspx), public state WIC application guidance. Under “Income Eligibility Criteria - Effective July 1, 2026,” the **three-person household** row lists **annual $50,452**, monthly $4,212, twice-monthly $2,106, biweekly $1,944, and weekly $972. The table also retains a conflicting second caption, “Income Eligibility Criteria - Effective June 1, 2024.”
2. [USDA Food and Nutrition Administration — WIC Income Eligibility Guidelines 2026–2027](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/wic/fr-042926), published April 29, effective July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027. The contiguous-state 185%-FPL **three-person household** row lists **annual $50,542**, monthly $4,212, twice-monthly $2,106, biweekly $1,944, and weekly $972.
3. [Oregon DHS/OHA Combined Standards PDF](https://sharedsystems.dhsoha.state.or.us/DHSForms/Served/de5530.pdf), 2026–27 Summer EBT 185%-FPL table, independently lists **$50,542 annual** and the same four shorter-period figures for a three-person household. This is a **different program**, used only to corroborate the 185%-FPL arithmetic, not as proof of Oregon WIC's binding operational standard.
4. [Massachusetts official WIC 2026–27 table](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/check-eligibility-for-wic) independently lists **$50,542** and the same shorter-period figures, but is not an Oregon eligibility authority.

The Oregon page says the income criterion is **185% of the federal poverty limit**, and all its other listed 2026–27 annual rows and the four other columns for a three-person household match USDA. The $50,452 figure appears to transpose the middle digits of $50,542. A family with **$50,500 gross annual income** illustrates why an annual-income reader might be deterred by the lower printed figure, but **no actual eligibility denial, applicant reliance, or income-period screening method has been verified**.

**Legal/program qualification:** USDA allows state agencies to choose WIC income guidelines within specified limits, and a WIC clinic evaluates other eligibility criteria. We have **not** audited Oregon's actual eligibility system or established that it uses a $50,542 annual threshold in all cases. The observed discrepancy is in the state's own public chart against its stated 185%-FPL criterion, not proof of unlawful administration.
