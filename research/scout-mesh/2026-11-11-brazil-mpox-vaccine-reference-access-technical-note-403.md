# Brazil mpox vaccine access: November 2026 official-source crosswalk (review-only)

**Review date:** 2026-11-11 UTC (November 10 America/Chicago). **Status:** primary-source checked, public-safe, **not externally distributed**. **Work type:** distinct external public-health service-access research; not an independent contributor submission, medical advice, or a verified correction. **Geography/language:** Brazil / Portuguese; cross-cutting public-health review, **not** a completed registered `latam-es-public-health` Spanish Scout cell.

## Why this is useful

The Ministry of Health's November 8–9 communications announce **25,000 newly secured mpox vaccine doses with delivery expected within about three weeks**. This is **not proof that those 25,000 doses are already delivered or locally bookable**. The Ministry also reports **3,650 doses already distributed to nine federative units**; no local clinic stock, appointment or individual eligibility was independently checked.

The **signed November 5 technical note (Nota Técnica Conjunta 403/2026-DPNI/SVSA/MS)** contains practical safety and access details not apparent from the short vaccine-supply headline: which adults can receive the dose-sparing intradermal route, the continued subcutaneous route for people with HIV/immunodeficiency, the phased use of thawed stock, and the narrower post-exposure eligibility/time window. These distinctions matter for accurate public-facing explanations.

## Primary-source evidence and precise findings

1. **Supply announcement (Nov 8):** [Ministério da Saúde, 25,000 doses](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/novembro/mpox-ministerio-da-saude-garante-25-mil-doses-de-vacina-apos-negociacao-internacional). It reports 25,000 acquired/secured via PAHO, expected delivery in about three weeks; 3,650 doses distributed to nine units; 832 confirmed cases and one death in Brazil through **Nov 6**. The count is a dated national snapshot, not today's tally.
2. **Second official announcement (Nov 9):** [Secretaria de Comunicação Social](https://www.gov.br/secom/pt-br/acompanhe-a-secom/noticias/2026/11/ministerio-da-saude-compra-lote-com-25-mil-doses-da-vacina-contra-mpox). Independently corroborates 25,000 pending delivery and existing distribution; neither establishes availability at a named reference centre.
3. **Controlling detailed vaccination guidance (signed Nov 5):** [Nota Técnica Conjunta 403/2026, original 4-page PDF](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/publicacoes/notas-tecnicas/2026/nota-tecnica-conjunta-no-4032026-dpni-svsa-ms.pdf). Page 1 §§2.1–2.4: intradermal 0.1 mL is a dose-sparing option, **but explicitly not indicated for people living with HIV, regardless of CD4 count**. Page 2 §§2.5–2.8: the 0.1 mL intradermal route is recommended for **adults 18+ using HIV PrEP and laboratory personnel handling mpox samples** only when they do **not** have HIV or the specified immunodeficiencies; **0.5 mL subcutaneous** remains for people living with HIV or other listed immunodeficiencies. Two doses, 28 days apart.
4. **Phased eligibility (same note, p.2 §2.9):** priority remains people living with HIV with CD4 below 100, through prior registration/scheduling at their reference service; after **10 days following thawing** remaining stock may expand to CD4 below 200, PrEP users and laboratory personnel; after **20 days** it may expand to people living with HIV irrespective of CD4. This is a **conditional dose-management sequence**, not a nationwide promise that everyone in those groups can book immediately. Timing depends on each vial/batch thaw and actual stock.
5. **Post-exposure vaccination (same note, p.2 §2.10):** specifies **ages 18–49**, medium/high-risk exposure to suspected/probable/confirmed cases, **preferably within 4 days**, possibly **through 14 days if no signs/symptoms**, and subject to the operational protocol and health surveillance assessment. Anyone with suspected symptoms or exposure should seek a health service promptly; this research is not an eligibility determination.
6. **Service route:** [Ministry Nov 5 access announcement](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/novembro/ministerio-da-saude-autoriza-aquisicao-de-20-mil-doses-da-vacina-contra-mpox-por-meio-da-opas/) directs the highest-priority group to the **reference centre where they already receive care** for registration and subsequent scheduling; [Nov 8 announcement](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/novembro/mpox-ministerio-da-saude-garante-25-mil-doses-de-vacina-apos-negociacao-internacional) says mpox vaccination is **not routine UBS walk-in vaccination** but via designated reference immunization services. Exact local sites, stock, and booking routes were not verified.
7. **General disease information:** [Ministry mpox topic page](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/saude-de-a-a-z/m/mpox) describes prevention and generic pre/post-exposure categories but does not itself reproduce all November 5 dose/age/stock sequencing. This is a level-of-detail difference, **not established institutional misinformation**.

## Public-facing Portuguese draft — NOT SENT

> **Vacina contra mpox no Brasil (informações oficiais de 5–9/11/2026):** o Ministério da Saúde anunciou a compra de 25 mil doses, com entrega prevista para as próximas semanas. Isso **não significa** que essas doses já chegaram aos serviços de vacinação. A oferta é direcionada a grupos definidos e depende da disponibilidade local. Pessoas vivendo com HIV, especialmente com CD4 abaixo de 100, devem consultar o serviço de referência onde já são acompanhadas para avaliação e agendamento. A nota técnica de 5/11 prevê a via intradérmica de menor volume para determinados adultos que usam PrEP e profissionais de laboratório **sem HIV/imunodeficiência**; para pessoas com HIV ou imunodeficiência, mantém a via subcutânea. Após contato de risco com caso suspeito ou confirmado, procure rapidamente um serviço de saúde para avaliação; a nota define condições e prazos específicos para vacinação pós-exposição. **Não se automedique nem presuma elegibilidade ou estoque local com base em manchetes.** Consulte os links oficiais acima e a secretaria local de saúde.
