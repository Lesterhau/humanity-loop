## EPA's stated reasoning and limitations
Decision document section 2.D.iii: two ACME sectors have chlorophyll-a exceedances; four dissolved-oxygen segments retained after EPA rejected automatic exclusion of older data without sufficient justification; eight lead listings concern excluded hardness/lead results under laboratory practical-quantification-limit treatment. The eight lead cases are not proof of drinking-water exposure or an actual human-health event. The document identifies assessment units, not verified current sampling outcomes. EPA's notice invites comments by November 26; the agency says it will consider comments and any revisions before final transmission.
