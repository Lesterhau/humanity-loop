## Verified observations
- EPA published a drinking-water warning on April 23, 2026, after at least 10% of tap samples in the relevant monitoring set exceeded the 0.015 mg/L lead action level. This does **not** mean 10% of all customers or all taps exceeded the level.
- EPA describes particular risk to infants, children, and pregnant people; its notice recommends certified lead-removal filters, cold water for consumption, and testing individual taps. **Boiling does not remove lead.**
- EPA's public notification index still lists the April 23 notice. This is evidence of a historical notice, **not** independent proof that the exceedance continues on November 17.
- The source checked does not establish a new sampling result, completed corrosion-control action, service-line replacement, notification receipt by every customer, or a return to compliant monitoring.
