# QIOMALA electric pressure washer HWT12P — CPSC recall 27-084
Observed: 2026-11-15 UTC. Status: source-reviewed Tier-0 public safety finding; UNSENT; human editorial review required.

## Official primary evidence
- CPSC recall issued 2026-11-12: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/QIOMALA-Pressure-Washers-Recalled-Due-to-Serious-Risk-of-Injury-or-Death-from-Shock-and-Electrocution-Hazards
- CPSC electrocution recall index (independent CPSC category cross-reference): https://www.cpsc.gov/recall-hazards/electrocution

## Verified scope
- Recall 27-084 covers **80 QIOMALA model HWT12P electric pressure washers**, green/black or blue/black, 1.2 GPM, model label on back near power-cord entry. Sold on Amazon April–June 2026, about $130.
- CPSC describes an **inadequate-length power cord**, which may encourage extension-cord use and creates shock/electrocution hazard. Do **not** substitute a claim that CPSC found an absent integral GFCI: that is a different defect identified in some OTHER pressure-washer recalls.
- **No incidents or injuries reported** for this recall as of the retrieved CPSC notice.
- CPSC instructs immediate stop-use and contact QIOMALA at QIOMALA@163.com for a **full refund**. Its refund evidence process asks consumers to **unplug first**, then cut the cord in half and photograph the destroyed washer with model number visible. This is the CPSC-published procedure, not an instruction to cut any energized cord.
- The actual retailer refund handling, seller response, and consumer outcomes were NOT independently tested.

## Human-review-only consumer note (not distributed)
Owners of QIOMALA HWT12P pressure washers should stop using them immediately and consult CPSC recall 27-084 for model identification and refund steps. Keep the unit unplugged. Do not assume that adding an extension cord resolves the hazard. CPSC's published remedy is a full refund after specified proof of destruction; follow the official instructions and confirm current process before acting.
