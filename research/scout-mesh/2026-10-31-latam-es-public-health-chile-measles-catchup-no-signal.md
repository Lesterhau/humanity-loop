## Finding / duplication check

A serious **remaining immunization gap** is documented, but the Chilean government and PAHO are already publicly coordinating active catch-up, outreach, free vaccination and surveillance. The existence of 44,386 newly vaccinated children and more than 140,000 still behind is **not itself a mathematical contradiction**: the July and October cohorts, eligibility and updated denominators are not established as identical. No reliable evidence here proves a specific incorrect official instruction, inaccessible vaccination service, unserved group that current programs overlook, or a request for Humanity Loop to intervene.

**Decision:** no new external report, outreach, duplicate campaign, AI-run medical advice, or app build. Preserve this as a source-reviewed `no_signal` result. A future opportunity would require a reproducible, current, specific access obstacle or explicit institution/partner request, then independent validation before any intervention.
