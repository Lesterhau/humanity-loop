## Verified distinctions
EPA alleges the City of Worley wastewater plant, NPDES ID0022713, discharging to North Fork Rock Creek, failed to use approved methods, report certain noncompliance promptly, and maintain records. The worksheet (p.4) specifically lists April 2025 total ammonia nitrogen exceedances: daily maxima 56% and 199% above limit and monthly averages 302% and 704% above limit; the worksheet describes two missed 24-hour notices for April 2025 ammonia violations. It also says the facility used non-EPA-approved Hach meter analytical methods for pH and dissolved oxygen, lacked receiving-water visual observation logs for July 2024 and March 2026, and had a Quality Assurance Plan missing required elements (pp.4-5). The draft offers an $800 civil penalty, not an independent assessment of environmental damage.

The agreement (p.1) states respondent neither admits nor denies the specific allegations and certifies corrections, with a written corrective-action report required. The actual corrective-action report, current water quality, harm/exposure, final order, and payment are not independently verified. No assertion of present unsafe drinking water or ongoing violations.

EPA's notice gives **December 7, 2026** as public-comment deadline. Email comments: R10_RHC@epa.gov, reference docket CWA-10-2026-0267. If email inaccessible, EPA lists Regional Hearing Clerk Salee Porter at 206-553-0531. Public comments are not submitted by this review.
