Official Government Publishing Office Federal Register text, **91 FR 66698–66699**, published **October 22, 2026**, document **2026-21609**: <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-22/html/2026-21609.htm>. ## Verified public-interest result

The EPA summary and GPO notice agree on the case, docket and **Nov 23** deadline. The GPO notice adds material participation cautions absent from the short EPA summary: comments may be public and non-removable, and late comments need not be considered. This is a **neutral access clarification**, not a demonstrated agency error, evidence of a misfiled comment, or a recommendation for or against the consent decree. The complaint's allegations are not adjudicated findings. The proposed decree is not established as court-approved or in force.

## Unsent, human-review-only public-access card

**A proposed Clean Air Act settlement affecting California air-plan decisions is open for comment.** EPA says the proposal concerns its timetable for acting on outstanding California SIP submissions, in *Our Children's Earth Foundation v. Zeldin*. The Federal Register identifies **docket EPA-HQ-OGC-2026-9077** and says written comments must be **received by November 23, 2026**. Review the actual proposal and current docket status before deciding whether to comment. The comment is about the proposed settlement's terms; it is not a vote on individual air permits or automatic approval of California plans. The official notice cautions that submissions may become public, including contact details, and cannot be edited or removed after submission. Avoid confidential personal or business information. This is neutral process information, not legal advice.
