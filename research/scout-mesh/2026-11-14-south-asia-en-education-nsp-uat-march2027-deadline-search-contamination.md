# NSP CSSS 2026–27: UAT-host search result is not a production deadline notice

**Observed:** 2026-11-14 UTC (automated public web-reader; source pages crawled today). **Cell:** south-asia-en-education. **Status:** verified source-provenance hazard / human-review-only informational note. **Related prior case:** PM-USP CSSS conflicting 2026–27 renewal dates, tracked in HOURLY-CONTINUITY-QUEUE.md. **No student outcome or agency defect attributed.**

## New, distinct primary-source finding

An indexed, publicly retrievable NSP-branded page on **https://uat.nsp.gov.in/All-Scholarships** lists PM-USP CSSS renewal student, defective-application, institute and officer deadlines as **31-03-2027**, and marks the scheme **NOT YET OPENED**. The same March 2027 pattern appears across numerous unrelated scholarship schemes on that UAT-labeled host. The host name contains **uat** (commonly user-acceptance testing), so it is *not established as the controlling live public scholarship portal*. A search engine can surface it beside genuine production scholarship pages; copying its March dates into applicant guidance would be unsupported. This is a new provenance distinction, not a new extension order.

The **production** NSP [Students announcements](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students) and [full announcements](https://scholarships.gov.in/ViewMoreAnnouncement) still state for **CSSS renewals**: student **31-10-2026**, institute **15-11-2026**, L2 **30-11-2026**. The **production** [All Scholarships](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) currently lists CSSS **NOT YET OPENED** alongside **30-11-2026** student and **15-12-2026** institute deadlines. The separately observed [production /iver/All-Scholarships](https://scholarships.gov.in/iver/All-Scholarships) identifies renewal opening **01-06-2026** and the later Nov/Dec dates. These production inconsistencies remain unresolved; the UAT page does not resolve them.

## Evidence and falsifiability

- [NSP-branded UAT-host scheme listing](https://uat.nsp.gov.in/All-Scholarships): CSSS March 31, 2027 dates; the same future dates across many unrelated schemes. Direct public web-reader open on Nov 14 UTC.
- [NSP production Students announcement](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students): October 31 / November 15 / November 30 CSSS renewal dates. Direct open on Nov 14 UTC.
- [NSP production All Scholarships](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships): CSSS status “NOT YET OPENED” with November 30 / December 15 dates. Direct open on Nov 14 UTC.
- [NSP production renewal-specific alternate listing](https://scholarships.gov.in/iver/All-Scholarships): June 1 renewal opening with later dates. Direct open on Nov 14 UTC.

**Unknown:** whether the UAT host is intentionally public, who maintains it, whether any applicant saw or relied on it, which dated production order legally controls, and whether the actual authenticated applicant/institute screens reflect either production listing. A web-reader observation does not prove uncached human-browser state or the validity of a March 2027 extension. No test login or form action attempted.

## Bounded, unpublished clarification for qualified review

“Do not rely on scholarship deadline dates from a search result whose address begins `uat.nsp.gov.in`. That page currently displays March 31, 2027 dates, but it has not been established as the live application system. Official production NSP pages also show conflicting CSSS renewal dates. Until the scheme owner confirms the controlling notice, an applicant with an existing renewal should urgently check the status in their own authorized NSP account and contact their institute nodal officer before the earlier November 15 verification date, preserving a dated screenshot of any discrepancy. Do not assume a deadline was extended.”
