# Texas 2026 data-center water reporting: two distinct disclosure populations and public verification gates

**Humanity Loop Tier-0 Scout | global-en-ai-infra | 2026-11-09 UTC | read-only official-source review**

## Verdict
**Verified procedural clarification / candidate public-accountability follow-up, NOT a verified violation, data-center water-use estimate, enforcement result, or institutional correction.**

Texas has two related but **non-interchangeable** data-collection pathways. The Texas Water Development Board (TWDB) annual **2026 Water Use Survey** asks covered **operational facilities that used water during calendar 2025** to report 2025 water use. The ERCOT **State and Community Impact RFI** targets certain **proposed, not-yet-energized data centers seeking ERCOT interconnection, at or above 25 MW**; its questions include projected water sources and use, cooling and community effects. A missing annual survey cannot automatically be interpreted as noncompliance by a project that did not operate in 2025. Likewise, an ERCOT RFI response does not by itself prove completion of the separate annual survey.

## Reusable citizen / reporter verification gate

Before describing a data center as a **noncompliant water user**, establish in order:
1. **Identity and deduplication:** one facility versus aliases or duplicate survey records; distinguish company, campus and project.
2. **Operational year:** evidence that it operated during 2025, rather than construction-only or future load.
3. **Actual notice and obligation:** evidence it was a recipient of the relevant TWDB annual survey; distinguish an ERCOT RFI recipient.
4. **Submission and cure status:** verify the relevant survey year, late PDF submission, administrative completeness, correction and date using TWDB's Report ID 19 or a dated agency record.
5. **Official enforcement outcome:** require a named agency action or docket before claiming a referral, permit disqualification or offense.
6. **Water quantities:** distinguish **measured withdrawal/consumption**, **projected use**, **municipal purchased water**, **self-supplied water** and **reuse**; do not add or substitute these without common units, dates and definitions.
