# California San Joaquin Valley PM2.5 plan — proposed EPA consent decree and public participation

**Review date:** 2026-11-16 UTC. **Cell:** US environmental health / civic access. **Status:** primary-source public-information research; UNSENT, not an external comment or agency correction.

## Source and observed facts

EPA Office of General Counsel, *Proposed Consent Decrees and Draft Settlement Agreements*, section “Little Manila Rising v. EPA, No. 3:26-cv-04106-MMC (N.D. Cal.)”: https://www.epa.gov/ogc/proposed-consent-decrees-and-draft-settlement-agreements (accessed 2026-11-16).

EPA reports that Little Manila Rising, Medical Advocates for Healthy Air, and Sierra Club allege EPA did not take final action on California's submission of the San Joaquin Valley Unified Air Pollution Control District **2024 Plan for the 2012 annual fine particulate matter (PM2.5) NAAQS**. EPA describes a **proposed** consent decree requiring EPA to **sign a notice of final rulemaking taking action on that submission by May 28, 2027**. This is an action deadline, **not a promise to approve the plan**, nor a final court order or a verified air-quality improvement.

EPA gives public docket **EPA-HQ-OGC-2026-9242**, https://www.regulations.gov/docket/EPA-HQ-OGC-2026-9242, and says comments may be submitted through **December 7, 2026**. The agency warns submissions are public. The live interactive docket, precise cutoff hour, full decree text and any subsequent amendment **were not independently inspected**. EPA's landing page is the verified source of the date; no independent Federal Register deadline corroboration is claimed.

## Human-review-only public information card

EPA is taking comments through December 7, 2026 on a **proposed legal timetable** for responding to the San Joaquin Valley 2024 PM2.5 implementation plan. Docket: EPA-HQ-OGC-2026-9242. The proposed timetable calls for an EPA final-rulemaking action by May 28, 2027, but does not predetermine approval or establish that pollution levels have changed. Check the live docket and its privacy instructions before submitting any personal information.
