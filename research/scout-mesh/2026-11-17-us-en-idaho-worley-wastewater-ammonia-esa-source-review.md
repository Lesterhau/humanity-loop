# City of Worley wastewater settlement — primary-source review (2026-11-17 UTC)

**Status:** Tier-0 public-source research only; UNSENT; no finding of current unsafe water, proven environmental injury, or finalized penalty.

## Primary sources
- EPA Region 10 public notice (published Nov 6, 2026): https://www.epa.gov/publicnotices/proposed-settlement-city-worley-clean-water-act-violations-idaho
- EPA five-page draft expedited settlement and inspection worksheet: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-11/cwa-10-2026-0267-worley.pdf

## Findings
1. EPA lists public comments due **December 7, 2026**, docket **CWA-10-2026-0267**, by email to the EPA Region 10 Regional Hearing Clerk; the notice supplies a telephone alternative for people unable to comment by email. Do not infer an exact cutoff hour.
2. The proposed settlement concerns City of Worley's wastewater plant, permit **ID0022713**, discharging to **North Fork Rock Creek**. EPA proposes an **$800** civil penalty following a **March 9, 2026** inspection.
3. The worksheet (PDF p. 4) alleges **two April 2025 daily maximum total-ammonia exceedances of 56% and 199%**, and **two April 2025 monthly-average total-ammonia exceedances of 302% and 704%**. These are percentages *above permit limits*, not measured ammonia concentrations or a quantified health risk. The worksheet also alleges no timely 24-hour notification for two daily-limit instances.
4. Other alleged problems include use of non-EPA-approved analytical methods for pH/dissolved oxygen-related measurements, missing receiving-water observation logs and deficiencies in the quality assurance plan (PDF pp. 4–5).
5. The draft agreement says the respondent **neither admits nor denies** alleged violations, and certifies corrective actions, with documentation to accompany the agreement (PDF p. 1). That certification is not independently verified by this review. The draft expressly says it becomes effective **when a final order is executed and filed** (PDF pp. 1–2); the accessible draft's final-order signature is blank. Do not call this a final, effective order or verified completed remediation.

## Public-interest value and limits

A proposed settlement and an unverified corrective-action certification alone cannot establish current receiving-water quality, ecological injury, or continuing violations.
