# Hawaii County: overlapping 2026 disasters and the February 1, 2027 IRS deadline

**Observed:** 2026-11-12 UTC. **Scope:** English-language, public-source, human-review-only service-access card. **Status:** independently source-checked draft; not distributed, not tax advice, not evidence of a taxpayer outcome or IRS correction attributable to Humanity Loop.

## Why this needs a separate card

Two official IRS releases describe **different Hawaii disaster periods**:

1. **HI-2026-01**, severe storms/flooding/mudslides beginning **March 10, 2026**, covered **Hawaii, Honolulu, Kauai, and Maui counties**. The [April 10 release](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-announces-tax-relief-for-taxpayers-impacted-by-severe-storms-in-the-state-of-hawaii-various-deadlines-postponed-to-july-8-2026) has a **May 12 update banner** changing its originally announced July 8 deadline to **August 20, 2026**, but the body still repeats **July 8**. Treat the earlier release's body as superseded/internally inconsistent for current deadline advice.
2. **HI-2026-03**, earthquake beginning **May 22, 2026**, covers **Hawaii County**. The [September 10 IRS release, subsequently corrected](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-announces-tax-relief-for-taxpayers-impacted-by-the-earthquake-in-hawaii-county-various-deadlines-postponed-to-feb-1-2027) specifies **February 1, 2027** for covered filing/payment obligations. Its prominent **Correction Notice** explicitly says that taxpayers affected by **both HI-2026-01 and HI-2026-03** have until **February 1, 2027** for **April 15, 2026 tax payments and returns**. The IRS deleted a previous assertion excluding those April 15 payments.

**Critical distinction:** The February 1 extension of April 15 payments is explicitly stated for people affected by **both** disasters. Do **not** generalize it to everyone affected only by the May 22 earthquake or to Honolulu, Kauai or Maui counties solely because they appeared in the March storm declaration. Verify each person's eligibility and specific obligation against IRS guidance.

The IRS's current [Hawaii state index](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/around-the-nation-hawaii) and [disaster relief index](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/tax-relief-in-disaster-situations) list both notices. The correction notice is part of the IRS's own publication; Humanity Loop did **not** discover or cause the IRS correction.

## Public-facing, human-review-only action card (UNSENT)

**Hawaii County residents and businesses: two 2026 disasters may change your federal tax deadlines.** If you qualify for IRS disaster relief from the May 22 earthquake, certain federal tax filings and payments otherwise due in the relief period may be postponed to **February 1, 2027**. If you were affected by **both** the March storms (HI-2026-01) and the May earthquake (HI-2026-03), the IRS specifically says **April 15, 2026 federal tax payments and returns** are included in the February 1, 2027 extension.

**What to check:**
- Read the [corrected IRS Hawaii County notice](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-announces-tax-relief-for-taxpayers-impacted-by-the-earthquake-in-hawaii-county-various-deadlines-postponed-to-feb-1-2027) before relying on the older March-storm release. Confirm your covered location, disaster, tax type and original due date.
- If you receive an IRS late-filing or late-payment penalty notice for a covered deadline, the IRS instructs you to **call the number on that notice** to request abatement.
- Eligible taxpayers **outside** the covered area whose necessary records are in it may need to request relief through IRS Special Services at **866-562-5227**; the IRS generally identifies taxpayers with covered addresses automatically.
- An installment agreement's **direct debits may continue**, and interest/late-payment charges can continue to accrue even where missed installments do not default the agreement during the postponement period. Contact the IRS or your bank if you need to suspend direct debits.
- Do not assume every tax information return or payroll deposit is postponed. The IRS notice lists exclusions and narrower deposit-penalty windows. If unsure, consult the IRS or a qualified tax professional.

**This is a guide to published IRS relief, not a determination that you personally qualify or that taxes are forgiven.**
