# South Sudan cholera surveillance: national and WHO regional totals are not interchangeable

**Review date:** 2026-11-15 UTC. **Lane:** global-en public-health surveillance (cross-cutting Scout). **Status:** source-reviewed interpretation safeguard; UNSENT, no institutional error claim.

## Primary-source observations

1. [WHO South Sudan weekly IDSR bulletin, posted in November 2026, reporting cholera data as of 2 October 2026](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-11/South%20Sudan%20IDSR%20Weekly%20Bulletin%20Week%2038%2C%2014-20%20September%202026%20.pdf), p. 13: national bulletin states **112,186 cumulative cases and 1,724 deaths**, and **15 new cases and one death** during September 23–30; transmission identified in Mayom, Fangak and Renk. The bulletin itself distinguishes its weekly bulletin title/week from its more recent cholera situation-update cutoff.
2. [WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record 101-44, global cholera update](https://www.who.int/publications/journals/weekly-epidemiological-record/wer101-44/), **data cutoff 27 September 2026**, reports **14,909 South Sudan cases** within the **January 1–September 27, 2026** WHO African Region year-to-date table. Its Africa regional total is 169,880 cases. WHO expressly cautions about underreporting, reporting delay, heterogeneous case definitions, differing surveillance systems and retrospective adjustments.

## Interpretation / public-interest value

The two South Sudan cumulative numbers are **not automatically contradictory**: the national bulletin appears to report an outbreak-to-date cumulative series, while WHO WER44 explicitly reports a calendar-year surveillance series through September 27. Different start dates, inclusion criteria and cutoff dates may explain the difference, but **the national series start date and case-definition reconciliation have NOT been independently established**. It would be misleading to subtract 14,909 from 112,186 as 'missing cases' or to treat their ratio as a reporting completeness estimate. The national 1,724 deaths likewise must not be divided by the WHO 14,909 denominator.

**Uncertainty:** Full national case-definition footnote, outbreak start date and WHO source aggregation are not verified. This review establishes a denominator/cutoff caution, not a government mistake, hidden burden, present-day risk estimate or successful intervention.
