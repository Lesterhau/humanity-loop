## Falsifiable public-interest question

For someone reviewing the six pending FERC large-load interconnection proceedings, can they use the no-account `eComment` route to comment, and is November 16/20 the public comment deadline? An incorrect assumption about either could prevent informed participation. This crosswalk distinguishes FERC's own instructions without suggesting a position on the proposed rules.

## Directly checked official sources (November 10, 2026)

1. **FERC Office of Public Participation (OPP), updated Oct 19, 2026:** https://www.ferc.gov/cost-transparency-customer-protection — six regional `EL` dockets; five RTO/ISO *responses* due **November 16, 2026**, SPP response due **November 20, 2026**. OPP says the public may comment now on the show-cause orders **or within 30 days after each relevant filing is submitted**. These dates are **operator filing deadlines**, not a single universal public comment deadline. OPP explicitly states its explainer is educational and not a binding Commission ruling.
2. **FERC Participating Online guide:** https://ferc.gov/participating-online-ferc — eLibrary is public and does not require registration; eComment is restricted to P, PF, CP, PT, ET, AD, RM and PL docket prefixes; most `EL` electric proceedings require eRegistration and **eFiling**.
3. **FERC eComment instructions, updated March 5, 2026:** https://www.ferc.gov/how-file-ecomment — individual, text-only, up to 10,000 characters, no attachment, eligible docket prefixes only. **EL is not on the eligible list.**
4. **FERC eFiling instructions:** https://www.ferc.gov/how-to-e-file-comments — registration required; eFiling supports `EL` electric dockets, documents and attachments; a comment is not the same as a motion to intervene.
5. **FERC June 18 announcement:** https://ferc.gov/news-events/news/ferc-launches-aggressive-targeted-action-speed-large-load-integration — six show-cause proceedings are preliminary inquiries about large-load interconnection tariff rules and possible customer protections, **not final rates or proof of measured cost shifting**.

**Date handling:** The November 16/20 deadlines are **not** inferred public closing dates. OPP's `within 30 days after each filing` is relative to the *actual filing date*; future calendar end dates cannot be confirmed until a filing appears and the docket's orders/notices are checked. The related RM docket's current comment status likewise must be rechecked against eLibrary before any filing.

## Useful neutral public-facing summary (draft, not sent)

> FERC has six regional proceedings on how data centers and other large electric users connect to the grid. Anyone can read their public records without an account in FERC eLibrary by searching the docket number. To submit comments in the six EL dockets, FERC says you need an eRegistration account and its eFiling system; its no-account eComment tool is not available for EL dockets. The related RM26-4-000 rulemaking does allow eligible individuals to use eComment. The November 16 and November 20 dates concern grid operators' responses, not a blanket deadline for the public. Check the latest docket notice for your actual comment deadline.
