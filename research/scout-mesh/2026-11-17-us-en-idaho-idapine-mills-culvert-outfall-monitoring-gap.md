## Verified observation
EPA's inspection worksheet alleges missing quarterly visual assessments during **11 significant rain events in 2021–2024**, and late discharge monitoring reports / omitted sampling during a period in which discharges were considered likely over **11 months**. The worksheet gives a specific physical mechanism: a site culvert receives stormwater before the outfall, and stormwater discharges were not observed from the actual discharge point. The proposed agreement also lists unsigned/uncertified site inspections and missing stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP) site-map details including drainage arrows, drains, vegetative swale, and fuel storage. This is an actionable *monitoring-location/documentation* question; neither the existence of actual pollutant exceedances nor present-day pollution is established by the worksheet.

EPA proposes a **$5,845** penalty. Respondent neither admits nor denies the specific alleged violations. Respondent's certification that violations were corrected is not independent confirmation of remediation. The PDF's final-order signature/date line is blank; final-order execution/filing remains unverified.
