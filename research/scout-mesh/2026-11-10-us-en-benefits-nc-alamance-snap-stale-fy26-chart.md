## Interpretation and safeguards

- The county's figures correctly describe the **prior** fiscal year; the issue is that an expired chart remains embedded in an application-facing public page after the new period began.
- 130% versus 200% are **different eligibility categories**; the 200% column is not a universal guarantee of eligibility. The state policy also specifies different treatment for households with specified members and categorical eligibility (§360.04(B)). Actual allotments are frequently lower than maxima and depend on net income and other factors.
- No evidence of a county processing-system defect, denied applicant, underpayment, live-browser screenshot, or institutional awareness. A web reader retrieved the public text; a normal interactive browser capture was not performed.
- This is a distinct **Alamance County** page, not the previously logged Montana, New Jersey, U.S. Virgin Islands or California county observations.

## Proposed editorial correction — NOT SENT

Subject: Alamance County Food and Nutrition Services webpage — expired FY2026 income/allotment chart

The county's public Food and Nutrition Services page currently displays an income and maximum-benefit table labeled October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026. North Carolina DHHS FNS 360, Change #04-2026, §360.02, has published the replacement thresholds effective October 1, 2026. For example, for a four-person household, the current state table lists a 130% gross monthly threshold of $3,575, a 200% gross threshold of $5,500 and a maximum allotment of $1,023, while the county page displays $3,483 / $5,360 / $994. Please consider replacing the expired chart and its effective-date heading with the current official NCDHHS figures, or linking directly to the maintained state table. This is a request to review website guidance, not an allegation of incorrect case processing.
