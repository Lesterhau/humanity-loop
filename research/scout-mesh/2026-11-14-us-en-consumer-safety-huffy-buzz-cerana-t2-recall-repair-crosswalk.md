## Verified, bounded finding

- The recalled product is the **gray Buzz Bicycles Cerana T2 pedal-assist e-trike, model E4004**, with model label near the pedals on the lower frame downtube. CPSC lists approximately **286 units** sold through independent bicycle shops and Buzz online, November 2024–July 2026.
- The **rear wheels can loosen or separate**, causing a fall hazard. CPSC reports **12 incidents** of wheel looseness/separation and **three injuries** (sprains, bruising and abrasions). These are reported counts, not an estimate of overall risk.
- CPSC and the manufacturer both instruct affected owners to **stop using the e-trike immediately** and arrange a **free replacement rear axle assembly**. The manufacturer can send the parts to the owner or a local bike shop. CPSC says Huffy reimburses up to **$100** for a third-party repair.
- The manufacturer adds a practically important instruction not explicit in CPSC's abbreviated remedy: **contact Buzz if the local repair will cost more than $100**. This is **not** a promise to reimburse amounts above $100; the manufacturer must clarify any additional arrangement. The manufacturer's recall contact is **855-430-6240** (8 a.m.–5 p.m. ET weekdays) or **service@huffy.com**, with a service form linked from the dedicated recall page.
- This is an **active repair recall**, not a stop-use-and-dispose warning, a blanket refund, or an ordinary 90-day return. The general store warranty/return restrictions should not be substituted for the specific recall instructions. There is no evidence here that any particular owner has received parts, obtained reimbursement, or completed a safe repair.

## Candidate plain-language consumer clarification — NOT SENT OR DISTRIBUTED

> If you own a gray Buzz Cerana T2 electric tricycle, check the frame label near the pedals for **model E4004**. CPSC recall **27-086** (November 12, 2026) warns that its rear wheels can loosen or detach. **Stop riding it immediately.** Contact Buzz/Huffy at **855-430-6240** or the manufacturer's [dedicated recall notice](https://www.buzzbicycles.com/buzz-cerana-t-recall) to arrange replacement rear axles. Parts are free; third-party installation is reimbursable up to $100 under the announced recall. **If your bike shop quotes more than $100, contact Buzz before proceeding**; the manufacturer's notice asks customers to do so but does not promise unlimited reimbursement. Do not assume ordinary warranty or return deadlines replace the recall remedy.
