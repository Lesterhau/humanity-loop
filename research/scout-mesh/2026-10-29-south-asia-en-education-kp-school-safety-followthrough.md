## What changed since the October 24 Scout

The [October 24 Humanity Loop investigation](2026-10-24-south-asia-en-education-peshawar-school-safety-audit.md) documented distinct post-collapse inspection directives and their **October 21** education-directorate, **October 26** C&W technical, and **approximately October 31** private-school legislative-report milestones. It did **not** locate an official completed province-wide results register or independently verify student relocation, closures and fitness certificates.

**October 27 — newly published follow-through:** Local journalist Mohammad Ashfaq in [Dawn, 27 October](https://www.dawn.com/news/2040048/over-4000-govt-school-buildings-declared-dilapidated) reports that the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa elementary/secondary education department's district and tehsil survey identified **4,049 government schools** needing reconstruction or rehabilitation, covering **8,088 classrooms, 2,949 group latrines and 48 examination halls**, with an **initial estimated PKR 16.2 billion** cost. This is **department-attributed reporting in an independent newspaper**, not an independently retrieved official engineering dataset or an approved appropriation. The same report says an education-department official described **more than 6,000 inspections of 12,493 registered private schools** (over ~48% of that reported denominator), with approximately another week anticipated for the private-school exercise. These are reported inspection counts, **not verified safety clearances, repairs or safe relocations**.

**October 18 baseline:** [Dawn, 18 October](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037891/education-dept-orders-safety-audit-of-school-buildings) described a departmental order to suspend classes in unsafe buildings, relocate pupils and resume only after competent technical fitness certification, with an October 21 district-report deadline. A distinct [C&W inspection directive reported October 16](https://www.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/10/16/kp-orders-province-wide-safety-inspection-of-government-schools) sought district technical reports by October 26 and safety clearance certificates. The [Governor House October 16 statement](https://www.governor.kp.gov.pk/node/776) requested a private-school safety legislative/report proposal within 15 days. These are **different scopes and deadlines**. The new Dawn survey figures do not independently establish delivery of the C&W certificates or the governor's later legislative report.

**Data caution:** A separate [October 17 report](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/17/nearly-37000-kp-govt-school-classrooms-in-poor-condition/) cited **36,967 dilapidated classrooms** across the province, while the October 27 survey describes **4,049 schools** and **8,088 classrooms** requiring work. Do not subtract, equate or call these contradictory without obtaining the two survey definitions, dates, coverage and counting rules. Similarly, reported private-school registry denominators vary by source/date. No names or personal details of children are recorded here.

## Verification status and decision

**Verified:** A dated, externally accessible October 27 local report contains concrete department-attributed district-level counts and a preliminary repair-cost estimate. This is meaningful *partial reporting progress* relative to the earlier no-results-found review. Credit the KP education authorities for undertaking and reporting the survey, and Dawn for reporting it. Humanity Loop did not initiate these inspections or cause the reported progress.

**Not verified:** An original government-issued full dataset; completeness of C&W technical assessments; risk classifications and safety certificates for every inspected school; number of dangerous buildings closed; actual student relocations and safe temporary instruction; repairs completed; money appropriated or spent; private-school inspection completion; legislative proposal delivered; any beneficiary outcome from Humanity Loop. A web search not locating a public dataset does **not** prove it was not submitted internally.

**Existing-solution check:** Government district teams, C&W engineers, the education department, private-school regulators and established reporting already own the response. A new inspection app, crowdsourced child-location map or structural-risk classifier would duplicate work and introduce safeguarding risks. The useful role for Humanity Loop is a **single public-record follow-through**, not unsupervised inspection or accusatory advocacy.

**Next action, owner and trigger:** **Scout/Outcome Verifier, 2026-11-04** (or sooner only upon a substantive official release): review a dated KP education/C&W/ESEA statement or credible follow-up report for **(a)** certified inspection totals/coverage, **(b)** unsafe-school closures and safe student relocation counts, **(c)** repair/financing implementation versus the preliminary PKR 16.2bn estimate, and **(d)** completion of private-school inspections and the governor's proposed report. Preserve differences between public/private and engineering/administrative scopes.
