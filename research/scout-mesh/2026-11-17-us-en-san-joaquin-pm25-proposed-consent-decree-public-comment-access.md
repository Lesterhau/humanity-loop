## Findings
- EPA says Little Manila Rising, Medical Advocates for Healthy Air, and Sierra Club alleged EPA had not taken final action on California's San Joaquin Valley Unified Air Pollution Control District 2024 Plan for the 2012 annual PM2.5 NAAQS. This is a description of a complaint, not a court finding.
- EPA describes a **proposed** consent decree requiring EPA to sign a notice of final rulemaking **by May 28, 2027**. That is an action deadline if the agreement becomes effective; it does not dictate approval, air-quality attainment, or proven health benefits.
