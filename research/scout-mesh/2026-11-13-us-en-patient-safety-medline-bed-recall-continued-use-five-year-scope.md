# US-EN patient safety — Medline 2026 bed recall: conditional continued use and five-year remedy distinction

**Source review:** 2026-11-13 UTC, after the November 13 FDA/company update. **Status:** source-verified, public-safe, **UNSENT** human-review-only patient/caregiver safety brief. **Decision:** distinct actionable information-access candidate, not a verified institutional error, correction, clinical recommendation, formal Supabase Scout completion, or beneficiary outcome.

## Official primary-source evidence

1. **FDA-hosted Medline company announcement, published November 13, 2026:** https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/medline-updates-customers-and-patients-repair-and-replace-program-recall-medline-brand-beds-rails
   - Medline initiated the recall/market exit **October 14, 2026**, covering **all Medline Brand beds and associated rails, mattresses and accessories**; U.S. and Canadian distribution during **2011–2026**. Potential **fire and entrapment** hazards.
   - **As of November 8, 2026**, the company had received reports **related to seven deaths and 35 serious injuries** associated with the issues. This is a reported association, **not a determination of causation** for every case.
   - The FDA publishes the company's announcement as a public service, **not an FDA endorsement**.
2. **Medline November 13 newsroom release (directly opened):** https://newsroom.medline.com/releases/statements-medline-brand-beds/
   - Repeats the updated October 24 **conditional continued-use** instructions: unplug the bed; reconnect power **only while repositioning the patient when necessary** and unplug when not repositioning; **do not use extension cords**; when possible have a second person assist with use/movement.
   - Certain beds may qualify for an **authorized repair**; others may qualify for replacement or full/partial refund. **No consumer should attempt an unauthorized repair or modification.**
   - **Crucial scope distinction:** the potential **five-year purchase window** for some repair/replacement/refund remedies **does not exempt beds bought more than five years ago from the recall/field action or continued-use safety instructions**. The October 24 section says this explicitly.
3. **Medline Beds Customer Recall Resource Center (directly opened, updated November 13):** https://www.medline.com/pages/product-recalls/medline-bed-accessories/
   - Provides the November 13, October 24 and October 14 customer updates, affected-products list **R-25-242-X9**, and a digital safety-label request. The specific product SKU must be checked against the manufacturer's list; **do not assume a generic Medline-branded non-bed product is covered**.
   - The FDA announcement contains **two differently spelled Medline recall URLs**: an earlier URL without the hyphen (`medline-bedaccessories`) and a later one with it (`medline-bed-accessories`). The hyphenated resource-center URL was directly opened successfully. The other URL was inaccessible to the web reader; **ordinary-browser 404 or user impact is not established**. The company release uses the hyphenated URL.
4. **Medline October 24 company update, included on the November 13 newsroom page:** https://newsroom.medline.com/releases/statements-medline-brand-beds/
   - Explicitly says that beds and accessories bought **more than five years ago remain subject** to the field action, although specific remedies may vary. This older text remains relevant to the newer November 13 summary.

## What this adds

**Potentially consequential public-information distinction, not an allegation of a new defect:** A reader could wrongly infer (a) that all recalled beds must be immediately discarded, despite the manufacturer's narrowly conditioned continued-use instructions, or (b) that a bed bought more than five years ago is excluded from the recall because the **remedy** may be limited. Both readings are contradicted by the official sources. Abruptly removing a medically necessary bed without an individualized care plan can also disrupt care; **do not advise an unassessed replacement or continued use contrary to the official instructions**.

## Unsent plain-language safety note — qualified review required

**Medline Brand beds, rails, mattresses and accessories: important November 13 recall update.** Medline says its October 2026 recall covers Medline Brand beds and related products because of possible fire and entrapment hazards. By November 8, the company had received reports related to **seven deaths and 35 serious injuries**.

**Do not assume that a bed is excluded because it was purchased more than five years ago.** Older affected beds remain subject to the safety action, even though the repair, replacement or refund options can differ.

Medline says an affected bed **may remain in use only under its updated precautions until repaired or replaced**: unplug it from power; plug it in only when an adjustment is necessary while repositioning the patient, and unplug it afterward; do not use an extension cord; and, where possible, have a second person assist. **Do not attempt your own repair or modification.** If a patient relies on the bed for safe positioning, ask the care team or equipment supplier to review the individual situation and the exact manufacturer instructions rather than improvising a replacement.

Check the **[official Medline recall resource center](https://www.medline.com/pages/product-recalls/medline-bed-accessories/)** and affected item list. Contact the provider/distributor that supplied the bed; direct Medline purchasers may contact **800-950-0128, option 7**. Report suspected device problems through **[FDA MedWatch](https://www.fda.gov/medwatch/report.htm)**. The November 13 [FDA-posted announcement](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/medline-updates-customers-and-patients-repair-and-replace-program-recall-medline-brand-beds-rails) contains the full instructions.
