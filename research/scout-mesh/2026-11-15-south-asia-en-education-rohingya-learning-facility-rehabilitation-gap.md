## Source-verified facts
UNICEF reports **2,051 Rohingya learning facilities requiring repair or reconstruction**; **828** were prioritized for rehabilitation following Refugee Relief and Repatriation Commissioner approval, including **359** requiring full reconstruction. It separately reports **200 learning centres with minor repairs completed** by implementing partners. It says **1,223 other facilities**, including **251** requiring full reconstruction, remain **unfunded**. UNICEF notes a new assessment may raise reconstruction needs. The arithmetic 828 + 1,223 = 2,051 is consistent with the stated total; the 200 minor repairs cannot safely be subtracted from either group without facility-level overlap/definition evidence.

UNICEF reports **more than 150,000 Rohingya children aged 5–17** (approximately 33%) outside Sector-supported learning. Some may attend private centres or madrassahs, for which comprehensive enrolment data are unavailable. Thus the figure is NOT an estimate of children receiving no education at all. The report separately describes 232,300 children reached by UNICEF and partners and 302,900 reached by the wider education response; these have different programme scopes and must not be summed.
