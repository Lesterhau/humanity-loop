## Evidence and provenance

1. **CPSC, product safety warning 27-089, dated 2026-11-12:** https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2027/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Tomreky-Adult-Portable-Bed-Rails-Immediately-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Entrapment-and-Asphyxiation-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Adult-Portable-Bed-Rails
   - The **Tomreky-branded adult portable bed rails** fail mandatory safety requirements: entrapment/asphyxiation risk in or between rail and mattress, deficient retention straps (fall risk), incorrectly sized push pins/holes (laceration risk), and missing required warnings.
   - **About 8,103 units**, sold on Amazon.com August 2023–September 2026 for about $39. Black tubing, black foam grips, black pocket with **“TOMREKY”** on pocket; about 13 in wide by 16.5 in tall and 5 lb.
   - **CPSC instruction:** immediately stop use, dispose, do not resell or give away. The retailer Ping Tian Wu / Sky Peculiar **has not responded to CPSC requests to conduct a recall or offer a remedy**. This is a **CPSC safety warning, not an announced cooperative recall/refund program**. No incident/injury reports listed as of the warning.
2. **FDA, Recommendations for Consumers and Caregivers about Adult Portable Bed Rails** (general guidance, not product-specific): https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/adult-portable-bed-rail-safety/recommendations-consumers-and-caregivers-about-adult-portable-bed-rails
   - Individual fall/entrapment risk matters; higher-risk users may have confusion, reduced motor control, balance impairment or other limitations. Bed rail, mattress and frame compatibility cannot be assumed.
   - FDA recommends considering alternatives **where appropriate**, such as lowering bed height, roll guards, foam bumpers, concave mattresses or a bed trapeze. In care facilities, an individualized assessment and care plan is important; bed rails do not substitute for monitoring.
3. **CPSC, 2023 adult portable bed rail standard announcement:** https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2023/CPSC-Issues-New-Federal-Mandatory-Safety-Standard-for-Adult-Portable-Bed-Rails
   - General context: mandatory safety requirements were adopted to reduce entrapment and other hazards; **do not apply historical population statistics to this particular Tomreky product**.

## Unsent plain-language caregiver note — human review required

**Important safety warning: Tomreky adult bed rails.** On November 12, 2026, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission warned that these rails can trap a person's body or neck, potentially causing suffocation, and can also create fall and injury hazards. The affected rails are black, with foam-covered grips and a storage pocket labeled **TOMREKY**. CPSC estimates about **8,103** units were sold on Amazon from August 2023 through September 2026.

**CPSC says to stop using these rails immediately, dispose of them, and do not sell or give them away.** This is a federal **product safety warning**; the CPSC notice does **not** announce a retailer refund or replacement program. No incidents were reported in the notice, but that does not make the product safe.

If someone relies on a bed rail for getting out of bed or to reduce falls, promptly involve their caregiver or healthcare professional in a safer replacement/transfer plan. The FDA notes that alternatives and bed setup must be evaluated for the person's circumstances; a different rail is not automatically safe merely because it fits visually. For the exact product photographs and official instructions, use the CPSC warning link above.
