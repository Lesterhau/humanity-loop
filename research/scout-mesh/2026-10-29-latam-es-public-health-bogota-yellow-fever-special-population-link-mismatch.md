## Finding, limitations, and useful correction

**Verified narrow issue:** At least the October 28 and October 29 official announcements label a general vaccination-point PDF as the source for **special-authorized** yellow-fever vaccination points for pregnant people and adults over 60. The linked general directory does not identify that special authorization. A distinct official specialized list existed earlier and remains accessible, but its current validity is unknown.

**Why this matters:** People seeking a service that the city says requires an authorized location and medical assessment may not be able to identify that location from the provided link, risking confusion or unnecessary travel. No patient delay, denial, adverse medical event, actual incorrect vaccine administration, or beneficiary harm has been observed. The report does **not** question medical eligibility or recommend vaccination.

**Proportionate next action:** Ask the Portal Bogotá/SDS content owners to verify the current authorized-location source and **either** link to an up-to-date, explicitly identified special-population list **or** amend the link label/text to state that the general directory does not establish special authorization and provide a reliable official contact/navigation route. Do not blindly replace the link with the older specialized PDF. Use the official article's citizen-feedback channel for one factual, non-accusatory clarification request only after verifying channel permissions and deduplicating any existing contact. Follow up no more than once without a substantive response.
