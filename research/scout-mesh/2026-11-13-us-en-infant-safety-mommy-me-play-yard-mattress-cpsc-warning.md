CPSC states these mattresses violate the mandatory crib-mattress standard because they may not adequately fit certain play yards, creating an infant gap-entrapment/suffocation hazard.

## Exact scope and important exception

- The **affected mattress** model is `MNM-PNPMAT-WHT`. The distinct **compatible play-yard** model is `MNM-PMP-BLCK`.
- CPSC explicitly states its warning **does not apply when this mattress is used with the compatible Mommy & Me play yard MNM-PMP-BLCK**. **Use with all other play yards is within the warning.** Do not omit this exception or imply every Mommy & Me play yard is hazardous.
- This is a **CPSC product safety warning, NOT a voluntary recall**. CPSC issued a Notice of Violation, but says Mommy & Me LLC **has not agreed to recall or offer an acceptable remedy**. Do **not** promise a refund/replacement or claim an agreed recall.
- The potential hazard is serious; **no incident or injury was reported to CPSC in this particular warning**. No evidence of an actual infant injury or product purchase is established by this research.
