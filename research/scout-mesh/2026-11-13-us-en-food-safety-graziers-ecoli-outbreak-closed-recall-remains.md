# US-EN food safety — November 13 E. coli raw-milk-cheese outbreak closure does NOT cancel product recall

**Observation:** 2026-11-13 ~21:15 UTC. **Status:** official-source reviewed; public-safe, **UNSENT** consumer/retailer clarification draft. **Lane:** US-EN public health / food-safety source check (not a completed private Supabase Scout cell). **Decision:** useful safety-status clarification; no agency information defect or institutional correction established.

## Primary-source evidence and exact scope

1. **CDC, updated/reviewed November 13, 2026:** https://www.cdc.gov/ecoli/outbreaks/raw-milk-cheese-09-26/index.html
   - CDC says its investigation of E. coli O26 infections linked to Sierra Nevada Cheese Company **Graziers raw milk cheese** closed November 13.
   - CDC nevertheless instructs people **not to eat recalled cheeses**, to throw them away or return them, and to wash items/surfaces that touched them.
   - CDC states that more than half of outbreak patients were children age 5 or younger. Do not infer the precise count from this phrase.
2. **FDA outbreak investigation, current update November 13, 2026:** https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-e-coli-o26h11-raw-milk-cheese-september-2026
   - Final reported outbreak tally: **18 illnesses across 10 U.S. states; 9 hospitalizations; 3 hemolytic uremic syndrome (HUS) cases; 0 deaths**. Illness onset July 7–September 20, 2026. 15 of 15 interviewed patients reported consuming Graziers raw milk cheese.
   - Opened raw medium cheddar sample from a California patient's home matched outbreak strain by whole-genome sequencing; a Michigan sample tested positive for a **different** E. coli strain. Do not conflate the two laboratory findings.
   - FDA labels investigation **ended/closed**, but says the recalled product has a **long shelf life** and remains a home/foodservice risk if retained. FDA says check refrigerators/freezers; if packaging was removed and identity cannot be determined, discard; clean/sanitize touched surfaces and containers.
   - FDA says recalled product was distributed nationally and to the **Republic of Korea**; this is distribution evidence, **not** proof of Korean cases or Korean recall enforcement.
3. **FDA-hosted company recall, company announcement September 28 / FDA posting September 29, 2026:** https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/sierra-nevada-cheese-company-recalls-graziers-raw-milk-cheese-because-possible-health-risk
   - All **Graziers raw milk** Monterey Jack, Jalapeño Jack, Medium Cheddar and Sharp Cheddar products identified in the notice, including 8-oz/16-oz retail packs and 5-lb/40-lb wholesale blocks. No lot/date-code limitation established in the company notice.
   - Retail UPCs: Monterey Jack 8 oz **6-87652-16400-9**; Jalapeño Jack 8 oz **6-87652-16403-0**; Medium Cheddar 8 oz **6-87652-16506-8**; Sharp Cheddar 8 oz **6-87652-16507-5**; Medium Cheddar 16 oz **6-87652-16501-3**. Wholesale item numbers and images are on the FDA notice.
   - Company notice was written while the outbreak was under investigation and cites **13 illnesses**; that is a **historical figure**, superseded by FDA's **November 13 final 18**, not a contradiction in current case counts.
   - Company announced return for refund or disposal. The FDA's November 13 update does **not** establish recall termination.

## Public-safe draft for qualified food-safety editorial review (NOT SENT)

**The E. coli outbreak investigation is over. The cheese warning is not.** On November 13, CDC and FDA closed their investigation into Sierra Nevada Graziers raw-milk cheese after 18 illnesses, 9 hospitalizations and 3 cases of serious kidney complication (HUS) across 10 states. No deaths were reported. The closure **does not make recalled cheese safe**.

Check for Graziers **raw milk** Monterey Jack, Jalapeño Jack, Medium Cheddar or Sharp Cheddar, including refrigerated or frozen packages. Do **not** eat, serve or sell recalled product. FDA advises discarding it or returning it to the seller for a refund. If packaging was removed and you cannot tell whether cheese is included, discard it. Clean items and surfaces that contacted it. If you ate recalled cheese and develop symptoms such as severe cramps, diarrhea (especially bloody diarrhea), vomiting or fever, contact a healthcare professional promptly. Product images and package identifiers: [FDA company recall](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/sierra-nevada-cheese-company-recalls-graziers-raw-milk-cheese-because-possible-health-risk).
