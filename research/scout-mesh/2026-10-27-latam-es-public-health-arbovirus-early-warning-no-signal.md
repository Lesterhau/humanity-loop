## Signal and novelty assessment
- **STEEP+:** Environmental (El Niño / seasonal mosquito habitats), social (household prevention and access to diagnosis), technological (entomological surveillance and integrated data), institutional/political (national and PAHO-led prevention), economic (outbreak-response resource constraints), ethical (avoid stigmatizing communities or misreading surveillance counts).
- **TIPPO / Three Horizons:** Weak signal = continued vector activity outside expected peaks; H1 = existing sentinel surveillance and local vector-control interventions; H2 = more integrated, anticipatory cross-border surveillance; H3 = resilient early-warning infrastructure with equitable local action. A plausible transition, not a unique unserved gap.
- **Futures Triangle:** Push = climate/epidemiological shifts; pull = integrated early warning and earlier care; weight = limited local capacity, data comparability, and resource constraints (specific magnitude unverified).
- **Cross-impact:** Weather shifts can alter vector distribution and testing demand; more surveillance can increase reported cases independently of actual transmission. Raw case increases alone do not establish an outbreak or intervention failure.
- **Existing solutions / duplication:** Argentina's Ministry of Health already operates sentinel monitoring, household prevention, training and diagnostic response; PAHO has an explicit regional strategy. No partner request for a new tool, proven missing service, or narrow contribution opportunity was identified in the inspected material.
- **Counterevidence / falsification:** A documented municipality with no functional access to published warnings or diagnostics, an official unmet request for volunteer technical help, or evidence of a specific broken public-information link could justify a new bounded verification. Conversely, functioning local response and no explicit unmet request favor no action.
- **Decision:** `no_signal`; no new outreach, software, agent, or medical warning. The absence of a selected Humanity Loop intervention does **not** imply absence of public-health need. Future review only on a new, source-verified gap or normal Scout cadence.
