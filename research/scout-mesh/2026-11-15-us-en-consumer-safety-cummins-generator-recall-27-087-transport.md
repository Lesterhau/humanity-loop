# Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI generator — CPSC 27-087 and manufacturer transport precautions (UNSENT)

Observed: 2026-11-15 UTC. Scope: independent public-source consumer-safety cross-check; not an original defect discovery or external notification.

## Primary sources
- CPSC recall 27-087 (November 12, 2026): https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Cummins-Recalls-Portable-Generators-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards
- Cummins Safety Campaign #7308: https://www.cummins.com/en-na/na/generators/portable-generators/safety-recalls/campaigns/7308

## Verified cross-check
CPSC identifies about 2,960 Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel portable generators. Incorrectly installed fuel-venting valves can cause tank overpressure, fuel discharge or fuel-cap ejection and fire/burn hazards. CPSC reports 11 incidents, including one consumer splashed in the face with fuel; no medical attention was needed. Recall remedy is **free authorized-service repair**, not a general refund. Consumers should stop using recalled units immediately.

Manufacturer independently specifies **model P5000iDF EFI + part A075C500** and a May 2025–August 2026 sales period. Cummins adds actionable transport restrictions: let unit cool to touch (typically 15–20 minutes); keep upright, secured, tilt no more than 15 degrees; if fuel remains, cap sealed and transport well-ventilated. **If tank is swollen/bulging, gasoline smell or leak is present, do NOT transport or further handle; keep away from ignition sources and contact Cummins for guidance** (1-800-286-6467). Confirm the service location is authorized and schedule before transport. Manufacturer's current page was read directly; service availability and individual unit eligibility were not tested.

## Human-review-only public safety clarification draft
“Cummins has recalled certain Onan P5000iDF EFI portable generators (part A075C500). Stop using affected units. Check the model/part label against Cummins Safety Campaign #7308 and arrange a free authorized-service repair. **Do not transport or handle a generator with a bulging tank, gasoline odor or fuel leak; keep it away from ignition sources and contact Cummins for instructions.** See the official CPSC recall and Cummins campaign for identification and safe transport details.”
