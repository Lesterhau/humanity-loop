# Housatonic Rest of River — PCB extraction-method comparability study: public-input source crosswalk

Observed: 2026-11-16 UTC. Status: Tier-0 official-source review; UNSENT, no external outreach, no technical finding of biased measurements.

## Verified public participation
EPA's Housatonic Rest of River permit-submittal page currently lists GE's **Extraction Method Comparability Study Work Plan** as open for public input through **Friday, November 20, 2026**. EPA states this is **not a formal public comment period**; input can be sent to R1Housatonic@epa.gov. EPA cautions that submitted emails and personal information may become public and recommends contacting EPA for confidential submission alternatives. This is an EPA-provided opportunity for input, not proof of any submitted comment or agency response.

Source: https://www.epa.gov/ge-housatonic/opportunities-input-housatonic-rest-river-permit-submittals

## Primary document facts
GE's October 16, 2026 transmittal reports that independent laboratory SGS changed soil/sediment PCB extraction from approved **SW-846 Method 3546 (microwave)** to **Method 3570 (microscale)** in April 2023 without the change being specified in the EPA-approved site FSP/QAPP. GE states Method 3570 was used for relevant 2023–2025 samples and Method 3546 resumed for 2026 samples. Method 3570 is itself an EPA-published method: the site-specific plan mismatch does **not** alone prove invalid PCB data or environmental harm.

The work plan proposes a **75–100 sample** comparison, at least 15 per stated medium/concentration grouping, comparing PCB Aroclor results with the two extraction procedures. Its stated purpose is to determine whether 2023–2025 data can be used directly or need a statistical adjustment. The work plan notes its sample selection generally targets non-detect through approximately **100 mg/kg**, while a few original samples exceeded that range. GE requested EPA concurrence on sample counts by October 23; whether EPA granted concurrence is not established by this review.

Source: https://semspub.epa.gov/src/document/01/705207 (transmittal pp. 1–2; plan pp. 3–9).

## Bounded qualified-review questions (not accusations)
1. Does the selected sample set adequately represent each matrix, the high-end outliers and the remedial decision thresholds?
2. How will the study distinguish extraction-method bias from sample heterogeneity and detection-limit differences, including nondetects?
3. What independent QA, split-sample review and decision rules will determine whether historical data need correction or resampling?
4. Has EPA already concurred with sample counts, and are any revisions publicly available?
