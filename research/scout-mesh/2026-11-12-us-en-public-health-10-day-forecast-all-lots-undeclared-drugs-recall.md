## Official source crosswalk

1. **Primary recall (FDA-hosted Game Changer Products LLC company announcement, dated and FDA-posted Nov 12, 2026):** https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/game-changer-products-llc-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-10-day-forecast-capsules-due-presence
   - Product: **10 Day Forecast capsules 3200mg**, marketed for male sexual enhancement; 1–2 blue capsules per card; **UPC 191645852717**; black background with white lettering. **ALL lots and ALL expiration dates** are included, not just a single batch.
   - FDA analysis detected **undeclared sildenafil and acetaminophen**. The recall announcement says the product is an unapproved new drug, not a legally marketed dietary supplement.
   - Company describes sales through its website and eBay, plus one Minnesota retail store, from **at least October 2021 through October 17, 2026**, nationwide including Puerto Rico, Guam and the Northern Mariana Islands. This is a reported **sales period**, not a claim that every unit remains on sale or was purchased.
   - Sildenafil may interact with nitrate medicines (e.g., nitroglycerin), causing dangerous low blood pressure. Undisclosed acetaminophen may be added unknowingly to other acetaminophen-containing products, raising overdose/liver-injury risk. **No specific quantity of either drug was supplied in this notice**; do not calculate a dose or imply all users suffered injury.
   - The company reports **no adverse-event reports received to date** in its announcement. This is not proof that no events occurred.
   - Company says consumers should stop using and safely discard remaining product; contact a healthcare professional for health concerns; MedWatch reporting is available.
2. **Independent FDA background on sildenafil in concealed-ingredient sexual enhancement products:** https://www.fda.gov/drugs/medication-health-fraud/vitality-may-be-harmful-due-hidden-drug-ingredients (Feb 10, 2025). Confirms nitrate interaction mechanism, but concerns **a different product**; not an additional 10 Day Forecast recall or patient count.
3. **Independent FDA acetaminophen safety reference:** https://www.fda.gov/drugs/safe-use-over-counter-pain-relievers-and-fever-reducers/acetaminophen (retrieved Nov 12, 2026). Taking too much acetaminophen can cause severe liver damage, liver failure and death. This general guidance does not establish an actual dose in the recalled capsules.
4. **Separate same-day FDA-hosted company recall (NOT the same product):** https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/eros-apothecary-issues-recall-candy-power-man-organic-honey-due-undeclared-tadalafil (Nov 12, 2026). Do not mix up the **all-lots sildenafil/acetaminophen capsule recall** with the **batch-specific tadalafil honey-candy recall**.

## Review-only, unsent consumer safety card

**Recall: “10 Day Forecast” 3200mg capsules — ALL lots/expiration dates.** The Nov 12 FDA-hosted company recall reports undeclared **sildenafil** (a prescription erectile-dysfunction drug ingredient) **and acetaminophen** (a pain/fever medicine ingredient). Product identification: black-and-white 1–2-blue-capsule card, UPC **191645852717**. The company reports sales online/eBay and limited retail distribution from at least October 2021 to October 17, 2026, including US territories.

**If you have this product:** Follow the recall announcement: **stop using it and safely discard unused capsules**. If you have used it and have concerns or symptoms, contact a healthcare professional. Sildenafil can dangerously lower blood pressure with nitrate medicines; additional undisclosed acetaminophen may raise liver-injury risk if combined with other products. Report suspected adverse effects via FDA MedWatch: https://www.fda.gov/medwatch/report.htm . Do not infer that other sexual-enhancement products are included; check the exact product and UPC.

**Different recall:** The same day, FDA also hosted an unrelated recall for **Candy Power For Man Organic Honey**, containing undeclared **tadalafil** (specific batch/UPC). The two announcements should not be conflated.
