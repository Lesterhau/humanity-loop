## New primary-source evidence obtained this review

1. **Official 2026–27 HEAP Desk Guide, LDSS-5005 (Rev. 08/26), page 1:** https://events.trainingspace.ny.gov/heap/materials/2026/rest-of-state/forms/HEAP%20Desk%20Guide%20(LDSS-5005).pdf . Retrieved and visually inspected page 1 on Nov 12. The *Regular Heater Base Benefits* table specifies **$990** for oil/kerosene/propane, **$700** for wood/pellets/coal/corn/other, and **$400** for natural gas or electric utility heat. The separate *Emergency Benefit Amounts* table specifies **$990** and **$700** for the corresponding delivered-fuel categories. Regular add-ons are **$61** (Tier I) and **$35** (vulnerable household). The desk guide also shows heat-included **$50** (Tier I), **$45** (Tier II), and **$21** (Heat and Eat). This is a **reference guide**, explicitly requiring use alongside OTDA policy/procedures and the HEAP Manual; amounts are not individual eligibility or payment guarantees.
2. **State of New York public HEAP application page:** https://www.ny.gov/services/apply-heating-assistance-heap . Fresh official-domain web-reader retrieval Nov 12: Overview still says **“2025-2026 Regular HEAP benefit is scheduled to open November 2, 2026”**. Its Regular benefit table explicitly labeled **2025–26** shows oil **$900**, wood **$635**, gas/electric **$400**. The separate Emergency benefit table is also explicitly labeled **2025–26** and shows **$900/$635**. Its **2026–27** income limits already match the desk guide (one person **$3,563**, four people **$6,852**); Emergency **2026–27** is described as scheduled for **January 4, 2027**.
3. **ACCESS NYC current 2026–27 HEAP guidance:** https://access.nyc.gov/programs/home-energy-assistance-program-heap/ . Fresh official city retrieval Nov 12 confirms **2026–27 Regular HEAP opened November 2, 2026**, with Emergency HEAP **scheduled** for January 4, 2027. City evidence corroborates opening status but is not a substitute for state benefit-policy authority.
4. **Erie County Regular HEAP:** https://www3.erie.gov/heap/regular-benefits . Nov 12 direct official-county retrieval lists *anticipated* **$990/$700** delivered-fuel amounts, independently consistent with the state-hosted LDSS-5005 desk guide.
