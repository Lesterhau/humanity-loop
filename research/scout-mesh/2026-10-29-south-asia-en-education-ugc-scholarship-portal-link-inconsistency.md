## Primary evidence (reviewed October 29–30 UTC)

1. **UGC Student Corner** — https://www.ugc.gov.in/Home/student_Corner . The numbered `Portals/Helplines for Students` entry **Web Portal for Fellowship/Scholarship Legacy Cases** (item 6) links to `https://scholarship.canarabank.in/`; the same page's `Quick links` section instead links **Web portal for Fellowship/Scholarship Legacy Cases** to `https://scholarship.canarabank.bank.in/`. The mismatch is present on one official UGC page, without any assumptions about the user's scholarship eligibility.
2. **UGC GATE/GPAT postgraduate scheme page** — https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship2 . Its `Canara Bank Portal` remark links to the old `.in` hostname; the page's `Quick links` uses the `.bank.in` hostname. The page's scheme details and amounts were **not** independently revalidated; only the link inconsistency is in scope.
3. **Pharmacy Council of India notice dated March 23, 2026** — https://www.pci.gov.in/media/documents/Announcement_of_disbursement_of_Pradhan_Mantri_Scholarship_for_Pharmacy.pdf . PCI expressly says the old `scholarship.canarabank.in/PCI/InstUser.aspx` address for its specified GPAT-related scholarship route is no longer in use and gives `https://scholarship.canarabank.bank.in/PCI/AdminLogin.aspx` instead. **Scope caution:** this is evidence for the PCI route's migration, not blanket proof that every UGC program has migrated or that every old URL fails.
4. **Current portal observed** — https://scholarship.canarabank.bank.in/ rendered the Canara Bank Scholarship and Fellowship Management Portal login screen in the independent web reader on October 29–30. The legacy `https://scholarship.canarabank.in/` returned a 502 in the same web reader. This is a **tool-specific access observation**, not a successful normal-browser reproduction or a universal availability claim.
5. **Additional bounded observation:** https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship3 displays the old domain as the *link text* for its SC/ST postgraduate scheme but the actual hyperlink destination in the parsed official page is `scholarships.gov.in`; this is a label/target inconsistency, **not** evidence that the hyperlink itself points to the old Canara host.

## Why this matters and what remains unverified

The official site presents two different scholarship portal destinations for apparently similar navigation labels. Students may waste time or choose an obsolete entry point, but **no actual student failure or missed payment was observed**. The current Canara portal's login page is not proof that it handles every UGC scholarship listed on older scheme pages. Before proposing a precise site edit, UGC or the relevant scheme administrator should confirm the intended destination for each entry; avoid mechanically replacing all old links with one unverified portal.

**Novelty/duplication:** No prior Humanity Loop public repository search hit for `canarabank`; earlier South Asia Scout work covers different topics. The PCI notice shows a pre-existing institutional correction for its own scholarship path, so the domain migration itself is not new.

**Recommended smallest action:** One neutral factual UGC website-maintainer clarification, quoting the two exact UGC page locations and asking which destination is intended; no applicant details, no allegation, no mass contact. Follow up at most once under governance, and only if no response after a reasonable interval. Independently re-check the exact page after any reply or at the scheduled review (2026-11-06). If a specific link changes, credit UGC and report the exact report→correction sequence without claiming exclusive causation.
