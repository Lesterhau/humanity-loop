# Huffy Buzz Cerana T2 e-trike recall — source-reviewed consumer safety brief (UNSENT)

Review date: 2026-11-15 UTC. Cell: US/English mobility and consumer safety; independent public-source research, not a private Supabase Scout completion.

## Primary source and verified scope

CPSC recall index, dated **November 12, 2026**: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls . The index identifies **Huffy Buzz Bicycles Cerana T2 E-Trikes**, approximately **286 units**. It states that rear wheels can loosen or separate from the rear frame, creating a fall hazard. CPSC directs owners to **stop using affected tricycles immediately** and contact Huffy for **free replacement rear axle assemblies**. Assemblies can be sent to owners or a third-party repairer; Huffy states it will reimburse up to **$100** for third-party repair charges. Official recall contact: https://www.buzzbicycles.com/recalls ; service@huffy.com ; (855) 430-6240.

## Consumer-facing clarification candidate (NOT published)

If you own a Buzz Cerana T2 electric tricycle, check the official Huffy recall information before riding. A rear-wheel axle defect can cause wheel separation and a fall. Stop using an affected unit; Huffy offers a free replacement axle assembly and may reimburse third-party installation up to $100. Verify product identification and eligibility with Huffy; do not assume all Huffy bicycles are recalled.
