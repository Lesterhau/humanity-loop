# 2027 HealthCare.gov enrollment: stale CMS March 2026 SEP job-aid footnote vs current official dates

**Observed:** November 11, 2026 UTC. **Scout cell:** US-English/Spanish, public health-insurance access / federal guidance integrity. **Status:** independently source-reviewed public-safe finding and unsent editorial clarification. No CMS contact, enrollment, account access, claimant data, or verified beneficiary outcome.

## Narrow verified inconsistency

CMS's still-accessible [Special Enrollment Periods (SEP) Job Aid, Version 3.0, March 2026](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/special-enrollment-periods-march-2026.pdf), **printed page 18, footnote i**, says that starting with plan year 2027 the federal Marketplace Open Enrollment Period (OEP) changes to **November 1–December 15**, and that state-based Marketplaces must end by December 31. That statement accurately describes the **then-planned 2025 Marketplace Integrity and Affordability rule**, but is no longer a safe statement of the **current** 2027 HealthCare.gov deadline.

CMS's [FFE Enrollment Manual effective October 1, 2026](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/ffe-enrollment-manual-2026.pdf), section **2.3, printed page 15**, explicitly specifies the current federal-platform selection/effective-date pairings, and footnote 8 says that after the **June 12, 2026** order in *City of Columbus v. Kennedy*, the OEP dates in 45 CFR 155.410(e)(4) were **not altered**. The separate [CMS July 31 statement, printed page 2](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/statement-re-city-columbus-080426.pdf) expressly says federal Exchange OEP is **November 1, 2026–January 15, 2027**. The [CMS October 30, 2026 PY2027 Marketplace Plans and Prices Fact Sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/plan-year-2027-marketplace-plans-prices-fact-sheet-0) independently confirms those dates.

Both current consumer pages, [HealthCare.gov English FAQ](https://www.healthcare.gov/get-answers/) and [CuidadoDeSalud.gov Spanish FAQ](https://www.cuidadodesalud.gov/es/get-answers/), independently show the **two different deadlines**:

| HealthCare.gov plan selection | Coverage effective date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 1–Dec 15, **2026** | Jan 1, **2027** | Current federal-platform guidance |
| Dec 16, **2026**–Jan 15, **2027** | Feb 1, **2027** | Current federal-platform guidance |
| After Jan 15, 2027 | Outside ordinary federal-platform OEP; a qualifying Special Enrollment Period or other program eligibility may apply | Do not infer automatic ineligibility for Medicaid/CHIP |

**Important scope:** State-run Marketplaces can have different enrollment calendars and effective-date rules. The March job aid's **state-marketplace Dec 31 assertion is also not reliable as current guidance**, but this review does **not** assert a single replacement date for every state. Check each state's official Marketplace. Court litigation and operational guidance can change; do not generalize the June order to every provision of the 2025 rule. This review is about one outdated footnote, not the accuracy of the entire SEP job aid or a finding of actual enrollment denials.

## Public-facing applicant note — English

**2027 HealthCare.gov coverage:** Federal Marketplace Open Enrollment runs **November 1, 2026–January 15, 2027**. For a plan beginning **January 1**, select by **December 15, 2026**; selecting **December 16–January 15** ordinarily starts coverage **February 1**. Confirm plan selection and pay the first premium to the insurer for coverage to take effect. For states with their own Marketplace, verify dates on the state's official site. Use [HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/get-answers/) and its free application-assistance options. Do not share private insurance information with Humanity Loop.

## Unsent, narrowly scoped CMS editorial correction

**Subject: March 2026 SEP Job Aid footnote i — superseded 2027 Open Enrollment dates**

The March 2026 Special Enrollment Periods Job Aid (Version 3.0, printed p. 18, footnote i) remains publicly accessible and says 2027 federal OEP ends December 15, 2026, and state OEPs must end December 31. CMS's October 2026 FFE Enrollment Manual, October 30, 2026 PY2027 fact sheet, July 31 legal statement, and current English/Spanish HealthCare.gov FAQs instead confirm that the federal-platform OEP continues through January 15, 2027 (December 15 is the cutoff for January 1 effective coverage). Please consider marking the March footnote **superseded** on the PDF landing page or revising it, and directing readers to the current 2026 FFE Enrollment Manual and state-specific enrollment calendars. We are not alleging an operational enrollment failure. **DRAFT ONLY — NOT SENT.**
