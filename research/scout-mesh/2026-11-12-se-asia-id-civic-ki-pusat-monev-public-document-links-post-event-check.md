## Assessment

**Observed signal:** Two named publication PDF links exposed by the official e-Monev portal yielded 404 in the automated public reader. A separate official reports index exposed no documents in extracted text. These are **candidate document-access problems** meriting independent ordinary-browser verification, particularly if a visitor needs the Monev methodology or historical report. The link names alone do not establish that either PDF was intended to be the *2026 final report*.

**Not established:** that a human browser sees 404; that the 2026 final report was due or withheld; that KI Pusat failed to evaluate any body; that the portal's displayed zeros are real; that a citizen was harmed; or that a government correction has occurred.

**Falsification tests:** In an ordinary uncached Indonesian browser, visit the official portal, open the two publication menu links, capture resulting URL/HTTP status and any redirect or PDF. Check whether documents are instead hosted on a different official publications page. Inspect any posted 2026 Monev report date, full 385-body roster, scores, and public-feedback mechanism before proposing changes. If both links work normally, close this as a web-reader-only artifact.
