## Interpretation and limits

The old 2025–26 season being closed is historically true. The service-access concern is that pages currently offered as program guidance **omit the open 2026–27 season** and continue to describe a **superseded 60+ HERR restriction**. This could discourage eligible readers, especially homeowners younger than 60, but there is **no verified individual denial, missed application, program-system failure, agency acknowledgment, or beneficiary harm**.

The web reader's crawl dates do **not** prove an uncached live-origin response or ordinary mobile/desktop browser rendering. A separate direct HTTPS no-cache attempt in the local container failed DNS resolution before any HTTP response; this does **not** prove the public website is unavailable. A guessed simplified-Chinese locale URL could not be opened and is not counted as a broken official link.
