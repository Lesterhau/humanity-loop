## Direct official sources and observations

1. **Georgia DFCS, SNAP Policy Manual Appendix A** — https://pamms.dhs.ga.gov/dfcs/snap/appendix-a-food-stamp-income-limits/ — directly retrieved in the 2026-11-10 UTC review. The page identifies **Effective Date: October 2025** and labels its table **Effective October 1, 2025**. It lists 130% gross $1,696, maximum allotment $298 (1 person); 130% gross $3,483, maximum allotment $994 (4 people); minimum allotment $24. It also links a FY26 basis-of-issuance table.
2. **Georgia DFCS, SNAP Policy Manual §3125, Disaster Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program** — https://pamms.dhs.ga.gov/dfcs/snap/3125/ — directly retrieved in the same review. Its header identifies **Effective Date: October 2025**. The chart explicitly covers **October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026**, with one-person D-SNAP gross-income screen $2,258 and maximum allotment $298; four-person D-SNAP gross-income screen $3,647 and maximum allotment $994. **Important qualifying language in this same section:** D-SNAP income limits are adjusted annually, and *current limits will be provided when D-SNAP operations are initiated*. D-SNAP is available only in a separately authorized disaster operation; the listed D-SNAP income limits are **not** regular SNAP limits.
3. **USDA FNS, FY2027 SNAP COLA** — https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/snap/allotment/cola — directly retrieved in the same review. It explicitly applies **October 1, 2026–September 30, 2027**. For the 48 states and DC, it lists one-person gross 130% $1,729, maximum allotment $306, minimum 1–2 person allotment $25; four-person gross 130% $3,575 and maximum allotment $1,023.
