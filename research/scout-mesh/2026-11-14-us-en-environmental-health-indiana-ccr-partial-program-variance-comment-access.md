## Evidence crosswalk

- **Actual written-comment deadline:** Federal Register DATES explicitly says comments must be **received on or before Monday, November 16, 2026**. EPA's shorter project page says 60 days after Federal Register publication without restating the exact end date. Use the **express Federal Register November 16 date**, not mental arithmetic from the 60-day summary or an unverified third-party deadline.
- **Correct docket:** **EPA-HQ-OLEM-2026-4326**. The Federal Register authorizes Regulations.gov and **Docket_OLEM@epa.gov** (include docket in email subject), plus physical alternatives. Comments may be posted with personal information; submitted docket comments cannot be edited or removed. The Regulations.gov interactive docket/comment form returned HTTP 403 to the research reader, so its live status, attachment list, and any later extension were **not independently verified**. Do not claim a filing occurred.
- **What is proposed:** EPA preliminarily proposes **partial approval** of Indiana's coal combustion residuals permit program, shifting permitting of approved portions to IDEM while retaining federal provisions not approved and EPA's oversight/enforcement authorities. IDEM identifies **101 CCR units** in the Federal Register record; that is the agency's inventory, not an independently audited count.
- **Material protectiveness nuance:** Federal Register IV.B.3, printed pp. 58623–24, explicitly proposes **not** to include Indiana's 329 IAC 14-1-6 hardship-variance provision in the federally approved program. EPA reasons that the state text is not expressly restricted to state-only requirements and could allow criteria below federal protectiveness standards despite its protective-language proviso. EPA says an amended variance provision would require specific state-only scope and new EPA review. This is **EPA's preliminary analysis**, not proof that a variance was granted, that a facility violated a rule, or that all of Indiana's program was rejected.
- **Other partial-program exceptions:** The Federal Register separately identifies federal provisions remaining applicable regarding remanded vegetative cover, groundwater monitoring suspension, standards for constituents without MCLs, and the February 2026 CCR management-unit deadline amendments. A state approval would **not** erase these federal requirements.
- **Process timing:** The public hearing was scheduled for November 12 and is in the past as of this review. IDEM says its 329 IAC 14 rule becomes effective contingent on final EPA approval; the proposal alone is not final authorization.
