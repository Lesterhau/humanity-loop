## Verified observation
The proposed **new** MEPDES permit for Bold Coast Seafood LLC in Gouldsboro, Maine concerns two different discharges to Prospect Harbor, Class SB:
1. Outfall **001A**: treated seafood-processing wastewater, proposed monthly-average flow limit **120,000 gallons/day**, with TSS, BOD5, oil/grease and other requirements (proposed permit pp. 2, 6–7).
2. Outfall **002A**: **untreated flow-through seawater** from live-animal holding/transport tanks, proposed monthly-average flow **120,000 gallons/day**. Proposed permit p. 8 says **no chemicals or food added** and states this outfall is **not monitored due to the nature of discharge**. This is an explicit proposed monitoring distinction, not proof of pollution or an unlawful exemption.
3. For 001A, proposed nitrogen monitoring is report-only during May–October of 2027 and 2028 (p. 7); the permit does not thereby establish a numeric nitrogen discharge limit.
4. Maine DEP's Oct 19 transmittal says written comments must be **received by close of business Wednesday, Nov 18, 2026**, at DEP, 17 State House Station, Augusta, ME 04333-0017. It describes **physical submissions** and provides a staff email for questions; **email filing acceptance and exact closing hour are not confirmed**. After Oct 28, it directs questions to Licensing Coordinator Laura Crossley. EPA's notice agrees on Nov 18.
5. The draft order has an unsigned signature/date field. Treat approval as **proposed**, not a final effective permit.
