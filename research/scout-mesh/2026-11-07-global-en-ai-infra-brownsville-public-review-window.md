Treat this later municipal timetable as the working expectation, subject to later official revisions.

As of this scout, a signed final ordinance/minutes was **not independently retrieved** from the City's official records portal.

## Finding, novelty and practical use

**Verified public-interest opportunity:** Brownsville has a formally constituted local review body with a near-term public reporting milestone and a specific official mandate to consider infrastructure, environmental, community, and fiscal consequences of data centers. This creates a bounded opportunity to evaluate whether its eventual public report contains decision-useful and comparable evidence, without assuming the report is deficient in advance.

**Not a proven correction case:** The October 28 city page uses pre-vote wording, appropriate when originally published. A later vote does not automatically make that historical news item erroneous. No final ordinance text, current operative permit status, current task-force report, specific facility's water/power data, or evidence of resident harm was independently verified in this run.
