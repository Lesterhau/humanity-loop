## Material new primary-source finding

The Ministry of Education's [PM-USP CSSS guidelines (applicable AY 2022–23 onward)](https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/upload_document/PM_USP_CSSS_guidelines_updated.pdf) distinguish **loss of a particular renewal year** from **permanent disqualification**:

- Section 4(vii) allows a student who missed a renewal application to apply in a *subsequent year* if renewal eligibility conditions are met (PDF lines 62–64).
- Section 6(ii), (vi) delegates opening/closing dates and latest timeline guidance to NSP; section 6(vii) says delayed renewal/verification does not permanently debar a student, **but missing that year's renewal cut-off means no renewal for that year** (PDF lines 96–115). This is NOT permission to submit a late 2026–27 renewal or a guarantee of later eligibility or payment.
- Section 11 provides a public grievance route via [CPGRAMS](https://pgportal.gov.in/) or the relevant NSP scheme nodal officer (PDF lines 149–154). The current [NSP Public Corner](https://scholarships.gov.in/Public) exposes Grievance Registration, NSP Helpdesk, and scheme-wise nodal-officer search; [NSP Officers](https://scholarships.gov.in/Officers) exposes scheme-wise Grievance Redressal Officers. These links were checked from official public pages; no form was submitted.
- [CPGRAMS official homepage](https://pgportal.gov.in/) states public grievances should be lodged **on the portal rather than by email**, and that the government does not charge a filing fee. No complaint was filed or account accessed.

## Review-only applicant safety guidance (not externally distributed)

1. **If already submitted:** Use only the official NSP login to check personal status, and ask the institution's nodal officer to verify promptly. The earlier public institute date is November 15, so do not rely on the later December 15 listing without written scheme-owner clarification.
2. **If not submitted:** The scheme-specific public listing says November 30 while the announcements say October 31. Check actual availability through the applicant's own official NSP account, not a third-party claim; request clarification through the official NSP grievance/helpdesk or scheme-wise nodal/grievance officer. Do not promise that a new submission is accepted.
3. **If this year's deadline was missed:** The Ministry guidelines say missing a year does **not** permanently debar a qualifying student, but renewal for the missed year is not permitted after the controlling cutoff. Confirm subsequent-year conditions with the official scheme authority. Do not claim that a missed 2026–27 award can be recovered automatically.
4. **Privacy and cost:** Never send OTR, Aadhaar, passwords, bank details, or identifiable application screenshots to Humanity Loop. Do not pay a third party to submit a government grievance.
