# Alaska WIC 2026–27: 11-person weekly income threshold appears copied from 12-person row

**Observation date:** 2026-11-12 UTC  
**Status:** New Tier-0 official-source comparison; **UNSENT** editorial correction candidate, not an agency report or eligibility determination.  
**Scope:** Alaska WIC public applicant guidance, **weekly** income limit for a household of 11 only. Other fields and operational eligibility screening were not audited.

## Primary-source evidence

1. **State of Alaska Department of Health**, *Women, Infants and Children (WIC) Program*, section "Income guidelines for July 1st 2026 to June 30th 2027": https://health.alaska.gov/en/services/division-of-public-assistance-dpa-services/wic/  
   On 2026-11-12, the public web-search reader returned official-domain content last crawled about two weeks earlier. It lists the **11-person** row as annual **$168,258**, monthly **$14,022**, twice monthly **$7,011**, biweekly **$6,472**, **weekly $3,489**. The **12-person** row also lists weekly **$3,489**. The row for 10 persons lists weekly **$2,984**. The page's additional-person increment is **+$253 weekly**. Direct origin retrieval in the web reader returned HTTP 403; **this is not an independently verified uncached live browser observation**.
2. **USDA Food and Nutrition Service**, *WIC Income Eligibility Guidelines 2026–2027*, published **April 29, 2026**, effective **July 1, 2026**: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/wic/fr-042926 . The USDA **Alaska, household size larger than 8** table gives **11-person weekly $3,236**, with the same annual **$168,258**, monthly **$14,022**, twice-monthly **$7,011**, and biweekly **$6,472**. USDA lists 12-person weekly **$3,489** and an increment of **+$253**. The USDA source was retrieved on November 12.

## Exact discrepancy

| Household size | Alaska WIC applicant page, weekly | USDA 2026–27 Alaska weekly | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | $2,984 | $2,984 | $0 |
| **11** | **$3,489** | **$3,236** | **+$253** |
| 12 | $3,489 | $3,489 | $0 |

**Interpretation:** The Alaska page's 11-person weekly value matches the 12-person value, despite its own +$253 incremental rule. All other income-frequency values in the 11-person row match USDA. This strongly suggests a single-cell public-table transcription error, but state program policy and its SPIRIT eligibility system have **not** been independently inspected. This is **not** proof of incorrect eligibility screening, any applicant's loss of benefits, or agency wrongdoing. The state page also notes that participation in certain other assistance programs can establish income eligibility; applicants should not self-disqualify or self-approve solely from this table.

## Public-safe, UNSENT editorial correction proposal

For Alaska Department of Health WIC staff review only:

> In the public 2026–27 WIC income chart, please verify the **11-person weekly gross-income** amount. The page currently shows **$3,489**, the same as its 12-person row. USDA's effective July 1, 2026 Alaska WIC guideline lists **$3,236** for 11 people and **$3,489** for 12 people. The Alaska page's other four income-frequency figures for 11 people match USDA. If confirmed, please consider changing only the 11-person weekly cell to **$3,236** and checking any linked downloadable charts or translations. No conclusion is drawn about actual eligibility processing.
