# Fort Fairfield wastewater permit: public-comment access and proposed E. coli safeguards (UNSENT)

**Checked:** 2026-11-16 UTC. **Scope:** independent read-only official-source review; not an allegation of unlawful discharge or deficient treatment.

## Primary-source crosswalk

1. [EPA official notice, published October 20, 2026](https://www.epa.gov/me/draft-permit-fort-fairfield-utilities-district-fort-fairfield-me-me0100226) identifies the **proposed draft**, permit **ME0100226**, Fort Fairfield Utilities District, Aroostook County, Maine, and the **October 20–November 20, 2026** public-comment period.
2. [Maine DEP proposed permit and transmittal, October 20, 2026](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-10/draftme0100226permit-2026.pdf), transmittal p. 1, says written comments **must be received by the close of business Friday, November 20**, at Maine DEP, Bureau of Water Quality, Division of Water Quality Management, 17 State House Station, Augusta, ME 04333-0017. The notice does **not** define an exact clock cutoff or establish electronic submission as an authorized alternative. Confirm with DEP before relying on email or postal delivery near deadline.
3. The draft permit, permit-summary pp. 1–2, proposes lowering the **daily maximum E. coli limit from 949 to 236 colonies/100 mL** and expanding the disinfection season from **May 15–September 30 to April 15–October 31**. Draft special condition A, p. 4, lists monthly geometric mean **126/100 mL**, daily maximum **236/100 mL**, twice-weekly grab sampling during that season. It also proposes adjusted BOD/TSS mass limits, total residual chlorine limits and updated Aroostook River dilution factors. These are **proposed**, not confirmed newly effective requirements.
4. Draft special condition A, pp. 5 and 8, proposes monthly phosphorus effluent and upstream ambient sampling during June–September 2027 and 2028, with a DEP-reviewed ambient sampling plan. The permit is a renewal of authorization for **0.600 million gallons/day monthly average** secondary-treated wastewater to the **Aroostook River**, not an assertion that this amount is continuously discharged.

## Public-interest utility and uncertainties

This creates a timely, neutral public-participation checklist: (a) identify proposed bacterial and seasonal protections; (b) distinguish effluent limits from evidence of actual compliance; (c) note that paper comments must **arrive** by close of business, not merely be postmarked; (d) ask DEP to confirm the clock cutoff and whether electronic delivery is accepted; (e) seek qualified wastewater/environmental and local/Tribal participation review before any outreach or publication. The transmittal copies Mi'kmaq Nation and Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians contacts; copying alone does not prove consultation or endorsement.
