## Verified facts and boundaries
- **Product:** SHEIN Distribution Corporation baby rattle and teether toy sets, seven pastel-colored toys; package SKU **sk25070936134444119**. The purple and orange knobs may detach.
- **Hazard:** Violation of the mandatory small-parts ban for toys intended for children under three; detached knobs pose choking risk.
- **Units:** approximately **2,657**. **Sold:** US.Shein.com, November 2025–July 2026, approximately $10.
- **Incidents/injuries:** **none reported** in CPSC notice. Do not claim a documented choking injury.
- **Consumer action:** stop using immediately, remove from children, and contact SHEIN for a refund. CPSC states consumers will be asked to affirm disposal. Official recall page points to https://us.shein.com/product-recalls/list ; the live refund workflow has **not** been independently tested.
- **Not established:** current possession, individual exposure, refund completion, manufacturer remediation beyond the notice, or actual beneficiary effect. The SKU identifies this set; do not extrapolate to all SHEIN teethers.
