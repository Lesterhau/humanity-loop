## Primary-source observations

1. Aidvantage's official Federal Student Aid servicer [Notices](https://aidvantage.studentaid.gov/notices) and [homepage](https://aidvantage.studentaid.gov/) are indexed with the following announcement: **Effective November 12, 2026**, mailed payments must be sent to **U.S. Department of Education, P.O. Box 4163, Binghamton, NY 13902**. The notice explicitly says the effective date **changed from November 4**. It states that payments sent to P.O. Box 4163 before November 12 will be forwarded for processing. It offers online payment and Auto Pay as alternatives. Source observed via official-domain indexed text in the public web search on November 13 UTC; direct HTML reader returned a JavaScript shell rather than this notice's content.
2. The same official servicer's [Contact Us](https://aidvantage.studentaid.gov/contact-us) was indexed (crawl labeled last week) with **Aidvantage – Federal Student Aid Loan Servicing, P.O. Box 4450, Portland, OR 97208-4450** under **Loan payments**. Its separate **general correspondence** address is P.O. Box 300001, Greenville, TX 75403-3001, and must not be confused with payment mailing.
3. Its official [About payments](https://aidvantage.studentaid.gov/in-repayment/about-payments/) was indexed (crawl labeled approximately two months old) with **Portland P.O. Box 4450** repeated in the instructions for mailed checks, one-time allocation, and saved billing instructions. The same page tells borrowers to include their account number and allow mailing time. The older crawl is **not evidence that Portland instructions remain in the live November 13 page**.
4. Federal Student Aid's [student-loan scam guidance](https://studentaid.gov/articles/avoid-student-loan-forgiveness-scams/) directs borrowers to their StudentAid.gov dashboard to identify the correct servicer and warns against disclosing account passwords to supposed servicers. This is independent federal guidance, not independent verification of the new postal address.

## Public-information issue and evidence limit

**Potential conflict:** The official November 12 effective-date announcement gives Binghamton, while the most recently retrieved indexed Contact Us and About payments text still contains Portland. Because the current Aidvantage pages rendered as navigation-only JavaScript shells in the public reader, and a direct independent HTTPS request from the analysis environment failed DNS before any HTTP response, **we cannot establish that the live uncached detailed pages still display Portland on November 13**. Search index lag could explain it. This is a high-value **version-parity check**, not a proven currently published error, and it does not establish failed delivery, late posting, borrower delinquency, or financial harm.

Do not generalize this address to Nelnet, MOHELA, CRI, Edfinancial or other servicers. Do not infer that mail sent to Portland after the change will be forwarded; the notice only explicitly discusses mail sent to **the new Binghamton address before November 12**. Do not assume an old billing statement or saved bank bill-pay address has been updated.
