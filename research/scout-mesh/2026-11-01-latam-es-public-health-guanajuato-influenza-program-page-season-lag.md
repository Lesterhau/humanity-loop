## Independently retrieved primary sources

1. **State health program page, retrieved November 1, 2026:** https://salud.guanajuato.gob.mx/programas/influenza — headline expressly says **“Este invierno 2025-2026”** and gives a previous-season start of October 13, with a **1.7 million influenza-dose** goal, **522,000 COVID-19 doses**, and **264,000 pneumococcal doses**. The page is under the standing Programs > Influenza navigation, rather than an obvious dated news archive. It *labels* its season, so it is not evidence that the figures are falsely presented as 2026–27.
2. **Guanajuato government bulletin, dated October 12, 2026:** https://boletines.guanajuato.gob.mx/2026/10/12/ssg-aplicara-mas-de-1-7-millones-de-vacunas-contra-covid-e-influenza-en-campana-invernal/ — announces the **2026–27** campaign, with **1,009,806 influenza** and **713,999 COVID-19** planned doses; states **377 vaccination points** will be enabled.
3. **Separate government bulletin, also October 12, 2026:** https://boletines.guanajuato.gob.mx/2026/10/12/ssg-establece-mas-de-900-puntos-de-vacunacion-para-campana-invernal/ — repeats the same 2026–27 dose targets and says **more than 900 points**, explained as **311 additional points plus more than 600 existing units**. The 377 and >900 numbers might describe different categories or scopes; this review does **not** establish a false site-count or that either is the precise number accessible to a particular resident.
4. **State healthcare network directory:** https://salud.guanajuato.gob.mx/somoslideres/ — describes **638** state healthcare establishments; these are not necessarily all campaign vaccination points. This provides context for different denominators, not a reconciliation.

## Finding, novelty and action threshold

The official standing influenza program page visibly remains on the 2025–26 season after the government announced the 2026–27 campaign on October 12. A visitor navigating the program section may encounter prior-season dose totals rather than the current campaign's figures. The dates make the mismatch identifiable, and newer official news exists, so **a potential low-risk page freshness/navigation improvement** is supported. This is not evidence of a failed vaccine service, a shortage, a false 2026–27 claim on the old page, or actual patient confusion. The two October bulletins have different site counts, but without scope definitions it is premature to call them contradictory operational data.
