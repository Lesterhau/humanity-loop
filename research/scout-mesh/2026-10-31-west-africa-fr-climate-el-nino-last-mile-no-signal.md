## Source-reviewed observations

1. **Regional seasonal forecast, not a village-scale warning.** AGRHYMET's October 23 French [technical note N°4 (PDF)](https://agrhymet.cilss.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Note_technique_AGRHYMET_MAJ-prevision_octobre2026.pdf), pages 1, 3 and 6–7, forecasts a contrasting OND 2026 transition: below-normal rain in portions of Senegal, Guinea, Sierra Leone and Liberia; near/above-normal rain in some central areas; locally wet conditions in parts of Nigeria and the Gulf of Guinea. NDJ 2026/27 has a different time horizon and potential deficits around Ghana/Togo/Benin/southern Nigeria. Above-normal heat and localized flooding remain plausible. These are **probabilistic regional forecasts**, not evidence of a current outage, a named community's exposure, or an individual flood event.
2. **Independent large-scale corroboration with limits.** [WMO October 8 ENSO update](https://wmo.int/media/news/sea-surface-temperatures-reflect-strengthening-el-nino) expects a strong event and shifts in seasonal rainfall, while its [October 26 humanitarian outlook](https://wmo.int/media/news/wmo-global-seasonal-climate-outlook-informs-humanitarian-action) explicitly distinguishes seasonal patterns from individual-event predictions and points to national/regional forecasts for action. Neither establishes a West African alert-channel failure.
3. **Existing last-mile improvement initiatives are already underway.** AGRHYMET's [October 13 WISER CLICK/ACCORD co-validation report](https://agrhymet.cilss.int/2026/10/13/services-climatiques-agrhymet-renforce-le-passage-de-la-prevision-climatique-a-la-decision/) identifies timeliness, local scale, appropriate formats, user feedback and sector-specific decisions as explicit design priorities. Its [October 29 AGRHYMET–WFP meeting report](https://agrhymet.cilss.int/2026/10/29/agrhymet-ccr-aos-et-pam-la-science-climatique-au-service-de-la-securite-alimentaire-pour-des-communautes-resilientes/) describes possible further joint early-action work; it is **not proof** that any proposed project is funded or operational. AGRHYMET also offers an [October 20 user-feedback survey](https://agrhymet.cilss.int/2026/10/20/7260/) in French and English. Do not submit a response without being an actual user.
4. **National current forecast access is not independently established by this Scout.** Search-index results for ANACIM's Senegal site showed an October 29–November 1 short-range forecast and October 30 video updates, but direct fetches of its homepage returned timeout/502 in this environment. This is an **access limitation of this check**, not evidence the national service is down or that residents cannot receive warnings.
