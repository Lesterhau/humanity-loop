## Narrow discrepancy and why it matters

The **December 9 mail postmark instruction in the news release conflicts with the explicit December 11 mail postmark instruction in the formal Proposed Plan**. The English and Spanish community flyers support December 11 as the general comment deadline but are ambiguous about the postal rule. A resident relying on the news release could believe the paper-mail opportunity ends **two days earlier** than the Proposed Plan says. Conversely, this evidence alone cannot establish which date EPA would actually apply when processing a mailed submission. Do **not** assert that a December 10–11 postmark would be accepted or rejected.

This is a **verified inter-document public-information inconsistency**, not evidence of an actual rejected comment, lost public participation, improper cleanup decision, or institutional acknowledgment. No public-facing correction has been independently verified.

The proposal concerns perchlorate-contaminated groundwater in Newfield and Vineland. The preferred remedy is **monitored natural attenuation with institutional controls and long-term monitoring**, with conditional point-of-entry treatment for affected private-well users if necessary. The plan is **proposed, not adopted**. The Proposed Plan also notes a preexisting OU1 well-restriction area from 2021; do not portray all restrictions as entirely new or claim every nearby private well is contaminated.
