# Scotch TL1302Z thermal laminator — CPSC recall 27-024 (UNSENT)

Observed 2026-11-15 UTC. Tier-0 public-source safety review; human editorial review required before distribution.

Primary source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/3M-Company-Recalls-ScotchTM-Thermal-Laminators-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-Hazard (directly read Nov 15, 2026). Manufacturer recall route linked by CPSC: https://www.scotchbrand.com/3M/en_US/scotch-brand/tl1302Z-laminator-recall/ (not independently tested for form completion).

## Verified official notice
- Recall dated October 15, 2026; recall number 27-024; about 53,634 units.
- **Exact model TL1302Z**; label on bottom. Light gray/white Scotch laminator, approximately 13-inch slot and right-side carrying handle. Do not extrapolate to all Scotch laminators.
- Overheating/fire hazard; 63 smoking/melting/overheating reports, including five fires and one minor property-damage report; **no reported injuries**. Counts are firm reports, not independently verified cases.
- Sold via Amazon.com June 2024–September 2026, $44–$56.
- CPSC says stop using and **unplug immediately**; full-refund procedure through 3M. CPSC describes an unplugged power-cord cut photograph as a required part of the refund submission. Avoid handling/cutting any cord while connected to power; consumers should follow official recall instructions and seek assistance if unable to perform safely. Refund is a mailed check after required confirmation, not an automatic cash payment.
- CPSC directs disposal under applicable local/state electronics-recycling laws; not ordinary household-trash disposal by default.

## Unsent consumer-information draft (human-review only)
If you own a Scotch thermal laminator, check the label underneath for **TL1302Z**. CPSC recalled this model on October 15, 2026 because it can overheat and catch fire. Stop using it and unplug it. Check the official CPSC recall and 3M's linked instructions for a full refund and safe electronics disposal. The reported incident count includes five fires but no reported injuries; this is not a recall of every Scotch laminator.
