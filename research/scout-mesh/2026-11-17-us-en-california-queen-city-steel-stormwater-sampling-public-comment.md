# Public-source scout: Queen City Steel industrial stormwater monitoring and public comment

Observed: 2026-11-17 UTC. Status: independent Tier-0 source review, NOT an agency report, adjudicated finding, submission, or demonstrated environmental outcome.

## Official evidence
- EPA Region 9 public notice, published 2026-11-16: https://www.epa.gov/publicnotices/CWA-09-2026-0138
- EPA California enforcement case summary: https://www.epa.gov/ca/CWA-09-2026-0138

EPA alleges that Patton Sales Corp, doing business as Queen City Steel in Burbank, failed to sample stormwater at all discharge locations, failed to sample enough qualifying storm events in reporting years since at least 2021, and collected no stormwater samples in the 2021–2022 reporting year. EPA also alleges deficiencies in best management practices, employee training, and stormwater pollution prevention plan implementation. The agency says the respondent documented corrective actions; the scope and ongoing effectiveness of those actions have NOT been independently tested here.

EPA proposes an expedited Class I Clean Water Act administrative settlement for $9,750, docket **CWA-09-2026-0138**, industrial permit **CAZ490776**. The public notice gives **December 18, 2026** as comments due and requires a written submission identifying the docket, including commenter full name and mailing address and intent to participate. EPA lists Region 9 Regional Hearing Clerk, r9hearingclerk@epa.gov, for submissions; language/disability assistance is offered without charge. Do not send on behalf of Humanity Loop absent separate authorization.

## Public-interest relevance and limits
The alleged sampling omissions create an evidence gap about historical industrial stormwater discharges, but do NOT establish a pollutant exceedance, contaminated receiving water, injury, or current violation. The proposed penalty is not a final order. This is a public-access and compliance-evidence lead, not an allegation of concealment.
