# Oregon LIHEAP/OEAP: PY2026 public guidance versus published PY2027 manual — 2026-11-10 UTC

**Status:** Primary-source verified public-documentation mismatch; **UNSENT** editorial correction candidate. No agency contact, applicant outcome, agency error in processing, or correction established.

## Scope and evidence (official Oregon sources, checked November 10, 2026 UTC)

1. Oregon Housing and Community Services (OHCS) public applicant-facing [Oregon Energy Assistance Programs](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/energy-weatherization/Pages/utility-bill-payment-assistance.aspx) still labels its eligibility section **"Income Guidelines for Oregon (Program Year 2026)"**, and explicitly specifies **October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026**, despite being accessible during November 2026. It describes 60% of Oregon state median income and sends applicants to local community action agencies. In the text-only web reader the promised income table was not visible after "See the table below"; **this is not proof that a table is absent in a fully rendered browser**.
2. OHCS [Manuals and State Plans](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/energy-weatherization/Pages/energy-service-provider-resources.aspx) explicitly links a **2027 LIHEAP and OEAP Program Operations and Policy Manual** as well as the older 2026 manual.
3. The official [2027 LIHEAP/OEAP manual (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/energy-weatherization/Documents/manuals/09-23-2027-PY-2027-EA-Operations-Policy-Manual.pdf), PDF pp. 2 and 71 (zero-based pages 1 and 70), states **effective October 1, 2026–September 30, 2027; published October 1, 2026**. Section Five gives the FFY2027 60%-SMI eligibility figures.
4. The official [2026 manual (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/energy-weatherization/Documents/2026%20Final%20Energy%20Assistance%20Intake%20Operations%20%26%20Policy%20Manual.pdf), PDF pp. 2 and 77, states effective October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026 and provides the previous 60%-SMI thresholds.

| Household size | Expired FFY2026 monthly gross limit | Current FFY2027 monthly gross limit | Increase |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $3,198.75 | $3,360.50 | $161.75 |
| 2 | $4,183.00 | $4,394.50 | $211.50 |
| 4 | $6,151.42 | $6,462.50 | $311.08 |

These are **eligibility income screening guidelines**, not promised payments, and local provider procedures, funding availability, additional requirements and other eligibility factors apply. The applicant page does not display the expired numeric table in the available text-only extraction; do **not** claim it directly publishes the old numeric limits without ordinary-browser confirmation. The mismatch is its **expired year/date reference** versus OHCS's already published operative 2027 manual.

## Proposed editorial correction — NOT SENT

On the public Energy Assistance Programs page, replace the PY2026 eligibility heading and October 2025–September 2026 date with the current PY2027 effective period, link directly to the OHCS 2027 manual's Section Five eligibility table, and provide an accessible household-size table or accessible equivalent. Preserve the existing direction to local community action agencies for individual screening and available funding. Confirm the actual rendered page, translated versions, and any separate OEAP program details before institutional contact.
