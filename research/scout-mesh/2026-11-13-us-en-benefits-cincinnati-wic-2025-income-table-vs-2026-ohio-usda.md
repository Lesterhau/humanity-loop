## Primary-source evidence

1. **Cincinnati Health Department applicant-facing WIC program page:** https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/health/chd-programs/women-infants-and-children-wic-program/ . Public web reader retrieved a copy (crawl within the prior week) showing an income table labeled **"Effective July 1, 2025"**, with 1-person **$28,953 annually / $2,413 monthly**, 2-person **$39,128 / $3,261**, 3-person **$49,303 / $4,109**, and 4-person **$59,478 / $4,957**. It says the guidelines are updated yearly and gives 513-821-7012 for questions. Exact reader lines 71–90. The page is an active general WIC program page with office locations, not an explicitly archived news item.
2. **USDA WIC 2026–27 Income Eligibility Guidelines**, published 2026-04-29, effective 2026-07-01 to 2027-06-30: https://www.fna.usda.gov/wic/fr-042926 . For the 48 contiguous states, at 185% of poverty, the 1/2/3/4-person figures are **$29,526 / $2,461**, **$40,034 / $3,337**, **$50,542 / $4,212**, **$61,050 / $5,088** (annual/monthly respectively). The federal notice allows state-specific income rules within specified limits; check state implementation before treating federal maximum as the only applicable local criterion.
3. **Canton City Public Health WIC Income Eligibility**, Ohio local public program page: https://cantonohio.gov/2031/WIC-Income-Eligibility . It expressly labels its table **"Effective July 1, 2026"** and displays the same four rows as USDA (annual/monthly) plus biweekly/weekly values. This is an independent Ohio-government confirmation of current implementation. The Ohio Department of Health eligibility page also appeared in official-domain indexed results with those values, but the direct web-reader open returned 404, so do not cite that open as independent live proof: https://odh.ohio.gov/know-our-programs/women-infants-children/resources/ohio-wic-program-eligibility .

## Falsification / limitations

- The web reader's Cincinnati content was a cached public retrieval (reported crawl about six days before this review); direct uncached JS-enabled ordinary-browser exposure was **not independently established**. A more recent search index still showed the old figures. The candidate is therefore a **source-parity mismatch in currently retrievable public content**, not proof that every visitor sees it right now.
- Canton and Cincinnati are separate local jurisdictions. Their matching state/federal numbers support a review request but do not constitute Cincinnati's own admission or a binding adjudication of a specific applicant.
- The 2025 Cincinnati values were correct for their labeled historical effective period; the defect candidate is their continued placement as the primary applicant-facing table after the 2026–27 update, not an arithmetic error in the 2025 table.
- The Ohio state WIC page's direct reader returned 404, so do not misstate it as a successful current direct fetch.
- No outreach, correction, program action, independent contributor, or beneficiary impact was observed.
