# FERC rulemaking public participation: wrong paper-filing docket copied across three official pages
**Observed:** 2026-11-14 UTC (direct public-page reads during Humanity Loop Scout review)
**Cell:** `global-en-ai-infra` — grid/data-center large-load public participation
**Status:** verified public-page text inconsistency; **UNSENT** editorial correction candidate for qualified review
**Authority:** public official sources only; no FERC contact, filing, form submission, or regulatory intervention

## Primary-source evidence
1. FERC's large-load interconnection rulemaking landing page, <https://www.ferc.gov/rm26-4>, heading **Docket No. RM26-4-000**, says in its *How to Participate* callout: “Tip: In your filing, reference Docket No. RM26-4-000.” But under **3. File a paper copy**, it instructs the reader to reference **Project Docket No. P-14890** instead. Page footer says last updated July 22, 2026. This page explicitly includes communities concerned about data centers, grid reliability and affordability.
2. FERC's hydro post-licensing rulemaking page, <https://www.ferc.gov/rm26-3>, identifies **RM26-3-000**, with the same incorrect **P-14890** paper-mail instruction. The page expressly states comments were due **February 10, 2026**; this finding does not reopen that deadline. Footer last updated July 22, 2026.
3. FERC's preliminary hydro permit rulemaking page, <https://www.ferc.gov/rm26-5>, identifies **RM26-5-000**, again with the same **P-14890** paper-mail instruction. Footer last updated January 12, 2026.
4. FERC's separate **Pushmataha County Pumped Storage Hydroelectric Project** page, <https://www.ferc.gov/pushmataha-county-pumped-storage-hydroelectric-project>, explicitly identifies **P-14890** as the Pushmataha project docket and appropriately uses that number for paper comments **on that project**. Its historical public-comment window ended November 5, 2024. This independently identifies P-14890 as a *different proceeding*, rather than an alternate number for the RM rulemakings.
5. FERC's generic filing instructions, <https://www.ferc.gov/how-file-ecomment>, say mailed paper comments must clearly identify the **project name and docket number on the first page**, and list `RM` as an eligible eComment docket prefix. This corroborates why a mismatched paper-filing docket number is consequential. The generic guide was last updated March 5, 2026.
6. The FERC notice of November 7, 2025, <https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/ferc-issues-notice-extending-comment-period-proposed-anopr-interconnection-large>, separately labels the large-load rulemaking **RM26-4-000**.

## Finding and limits
**Verified:** Three distinct official FERC `RM` rulemaking pages instruct paper commenters to put the unrelated hydro project number `P-14890` on their letters, contradicting the same pages' docket headings and electronic-filing guidance. The exact repeated wording strongly suggests a shared-page/template editorial error, but its technical cause is not verified.

**Plausible consequence, not established outcome:** A person following the paper-mail instructions could mislabel a submission. We have **no evidence** that any comment was actually misrouted, rejected, delayed or received under the wrong docket. We have **not** verified whether FERC would internally correct such a submission. We have **not** verified a currently open comment window for any of the three proceedings; readers must consult current docket-specific notices and deadlines, not assume the older “How to Participate” text grants an open period. The finding is about **public filing instructions**, not the substance of FERC's energy policy.
