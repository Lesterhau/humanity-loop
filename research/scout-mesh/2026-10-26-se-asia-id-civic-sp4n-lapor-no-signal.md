## Narrow research question

Can a bounded, nonduplicative public-interest intervention be justified by the 2026 SP4N-LAPOR! national coordination agreement and Ombudsman's Cilacap Sapa Daerah pilot, beyond restating institutions' existing work?

## Primary-source evidence independently reviewed on 26 October 2026

1. **Ombudsman RI, 29 September 2026:** Five national parties signed a memorandum to coordinate management of the public-service complaint platform **SP4N-LAPOR!**. Ombudsman is the **external overseer**, with the agreement covering governance, monitoring/evaluation, communication, public participation and complaint information across agencies. This is a signed cooperation framework, **not evidence that every local channel is already interoperable or that complaints are resolved**. Source: https://ombudsman.go.id/news/r/ombudsman-ri-perkuat-pengawasan-eksternal-sp4n-lapor
2. **Ombudsman Central Java, press release dated 14 August, published 15 August 2026:** Its **Sapa Daerah** local complaint-response pilot was handed to Cilacap government, initially at the public-service mall and RSUD Cilacap hospital, with a proposed phased expansion. This establishes a pilot and identified owners, **not measured response times, accessibility outcomes or confirmed integration with SP4N-LAPOR!**. Source: https://ombudsman.go.id/perwakilan/news/r/pwksiaran--ombudsman-jateng-perkuat-pengawasan-pelayanan-publik-melalui-inovasi-sapa-daerah-di-kabupaten-cilacap
3. **Ombudsman RI, 23 July 2026:** Meeting with Komdigi explicitly raised remote-region internet access, digital service interoperability, complaint channels and privacy, and proposed faster complaint handling and stronger joint review. This demonstrates that the broad problems already have a named responsible regulator and overseer; a public meeting does **not** prove a technical fix. Source: https://ombudsman.go.id/pers/r/ombudsman-ri-dan-komdigi-bahas-peningkatan-kualitas-pelayanan-publik-digital

## Existing-solutions and duplication check

SP4N-LAPOR! already has a national multiagency governance path. The Ombudsman has an oversight role, and Sapa Daerah already pilots local response at two named public services. A new generic complaint portal, open-ended awareness campaign, or speculative integration accusation would duplicate established efforts. Earlier Humanity Loop Indonesia Scouts already examined Makassar Lontara+ accessibility, welfare access, and the separate Batin Tikal municipal complaint-link defect. The Batin Tikal issue remains under its own November 1 follow-up; this review neither reopens it nor treats it as evidence that SP4N-LAPOR! or Sapa Daerah is broken.

**What was NOT independently verified:** a broken SP4N-LAPOR! route; a failure of Sapa Daerah to accept complaints; an unmet accessibility standard in the Cilacap pilot; a documented failed interagency handoff; published response-time data showing an unresolved breach; or a specific request from a maintainer for an outside technical contribution. No account, form submission, user record, or live-system testing was performed.

## 10th-Man check and decision

The announcement of an interagency agreement is not an implementation receipt. The pilot's existence does not establish its performance. Conversely, missing outcome figures in these announcements does **not** prove a service failure. Without a reproducible specific defect or a responsible maintainer's stated need, further contact or an unsolicited integration proposal would have poor marginal value.
