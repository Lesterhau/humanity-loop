## Official-source findings

1. EPA's live [public notice](https://www.epa.gov/npdes-permits/proposed-stormwater-permit-washington-department-transportation-ms4) and [2026 draft-document landing page](https://www.epa.gov/npdes-permits/draft-npdes-stormwater-permit-washington-department-transportation-ms4-washington) both expressly state that the comment deadline for **WAS026743** was **extended from October 9 to November 23, 2026**. Some older indexed EPA listings still display October 9; those are not the current live notice. Exact clock cutoff and any later extension remain unverified.
2. The proposal concerns WSDOT-owned/operated municipal separate storm sewer outfalls on federal trust lands within the 1873 Survey Area of the Puyallup Reservation and/or discharging directly to Puyallup Tribe surface waters, including the lower Puyallup River. EPA reports that 2021 and 2022 draft rounds were not finalized and this 2026 proposal reflects revisions; this is **not** an issued final permit or a finding of violation.
3. EPA's current notice instructs comments **or hearing requests** by email to **EPAR10WD-NPDES@epa.gov**, subject **“Comments on Draft NPDES Permit WAS026743.”** It asks for commenter name, address, telephone, factual basis; hearing requests additionally identify issues to be raised. For people unable to email, EPA lists Misha Vakoc at **206-553-6650**. Do not send on Humanity Loop's behalf without separate authority.
4. EPA identifies the **Puyallup Tribe** as the Clean Water Act Section 401 certifying authority; the Tribe's certification process is distinct from the EPA permit-comment route. EPA says it will assess neighboring-jurisdiction effects after receiving final certification. Do not conflate these pathways or imply the Tribe approved the draft.
5. EPA's public notice says accessibility accommodation requests should go to Misha Vakoc at least **one week before the end of the comment period** (nominally November 16 for the Nov 23 deadline); whether late requests can be accommodated is unknown. Do not discourage anyone from requesting accommodations after that recommendation.
