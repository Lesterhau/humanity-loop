## Distinct verified public-interest finding

India's official National Scholarship Portal (NSP) publishes a **Common Service Centre (CSC) assisted application option**. Its [student announcements](https://scholarships.gov.in/ViewMoreAnnouncement) explicitly fix the **total service charge at ₹30 per candidate, inclusive of taxes**, for the *entire listed activity*: generating a reference number, biometric authentication, generating the One-Time Registration (OTR) number, and filing the complete scholarship application. The portal links to a [CSC locator](https://locator.csccloud.in/). This is a practical assisted route for students who cannot complete online authentication or application unaided; it is **not free**, and availability at any particular CSC has not been independently confirmed.

The same official announcement states OTR is required for NSP scholarship applications from academic year 2024–25, can be generated with Aadhaar/Aadhaar enrolment ID, and is valid across the student's academic career. For disabled applicants it says to give consent through the UDID portal before applying. For failed face authentication, it suggests checking Aadhaar biometric-lock status and the UIDAI face-authentication app; these are sensitive identity operations to be performed **only by the applicant/authorized guardian using official channels**, not by Humanity Loop. The announcement itself links [UIDAI's biometric lock/unlock FAQ](https://uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support/have-any-question/1012-english-uk/faqs/aadhaar-online-services/aadhaar-lock-unlock.html).

### Time-sensitive application information, not a guarantee of portal acceptance

- [NSP scheme listing](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) on 2026-11-10 displays **National Means Cum Merit Scholarship** student application open through **2026-11-15**, institute verification through **2026-11-30**, and higher-level verification through **2026-12-15**. This applies to that listed scheme, not all scholarships.
- The same scheme listing displays **PM-USP CSSS renewal** student applications through **2026-11-30**, institute verification through **2026-12-15**, higher-level verification through **2026-12-31**.
- However, [NSP's student announcement](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students) still says PM-USP CSSS renewal student closing date **2026-10-31**, institute **2026-11-15**, L2 **2026-11-30**. This **unresolved same-portal contradiction** was already recorded in [the Nov 10 deadline-conflict investigation](2026-11-10-south-asia-en-education-india-nsp-pm-usp-renewal-deadline-conflict.md); the current research adds an **assisted-access/price** pathway and does not pretend to resolve that discrepancy.

## Human-review draft for public service navigation (UNSENT)

> Applying for an NSP scholarship but need assistance? The National Scholarship Portal says its listed CSC-assisted registration/authentication/application service has a fixed **₹30 total charge per candidate including taxes**. NSP links to the CSC locator at https://locator.csccloud.in/. Verify that your chosen centre currently offers the service before traveling, and keep your own Aadhaar/OTR credentials private. Scholarship deadlines vary by scheme: check https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships and the logged-in application status. The official PM-USP CSSS renewal dates currently conflict between two NSP pages, so do not assume the later date guarantees submission access; seek authoritative clarification. Humanity Loop has not tested a CSC transaction or submitted an application.
