## Independently checked published summary values
PAHO EW41: 1,877,925 cumulative suspected cases; 54% below corresponding 2025 period and 61% below five-year average. PAHO EW42: 1,914,591 cumulative suspected cases; 53% below corresponding 2025 period and 61% below five-year average. Arithmetic difference is **36,666 additional cases in the reported cumulative totals**, not proof of 36,666 infections acquired in a single week: retrospective reporting/revisions and coverage can affect cumulative comparisons. Both numbers are **suspected**, not confirmed cases. The regional decline masks local increases, as PAHO warned October 21. No verified official error or local outbreak inferred.
