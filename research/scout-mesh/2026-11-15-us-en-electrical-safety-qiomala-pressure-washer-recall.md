# Tier-0 external public-interest source check — QIOMALA pressure washer electrical hazard

Date: 2026-11-15 UTC. Status: official-source review, UNSENT; no external outcome claimed.

## Primary source
- U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), recall listings, November 12, 2026: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls (also https://m-recalls.cpsc.gov/).

## Verified facts
CPSC's November 12 listing reports a recall of 80 QIOMALA pressure washers because the power cord is inadequately long and may encourage extension-cord use, posing shock and electrocution hazards. CPSC instructs consumers to immediately stop using the recalled washer and contact QIOMALA at QIOMALA@163.com for a full refund. CPSC describes a photo-based destruction process: unplug the washer, cut the power cord in half, and photograph the destroyed product showing the model number. Do not attempt any destruction or handle electrical equipment while connected to power; refer to the current CPSC recall instructions and product identification before acting.
