# Housatonic Rest of River — PCB extraction method comparability and public input (UNSENT)

Observed: 2026-11-15 UTC. Status: official-source research only; qualified technical/editorial review required before any outreach.

## Verified primary sources

1. EPA public-input page: https://www.epa.gov/ge-housatonic/opportunities-input-housatonic-rest-river-permit-submittals . EPA lists the *PCB Extraction Methods Comparison Study Work Plan* as open for suggestions through **Friday, November 20, 2026**, via R1Housatonic@epa.gov. EPA explicitly says this is **not a formal public comment period**, and warns that submitted emails and personal information may be disclosed publicly; confidential submissions require contacting EPA about alternative methods. No cutoff hour is given.
2. EPA-hosted GE/consultant work plan, transmitted **October 16, 2026**: https://semspub.epa.gov/src/document/01/705207 . Cover letter and introduction explain that SGS changed soil/sediment PCB extraction from SW-846 Method 3546 to Method 3570 in April 2023; 3570 is an EPA-published method but was **not specified in the EPA-approved site FSP/QAPP**. GE states it learned of the change in July 2026. The lab returned to Method 3546 for 2026 samples. This is a documented method-deviation and planned comparability assessment, **not proof the PCB results were wrong, contamination was understated, or cleanup decisions were invalid**.
3. Work plan pp. 4–5 and 8 (document printed pages; PDF pages 5–6, 9): comparison seeks to determine whether 2023–2025 PCB Aroclor data can be used directly or require adjustment for statistically significant bias. Approximately **75–100** samples are proposed across soils and sediments. Results and EPA acceptance of the plan are not established by this document.

## Public-interest relevance and cautious questions for qualified review

- Which 2023–2025 design or cleanup decisions, if any, used data extracted with 3570, and what interim data-quality qualifications apply pending the study?
- Will EPA publish its decision on the comparability study, the final sample list, and any adjustments/reanalysis in an accessible form?
- How will the study handle concentration ranges, matrices, laboratory uncertainty, and possible selection bias when extrapolating to the earlier dataset?

These are questions, not allegations. The EPA page does not establish that the work plan has been approved, that the study is complete, or that the public has submitted any input.
