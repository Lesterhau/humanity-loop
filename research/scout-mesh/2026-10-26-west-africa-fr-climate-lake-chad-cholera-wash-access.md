# Scout Mesh — Lake Chad cholera/WASH access: documented localized service gaps amid existing responses

**Review:** 2026-10-26 (America/Chicago) / 2026-10-27 UTC. **Cell:** `west-africa-fr-climate` (West Africa; French; climate adaptation / water / energy). **Owner:** Humanity Loop main worker, Tier-0 public-source review. **Decision:** `verified_candidate` — documented unmet WASH access needs, **not** a novel intervention, completed service delivery, or authorized external outreach.

## Source-level findings

1. **Cameroon–Chad border, 21 September 2026 (UNICEF Cameroon, French).** UNICEF and local partner AAEDC describe community cholera prevention in the Nguéli/Kousséri border area. Their reported reach was **7,070 people in 2,056 households** plus nearly 900 in community sessions. **571 households in the intervention areas still lacked latrines**. These are UNICEF programme figures, not independently audited household census or an updated October needs assessment. Source: https://www.unicef.org/cameroon/fr/recits/riposte-au-chol%C3%A9ra-une-sensibilisation-sans-fronti%C3%A8re

2. **Chad's Lac province, 23 October 2026 (UNICEF Chad, French).** Authorities registered **more than 75,000 internally displaced people** in Fouli/Kaya across 16 sites during May–September 2026; Fourkoulom reportedly hosted **approximately 12,000 people**. UNICEF, CIAUD and the Ministry of Water and Energy report **163 shared family latrine/shower cabins benefiting 3,260 people** and **1,600 hygiene/water-treatment kits**. A community representative says existing latrines remain insufficient amid arrivals. **Do not subtract 3,260 from 12,000 to claim an exact number without sanitation access**: denominators, shared use, timing and other providers are not established. The story also cites insecurity and rainfall deficit, but does not quantify a climate-attributable cholera burden. Source: https://www.unicef.org/chad/fr/recits/%C2%AB-nous-ne-savons-pas-combien-de-temps-nous-pourrons-encore-tenir-dans-ces-conditions-%C2%BB-howa

3. **Regional existing response, 22 September 2026 (WHO Africa).** WHO and KSrelief signed a **US$5,327,244.97** implementation agreement for cholera preparedness and response covering Cameroon, Chad, Mali, Niger and Nigeria, including WASH, surveillance and clinical support. Signing is **not proof of disbursement, site coverage, implementation, or that the two above local gaps are filled**. Source: https://www.afro.who.int/news/ksrelief-and-who-sign-us-53-million-agreement-combat-cholera-lake-chad-basin

4. **Additional contextual cross-check, 7 October 2026 (WHO Africa).** Borno State's response includes treatment units and surveillance in an existing government/NCDC/WHO/partner structure; do not invent a missing emergency-response provider or extrapolate Borno statistics to Chad/Cameroon. Source: https://www.afro.who.int/countries/nigeria/news/bringing-lifesaving-cholera-care-closer-communities-borno-state

## Distinct signal and duplication test

**Signal:** A *quantified, partner-identified* latrine access shortfall persists in an already served cross-border area (571 households, September), while a rapidly growing displacement site in Chad reports that facilities are insufficient (October). These are credible local service-delivery needs. The signal is narrower than broad regional forecasts or general cholera appeals, and **not evidence of a new software, forecasting, or financing gap**.

**Existing solutions/owners:** UNICEF Cameroon with AAEDC; UNICEF Chad with CIAUD and Chad's Ministry of Water and Energy; WHO/KSrelief regional cholera programme. Local partners already act. A new Humanity Loop aid service, app, fundraising drive, or unsupervised outreach would risk duplication and bypass accountable actors. The WHO agreement's geographic scope overlaps, but site-level coverage is unverified.

## Falsification / missing evidence

- Are the **571 households** still without latrines after September? Obtain a dated partner/authority update before treating it as current.
- What proportion of Fourkoulom's **12,000** residents have adequate usable facilities today? Existing counts do not answer this.
- Is a local partner actively seeking a specific **no-cost, consented** research, translation, accessibility or data-quality contribution? No such request verified.
- Are safe water, latrine maintenance, gender/disability access, site security and ongoing consumables covered by other actors? Not resolved by these public stories.
- No causal health outcome, prevented case, climate-attributable effect, funding shortfall at these exact sites, or beneficiary impact can be inferred from programme narratives.

## Decision / next gate

**Retain as a bounded, verified *needs signal*, not an implementation-ready project.** No external contact or publicity. At a proportionate future Scout review, check a newer public partner situation report for whether the same specific gaps persist and whether an accountable local partner explicitly invites no-cost assistance. If a concrete invitation and safe deliverable are verified, propose a narrowly scoped partner-led contribution with prior deduplication and governance review. Otherwise close as already owned by existing response systems. No independent node install, verified correction, donation, deployment, or real-world beneficiary effect claimed.
