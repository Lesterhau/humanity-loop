## Exact discrepancy and significance

| Topic | Wake Forest CTSI May 12, 2026 guidance | Controlling/current NIH source | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manuscript acceptance cutoff | “on or after December 31, 2025” | **July 1, 2025** per NOT-OD-25-101 and March 2026 NIH Grants Policy Statement | **Confirmed published date mismatch**. The December date was in NIH's original 2024 notice but was moved forward in April 2025, before CTSI's May 2026 publication. |
| Required route | Says **all** author-accepted NIH-funded manuscripts “must now be deposited into” NIHMS | NIH recognizes either qualifying accepted-manuscript deposit to PMC (including NIHMS) **or** a journal/publisher's final-article deposit to PMC under an active formal NLM agreement, with **zero release delay** | **Overbroad route wording**. NIHMS is not required when a compliant direct-publisher deposit route applies. Verify the specific journal's active agreement and release delay; PMC indexing alone is not proof. |
| Active grant restriction | Describes manuscripts “supported by grants active on or after July 1, 2025” | NIH policy encompasses relevant NIH-funded works and does not generally end when an award closes; the NIH notice covers multiple funding mechanisms beyond grants | **Potentially misleading narrowing**, requires institutional grants-policy review before categorical correction. |

The current NIH policy applies to NIH-supported author-accepted journal manuscripts accepted **on or after July 1, 2025**, with deposit upon acceptance and **public availability without embargo upon the official date of publication**. The earlier December 31, 2025 date appears in the original NOT-OD-25-047, which explicitly links to the later amendment; quoting the original without its revision can create a stale date.

**Concrete illustrative case (not an observed author):** An NIH-supported manuscript accepted September 15, 2025 falls after the actual July 1 effective date but before the institution's December 31 cutoff. A researcher relying only on the institutional date could mistakenly believe the old embargo policy applies. Whether any particular manuscript is covered depends on the actual NIH funding and journal facts; this review inspected no individual manuscript.

**No paid open-access purchase is inherently required:** NIH's policy permits author-accepted manuscript deposit into PMC without a submission fee; eligible publisher deposit is another path. The institutional page's surrounding comments about journals requiring open-access publication should not be read as a general requirement to buy open-access publication.

## Unsent, human-review-only editorial suggestion

> The NIH 2024 Public Access Policy took effect **July 1, 2025**, under NIH Notice NOT-OD-25-101, and applies to qualifying NIH-supported journal manuscripts accepted for publication on or after that date. The author-accepted manuscript must be submitted for availability in PubMed Central without embargo upon the official publication date, unless a compliant final published article is deposited directly into PubMed Central by a journal/publisher under an applicable active NLM agreement with immediate release. Check the journal's current PMC deposit agreement and release delay; use NIHMS when appropriate. Consult NIH's current guidance for award-scope and compliance details.
