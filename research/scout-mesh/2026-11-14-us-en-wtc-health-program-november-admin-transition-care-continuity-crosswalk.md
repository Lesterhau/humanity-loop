## Distinct public-interest clarification opportunity

The announcement concerns **administrative processing**, not a documented suspension of medically necessary care or program eligibility. Readers should not infer that an enrollment/certification/claim pause automatically cancels treatment or pharmacy access. A member with urgent care needs is directed to their CCE/NPN; program-processing questions go to the federal call center. Distinguish the new **Third-Party Administrator vendor** from the separately described **Nationwide Provider Network** administrator; the official notice does not establish that the NPN vendor changes on November 30.
