**Critical distinctions:**
- These are **reported confirmed cases**, not a current Nov 14 live count, not all infections, and not a population infection-fatality rate. Do not infer a 26.9% population fatality rate from 18/67: ascertainment and reporting differ.
- The **WHO African Region** includes Cameroon and Central African Republic, so do not relabel all nine as geographically West African; WHO says the recent activity is *concentrated* in parts of West Africa.
- The 2025 French WHO fact sheet gives **global annual modeled burden estimates** (67,000–173,000 severe infections; 31,000–82,000 deaths). These figures are neither an alternative 2026 case count nor directly comparable to 67 laboratory-confirmed cases in the African Region.
- WHO assessed **moderate regional risk on 27 Oct** and **low global risk**, not zero local risk. Recent activity is concentrated in Burkina Faso, Côte d’Ivoire, Ghana and Liberia, including border areas; the WHO bulletin does **not** establish that every border district has active transmission.
- WHO says one WHO-approved yellow-fever vaccine dose generally confers lifelong protection and can satisfy international certificate validity; entry requirements vary by country. Contraindications/precautions need professional assessment. WHO does **not** recommend travel/trade restrictions in response to the described event.
