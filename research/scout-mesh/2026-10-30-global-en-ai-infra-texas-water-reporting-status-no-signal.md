## Question
Does Texas's October 2026 data-center water reporting and community-impact process reveal a newly verifiable, neglected public-interest defect that Humanity Loop can safely correct, rather than duplicate existing agency, newsroom or advocacy work?

## Authoritative evidence checked on October 30
1. [Texas Governor, September 14 directive](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-twdb-to-penalize-data-center-reporting-failures): directs the Texas Water Development Board (TWDB) to pursue water-use survey compliance and update the Governor by **October 14, 2026**. The directive and a scheduled update are **not** proof that a particular operator violated the law, that enforcement occurred, or that the update must be published online.
2. [TWDB 2026 digital-infrastructure Water Use Survey guidance](https://www.twdb.texas.gov/waterplanning/waterusesurvey/digitalinfrastructure.asp): currently offers a PDF submission path and contact details. It explicitly says the survey is for facilities **operating in 2025**; an initial nonresponse can reflect an uncertain operating status, duplicate facility entry or outdated contact. This limits claims based on nonresponse counts. The guidance points readers to a survey-submittal status report but does not itself establish any enforcement outcome.
3. [ERCOT Data Center State and Community Impact RFI](https://www.ercot.com/about/legal/data-center-impact-rfi): requests water, power, tax benefits, local effects and ownership information from covered developers pursuing interconnection at **25 MW or more**. The published response deadline was **October 12, 2026**, and ERCOT says it intends to publish a joint analysis with PUCT **on or before December 10, 2026**. The RFI is a separate collection process, not proof of current facility-wide water use or completed enforcement.
4. [TWDB October 14 work-session agenda](https://www.twdb.texas.gov/board/2026/10/WorkSession/index.asp): a publicly posted agenda, but it does not independently verify the content, submission or publication of the Governor-directed enforcement progress update.
5. [Texas Tribune, October 14 reporting](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/14/texas-data-centers-water-use-penalties-enforcement/), used as **secondary context only**, describes low survey response and statutory-enforcement limitations, while recording operator concerns about inaccurate survey contact data. This is already an actively covered topic, not an unreported gap uniquely found by Humanity Loop.
