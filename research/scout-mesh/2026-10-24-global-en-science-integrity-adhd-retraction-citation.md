The notice exists and is dated October 23, but its explanatory body was subscription-gated in the accessible view; **do not assert a specific retraction reason** from this evidence.
2. [Sun, Li & Okamura, *Child & Youth Care Forum*, 5 October 2026, DOI 10.1007/s10566-026-09981-w](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10566-026-09981-w) cites the now-retracted Ponomarev et al. 2023 study in its bibliography. In its **Interventions in the Medical Context** section, it cites Ponomarev together with Wong (2023) for a broad statement about mindfulness-based intervention improvements and long-term effects comparable to CBT. Its **Discussion / Medical Context** again includes Ponomarev in a statement about possible benefits, explicitly caveated as limited and heterogeneous evidence.
3. The citing review reports **42 included empirical studies** and uses a **narrative synthesis**, not a pooled meta-analysis. The public HTML shows Ponomarev cited in substantive discussion, but the available check did **not independently establish** whether that study was one of the 42 extracted studies, how it was weighted in Table 5, or whether the review's overall conclusions would change without it. The cited 2023 trial's abstract reports 90 children; this is **not** proof of inclusion in the 42-study synthesis.
4. The citing review's accessible HTML contained **no matching retraction notice text** during this scan. That is a time-specific observation, not proof that no Crossmark/supplemental/editorial metadata exists elsewhere.

## Duplication, bounded action and safeguards

- **Existing solution:** The original publisher has visibly marked the 2023 paper retracted and linked its notice. Humanity Loop should not duplicate that publisher action, accuse authors of misconduct, or broadcast claims that all ADHD digital therapies are ineffective.
- **Narrow potential contribution:** A reviewer or journal editor could check whether the citing review needs a reader-facing linked retraction notice, a qualified citation update, or a reconsideration of the specific long-term-comparability statement. This is a **candidate**, not a verified needed correction.
- **Next evidence gate:** Inspect the citing review's supplementary included-study table and publisher update history, ideally by **2026-11-06**. If the exact dependency is material and remains unflagged, prepare **one** non-accusatory, factual editorial inquiry for separate governance/outreach review; do not send automatically. Stop if the journal already flags the source or the claim does not materially depend on it.
- **Falsifiers:** A verified existing linked notice; evidence that Ponomarev is merely background context and other independent evidence fully supports the specific statement; an editorial update already addressing the issue.
- **10th Man:** Overstating this as an invalid systematic review could mislead families and unfairly damage authors. Retraction of **one cited trial** does not retract 42 studies or establish a changed treatment recommendation.
