# Colombia: Ministry measles "dosis cero" age-range conflict (public editorial review)

**Review date:** 2026-11-09 UTC  
**Lane:** `latam-es-public-health` / Tier-0 public-source access and clarity  
**Status:** directly reproduced official-page discrepancy; **unreported to institution**, **not** a verified clinical-policy change or adverse event. Human editorial/clinical review required before any external submission.

## Exact primary-source finding

The Colombian Ministry of Health and Social Protection's **September 21, 2026, bulletin 142-2026** says its measles vaccination actions prioritize **"niños y niñas de 1 a 11 meses (dosis cero)"**. This includes infants younger than six months if read literally.

- **Conflicting page:** https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Comunicaciones/noticias/2026/Paginas/Colombia-mantiene-la-eliminacion-del-sarampion-y-la-rubeola-pese-al-aumento-de-casos-en-las-Americas-y-refuerz-acciones.aspx — published 2026-09-21; directly retrieved public text; the exact phrase appears in the paragraph beginning `Las acciones priorizan`.
- **Current ministry's general measles information page:** https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/publica/Paginas/sarampion.aspx — directly retrieved public text; says **"Niños y niñas entre 6 y 11 meses"** for dose zero and explicitly says this dose does **not** replace regular 12- and 18-month doses. This page also links a technical guideline labeled `Lineamiento Vcaunación Dosis 0 Sarampión 6 a 11 meses`; the linked ZIP was **not** inspected.
- **Ministry bulletin 138-2026 (2026-09-15):** https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Comunicaciones/noticias/2026/Paginas/Confirmacion-de-caso-importado-de-sarampion-en-zona-de-frontera-refuerza-el-llamado-a-la-vacunacion-y-a-las-medidas-de-prev.aspx — says dose zero is for **6 to 11 months**.
- **Ministry bulletin 124-2026 (2026-08-22):** https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Comunicaciones/noticias/2026/Paginas/Colombia-fortalece-las-acciones-de-vacunacion-en-el-contexto-de-emergencia-nacional-por-el-terremoto-y-en-el-marco-del-Plan.aspx — says **6 to 11 months**.
- **Ministry bulletin 098-2026 (2026-06-19):** https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Comunicaciones/noticias/2026/Paginas/dosis-cero.aspx — says **6 to 11 months** and explicitly preserves the routine 12- and 18-month schedule.
- **Circular Conjunta 004/2026:** https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Normatividad_Nuevo/Circular%20Conjunta%20No%20004%20de%202026.pdf — official February circular supports travel-related measles vaccination starting **at six months**; it is earlier, partly travel-specific guidance and does **not** alone prove the current full national operational protocol.

**Reproducibility:** Open the September 21 ministry bulletin, find `1 a 11 meses`; compare against `6 y 11 meses` on the ministry's current general measles page and `6 a 11 meses` in the September 15 bulletin. The source pages were independently retrieved in a public text reader on November 9; an ordinary GUI browser, the downloadable ZIP guideline's actual content, and live point-of-care instructions were **not** tested.

## Interpretation and public-interest significance

This is a **narrow, independently reproducible public communications inconsistency** in a medically consequential age boundary. The weight of official cross-references supports an **editorial typo candidate** in the September 21 news bulletin, rather than a documented policy expansion to one-month-old infants. The public reader does not establish when the conflicting page was last edited, how many people viewed it, whether clinicians followed it, or whether a different later technical instruction superseded the cited pages. **Do not use the conflicting bulletin as a vaccination instruction for a specific child.** The appropriate practical route is to consult a qualified local vaccination provider and current official Ministry/PAI guidance.

## Human-review-only proposed editorial correction (Spanish)

> En el boletín 142-2026 del 21 de septiembre, verificar la expresión “niños y niñas de 1 a 11 meses (dosis cero)”. La página oficial de sarampión y los boletines 098, 124 y 138 de 2026 indican “6 a 11 meses” para la dosis cero. Si la indicación técnica vigente sigue siendo de 6 a 11 meses, se solicita corregir ese rango en el boletín y conservar la aclaración de que la dosis cero no sustituye las dosis habituales a los 12 y 18 meses.
