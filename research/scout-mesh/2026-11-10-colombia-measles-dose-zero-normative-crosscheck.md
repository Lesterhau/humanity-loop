# Colombia measles “dosis cero”: normative and technical-index cross-check (follow-up)

**Checked:** 2026-11-10 00:42 UTC (Nov 9, Colombia evening). **Lane:** `latam-es-public-health`. **Type:** source-backed verification of a previously recorded editorial discrepancy, not a new independent case. **Status:** evidence strengthened; external correction and clinical policy change NOT established.

## What was independently verified

1. **Conflicting public copy remains accessible:** The Ministry of Health's September 21, 2026 bulletin 142-2026 still states that priority actions include infants **“1 a 11 meses (dosis cero)”**. Exact source: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Comunicaciones/noticias/2026/Paginas/Colombia-mantiene-la-eliminacion-del-sarampion-y-la-rubeola-pese-al-aumento-de-casos-en-las-Americas-y-refuerz-acciones.aspx
2. **Formal circular is narrower and explicit:** Circular Conjunta 000004 of February 25, 2026, §3.2 (PDF page 11) calls for monitoring dose-zero vaccination at **6–11 months**. Section 3.4 (PDF page 12) instructs use of SR dose zero for children **6 months through 11 months and 29 days** traveling with parents/guardians to outbreak countries or World Cup hosts, and preserves regular SRP doses at 12 and 18 months. Exact primary document: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Normatividad_Nuevo/Circular%20Conjunta%20No%20004%20de%202026.pdf
3. **Ministry's technical guidance index is specifically labeled 6–11 months:** Its current PAI technical-guidelines page has a link titled *“Lineamientos técnicos y operativos para la vacunación contra el sarampión y la rubéola en población de 6 a 11 meses”*. This points to a ZIP file, **not** a directly inspected technical text. Index: https://vacunacion.minsalud.gov.co/RT/Paginas/lineamientos-tecnicos.aspx ; linked file: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/sites/rid/Lists/BibliotecaDigital/RIDE/VS/PP/PAI/lin-tec-op-vac-sar-rub-6-11-meses.zip
4. **Current public health pages independently reinforce 6–11 months:** Ministry general measles guidance https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/publica/Paginas/sarampion.aspx and Ministry childhood schedule https://vacunacion.minsalud.gov.co/EV/Paginas/ninos-y-ninas-menores-de-6-anos.aspx both describe dose zero for **6–11 months**. The June 19 and September 15 bulletins likewise use that age range.

## Interpretation and falsification

The **September 21 news paragraph conflicts with a formal circular, a Ministry technical-guideline index, and current Ministry public-facing guidance**. A one-digit editorial typo is the strongest supported hypothesis; it is not proved. The February circular is travel-specific and predates the broader June dose-zero announcement, so its scope cannot be generalized to every infant. The technical ZIP contents, any later superseding directive, ordinary browser rendering and point-of-care practice were **not verified**. The linked ZIP was not readable in the web reader (unsupported ZIP content type); a direct source download from the worker's container failed DNS. No alternate access-control or safety workaround was attempted.

**Falsification gate:** A dated Ministry/PAI technical document explicitly authorizing a 1-month start for the same 2026 dose-zero program would invalidate the typo hypothesis. A corrected September 21 webpage would close the *public-copy* discrepancy without proving that this report caused the change.
