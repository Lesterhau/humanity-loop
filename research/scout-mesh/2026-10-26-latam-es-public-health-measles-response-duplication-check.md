## Source-reviewed evidence (Spanish-language official sources)

1. **OPS/PAHO situation report #14, October 22, 2026:** Through epidemiological week 41 (ending October 17), the Americas reported **56,753 confirmed measles cases in 17 countries/territories and 81 deaths** in 2026. Guatemala (34,535), Mexico (13,691), United States (4,410), Peru (2,698), and Canada (1,124) account for most confirmed cases. PAHO cautions that low-level transmission may persist despite some local declines. These are reported surveillance figures, subject to updates and retrospective adjustments; do not interpret changes between cumulative reports as exact new infections.  
   https://www.paho.org/es/documentos/informe-situacion-14-sarampion-region-americas-22-octubre-2026
2. **OPS regional risk assessment/news, October 9, 2026:** The risk of measles spread remains classified **very high**, with vaccination gaps and population movement around large gatherings as factors. OPS calls for vaccination, surveillance, and rapid outbreak response; it is already coordinating with countries.  
   https://www.paho.org/es/noticias/9-10-2026-ops-evalua-como-muy-alto-riesgo-propagacion-sarampion-ante-proximos-eventos
3. **OPS, Alta Verapaz, Guatemala, October 21, 2026:** The Ministry of Public Health and Social Assistance (MSPAS), PAHO and the Guatemala Humanitarian Fund already support territorial microplanning to identify vaccination populations, resources, routes and operational gaps. This is evidence of an existing on-the-ground response, not proof that every local need is met.  
   https://www.paho.org/es/noticias/21-10-2026-alta-verapaz-fortalece-planificacion-territorial-vacunacion-ante-brote
4. **OPS response strategy and donor appeal, July 3, 2026:** A public **USD 10.7 million** measles emergency-response appeal describes surveillance, vaccination and response needs. It is a legitimate *existing support channel*, not evidence of the amount currently unfunded or of a novel gap requiring another platform.  
   https://www.paho.org/es/documentos/estrategia-respuesta-llamamiento-donantes-brotes-sarampion-region-americas
5. **OPS Guatemala campaign report, June 25, 2026:** MSPAS/PAHO already describe mobile, community and institutional vaccination delivery in municipalities in Guatemala department. These actions are not necessarily sufficient, but they establish a pre-existing intervention to avoid duplicating.  
   https://www.paho.org/es/noticias/25-6-2026-guatemala-extiende-campana-contencion-vacunacion-contra-sarampion-al-nororiente
