## What was independently checked

1. **WHO Chad country office, Integrated Weekly Public Health Emergencies Bulletin, epidemiological week 40 (28 September–4 October 2026), file labeled 19 October 2026.** WHO regional directory: https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/chad/publication/bulletin-hebdomadaire-integre-des-urgences-de-sante-publique-au-tchad-2026 ; original five-page PDF: https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-10/Bulletin%20hebdo%20int%C3%A9gr%C3%A9%20Urgences%20de%20sant%C3%A9%20publique_S40_19102026.pdf . **Page 1** explicitly says the cholera epidemiological data are updated **through 1 October 2026**, citing the Ministry of Health's SITREP 0044. **Pages 2–3** state **1,329 cumulative reported cholera cases, 58 reported deaths, 4.3% reported CFR**, across **14 health districts, four provinces, 26 responsibility zones, 170 villages/neighborhoods**. They also state **139 new cases and 7 deaths during 21 September–1 October**. WHO names **Lac** as the principal focus, with Hadjer-Lamis, N'Djamena and Chari-Baguirmi also affected. The PDF text and pages 1–2 images were checked directly.
2. **WHO global Weekly Epidemiological Record 101(44), published 6 November 2026, section “Multi-country outbreak of cholera,” Table 1**, https://www.who.int/publications/journals/weekly-epidemiological-record/wer101-44/ . Although *published later*, its **data cutoff is 27 September 2026** and it lists Chad **867 cases / 42 deaths (4.8% reported CFR)** cumulatively and **168 cases / 22 deaths (13.1% reported CFR)** in its last-28-day column. It explicitly warns about different definitions, backfill, delayed reports and surveillance coverage.
3. **Previous Humanity Loop source review:** https://github.com/Lesterhau/humanity-loop/blob/main/research/scout-mesh/2026-11-11-lake-chad-cholera-who-wer44-chad-28day-fatality-context.md . ## Reconciliation / risk to public communication

| Official WHO product | Publication/file date | Data cutoff | Chad cumulative reported cases / deaths | Reported CFR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WHO global WER 101(44) Table 1 | 6 Nov 2026 | 27 Sep 2026 | 867 / 42 | 4.8% |
| WHO Chad week-40 country bulletin, citing Ministry SITREP 0044 | PDF labeled 19 Oct 2026 | 1 Oct 2026 | **1,329 / 58** | **4.3%** |

**Important:** These are *different surveillance snapshots*, not two contradictory claims for the same cutoff. The **462-case / 16-death arithmetic difference** must **NOT** be presented as confirmed incidence occurring exclusively during 28 September–1 October. The country bulletin itself attributes **139 cases / 7 deaths** to its 21 September–1 October interval. The remainder may involve different inclusion criteria, delayed data, retroactive revision, or reporting scope; the actual reconciliation mechanism was **not independently established**. Likewise the global last-28-day **22/168** is **not** comparable to the country bulletin's **7/139** (different intervals and potentially different case definitions). No inference about changing clinical care quality or a local facility's fatality rate is justified.

**Material improvement:** The earlier note's `867/42` figures are an accurately labeled older WHO global table, **not the most recent official Chad-country surveillance snapshot found in this check**. Any future public brief must lead with cutoff date and source, and acknowledge the **1 October country snapshot**. A publication date of 6 November does **not** make the underlying global September figures newer than a 19 October country PDF.

**Local response relevance:** The country bulletin itself reports **75,210 displaced people in 16 sites** in Lac as of 30 September, with unmet needs across health, nutrition, water, hygiene, sanitation, protection and shelter; it documents existing coordination and outreach. These are **dated regional needs**, not proof that a specific site lacks a latrine today or that Humanity Loop has a local delivery partner. The bulletin mentions a high share of community deaths but gives no denominator in the extracted passage. Avoid inventing one.

## Public-safe French editorial correction — UNSENT

> Deux publications officielles de l'OMS utilisent des dates de surveillance différentes. Le bulletin mondial publié le 6 novembre présente pour le Tchad 867 cas et 42 décès jusqu'au 27 septembre 2026. Un bulletin du bureau de l'OMS au Tchad, daté du 19 octobre et reprenant le SITREP 0044, fait état de 1 329 cas et 58 décès au 1er octobre. On ne peut pas attribuer automatiquement la différence à quatre jours de nouvelles infections : les définitions, la remontée des données et les révisions peuvent varier. Aucun de ces chiffres ne décrit à lui seul la situation en temps réel, ni l'accès aux soins dans un site particulier.
