## Primary-source review

1. **FERC Office of Public Participation, page updated 2026-10-19:** The June 18 six-region show-cause orders identified **preliminary** shortcomings in public disclosure of which grid upgrades serve large loads, their costs, and potential stranded-cost allocation. FERC's OPP explicitly cautions its explainer is educational, not a binding Commission order. Five RTO/ISO responses are due **2026-11-16**; Southwest Power Pool's response is due **2026-11-20**. OPP already lists six docket IDs (EL26-67-000 through EL26-72-000), describes the federal/state rate-allocation split, and explains that comments can be submitted now or within 30 days of the filings. Source: https://www.ferc.gov/cost-transparency-customer-protection .
2. **FERC Commissioner Rosner concurrence, 2026-10-09, Duke Energy Carolinas dockets ER26-2691-000 and -001:** The commissioner says Duke's prior tariff did not protect smaller consumers from transmission costs of new large loads, and regards the accepted proposal as a meaningful but imperfect improvement. This is an individual Commissioner's assessment of a particular proceeding, **not** proof that all tariffs have the same defect or that households have incurred a measured cost. Source: https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/commissioner-rosners-concurrence-duke-energy-carolinas-llc-er26-2691-000-er26-2691 .
3. **International Energy Agency, 2026-10-14:** `Powering Artificial Intelligence` reviews policy approaches in 34 jurisdictions representing approximately 90% of global data-center capacity, including affordability and energy-security questions. Existing cross-jurisdiction analysis weakens the case for an unsourced generic new policy report. Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/powering-artificial-intelligence .
4. **U.S. Energy Information Administration, 2026-03-25:** EIA announced three **voluntary pilot** studies covering data-center electricity, energy sources, cooling and server metrics in Texas, Washington and Northern Virginia/DC, with 196 identified operating companies. Pilot announcement does not establish a completed nationwide survey or verified public dataset. Source: https://www.eia.gov/pressroom/releases/press585.php .
