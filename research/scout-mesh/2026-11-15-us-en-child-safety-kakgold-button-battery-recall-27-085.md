# Kakgold 4WD stunt vehicle recall 27-085 — source-checked child safety brief (UNSENT)
Date checked: 2026-11-15 UTC. Status: official-source research and human-review candidate; NOT distributed, NOT an original defect discovery.

## Primary source
US Consumer Product Safety Commission, recall 27-085, dated November 12, 2026: https://www.cpsc.gov/Safety-Education/Button-Batteries (Kakgold recall listing; official recall detail linked there).

## Verified facts
- Approximately 294 Kakgold 4WD stunt vehicle toys are recalled. The remote control contains accessible button-cell and lithium-ion batteries, violating the mandatory toy standard and posing a serious battery-ingestion hazard to children.
- Identifiers: lime-green and blue vehicle body, black chassis, four black wheels with lime-green trim and diagonal rollers, 'PRO' and 'RACING' markings, white 'Do Not Twist the Shaft' hang tag. Sold on Amazon.com July–September 2026 for approximately $34–$50.
- CPSC reports no incidents or injuries. That does not mean the hazard is harmless.
- Official instruction: stop using immediately and contact Kakgold (kakrecall@outlook.com) for a full refund. The recall describes writing 'RECALLED' on the product and emailing a disposal photograph. Crucially, CPSC separately instructs that button-cell batteries must be disposed of/recycled under local hazardous-waste procedures. Do not imply batteries should go into household trash.
- Recall is specific to this product, not all remote-control cars.

## Unsent consumer-facing clarification for qualified review
'If you have the recalled Kakgold 4WD stunt vehicle, stop using it and keep the toy and remote away from children. Follow CPSC recall 27-085 to request a refund.
