# Medicare 2027 plan-review action card | Tarjeta de revisión de planes de Medicare 2027

**Source-reviewed:** 2026-11-11 UTC. **Scope:** United States, English and Spanish; Medicare annual Open Enrollment and Part D prescription coverage for calendar 2027. **Status:** independent, public-source educational draft; not a government notice, individualized plan recommendation, application, or accepted external contribution. No outreach or enrollment was performed.

## English — action before December 7, 2026

**If you already have Medicare:** The annual Open Enrollment period is **October 15–December 7, 2026**. A plan must receive an enrollment request **by December 7** for a change made in this period to take effect **January 1, 2027**. This is for changing Medicare Advantage and/or Medicare drug coverage, not a substitute for the separate first-time Part A/B enrollment rules. [Medicare official Open Enrollment](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment); [CMS September 28, 2026 announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/medicare-advantage-medicare-prescription-drug-programs-expected-remain-stable-2027).

**Two annual Part D numbers changed:**

| Defined-standard Part D parameter | Calendar 2026 | Calendar 2027 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Maximum annual plan deductible | $615 | **$700** | Individual plans can set a lower deductible, including $0; this is not what everyone pays. |
| Annual covered Part D out-of-pocket threshold | $2,100 | **$2,400** | After the applicable covered-drug spending threshold (including certain qualifying payments on your behalf), catastrophic-stage cost-sharing for covered Part D drugs is $0 for the remainder of that calendar year. |

Primary sources: [CMS April 6, 2026 CY2027 Rate Announcement, Attachment V, Table V-6](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/2027-announcement.pdf); [Medicare.gov Part D cost guidance](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/part-d/basics/costs). These are **different calendar years**, not competing values for the same year.

**What the $2,400 threshold does NOT promise:** It is not a cap on premiums, uncovered prescriptions, all medical costs, or a guarantee that a given drug is on a plan's formulary. It is not a reason to ignore plan networks, drug tiers, prior authorizations or pharmacy costs. The separate [Medicare Prescription Payment Plan](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/part-d/basics/costs) spreads qualifying costs over the year; Medicare says it does **not** reduce total drug costs.

**Three steps to take:**
1. Check your plan's **Annual Notice of Change** and your actual 2027 prescriptions, pharmacy, providers, monthly premiums, deductible and expected annual cost. Compare at [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment), not only by the national maximum.
2. If you want a change, submit it through the official plan/Medicare enrollment route in time for the plan to **receive it by December 7, 2026**. Retain the confirmation. Do not send personal health/insurance details to Humanity Loop.
3. For **free, personalized, non-sales counseling**, Medicare points to your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) via [Medicare's contact page](https://www.medicare.gov/talk-to-someone), or call **1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)**. Ask about **Extra Help** and Medicare Savings Programs if costs are difficult. Do not assume everyone qualifies.

## Español — actúe antes del 7 de diciembre de 2026

**Si ya tiene Medicare:** La Inscripción Abierta anual es del **15 de octubre al 7 de diciembre de 2026**. El plan debe **recibir** la solicitud de cambio a más tardar el **7 de diciembre**; los cambios de este período entran en vigor el **1 de enero de 2027**. Esto se refiere a los cambios de Medicare Advantage y/o cobertura de medicamentos, no a todas las reglas de inscripción inicial en las Partes A y B. [Fuente oficial en español](https://es.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment).

**Dos cifras de la Parte D cambian para 2027:**

| Concepto | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deducible anual máximo de un plan de medicamentos | $615 | **$700** |
| Umbral anual de gastos de bolsillo que cuentan para medicamentos cubiertos de la Parte D | $2,100 | **$2,400** |

El deducible de su plan puede ser menor o incluso $0. Cuando se alcanza el umbral aplicable de gastos por medicamentos cubiertos (incluidos ciertos pagos realizados en su nombre), no hay costos de bolsillo por esos medicamentos cubiertos durante el resto del año calendario. **El límite no cubre todas las primas, medicamentos no cubiertos ni todos los gastos médicos.** Consulte [los costos oficiales de la Parte D en español](https://es.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/part-d/basics/costs).

**Antes de elegir:** Compare los medicamentos específicos, farmacias, médicos, primas, deducible y costos totales previstos de cada plan para 2027. Revise el aviso anual de cambios de su plan. Si desea cambiar, complete el trámite oficial antes del plazo y guarde su confirmación. Para asesoramiento gratuito de SHIP y el teléfono de Medicare, use [Comuníquese con Medicare](https://es.medicare.gov/talk-to-someone) o llame al **1-800-633-4227**. Pregunte si reúne los requisitos de Ayuda Adicional u otros programas de ahorro. No envíe información personal a Humanity Loop.
