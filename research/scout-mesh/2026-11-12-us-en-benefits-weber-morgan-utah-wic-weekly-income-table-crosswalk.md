The local eight-person weekly amount equals the state **nine-person** weekly amount; the local nine-person amount equals the state **ten-person** amount. The local ten-person $2,318 figure does not match the state ten-person threshold. This suggests table transcription/alignment errors, **not** a demonstrated erroneous eligibility system. The 2025–26 effective-date line is inconsistent with the 2026–27 heading and with the row amounts; it needs correction or explanation.

### Applicant-facing significance (illustrations, not actual determinations)

- A **10-person household with $2,350 gross weekly income** could read the local $2,318 figure and mistakenly self-screen out, even though $2,350 is below the current state/federal $2,387 weekly maximum. Other WIC eligibility requirements and program rules still apply.
- An **8-person household with $2,100 gross weekly income** could read the local $2,185 and wrongly expect to pass the weekly income screen, whereas the state/federal weekly maximum is $1,983. Other income periods or adjunctive eligibility may affect a real assessment.

**Do not tell applicants they are definitively eligible/ineligible from this comparison.** WIC staff determine eligibility, and Medicaid/SNAP/TANF participation can confer income eligibility. Applicants in Weber or Morgan County can use the local department's published WIC contact **801-399-7200** and the official [Utah WIC application guidance](https://wic.utah.gov/about/apply/).
