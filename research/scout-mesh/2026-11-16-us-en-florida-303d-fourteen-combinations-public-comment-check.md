# Florida 2024 CWA 303(d) partial approval — public participation evidence check

Date checked: 2026-11-16 UTC. Status: UNSENT, Tier-0 primary-source review; no external submission or verified environmental outcome.

## Official source
EPA Region 4 public notice, published 2026-10-26: https://www.epa.gov/publicnotices/epa-decision-add-14-waterbody-pollutant-combinations-floridas-2024-clean-water-act

EPA states that it partially approved Florida's 2024 Clean Water Act section 303(d) list, agreeing with 3,202 listed waterbody–pollutant combinations and removal of 266 combinations, but determined that **14 additional waterbody–pollutant combinations** should be included. The public notice's introductory wording says “14 waterbodies,” while its title and substantive explanation say “14 waterbody-pollutant combinations”; these are not necessarily 14 distinct waterbodies. Avoid interpreting the number as 14 separately named waters without checking the linked decision document.

**Comment deadline: November 26, 2026.** The notice directs comments to **pohnan.joseph@epa.gov**. No exact cutoff time is specified in the page reviewed. EPA says it will consider comments and appropriate revisions before transmitting its final list to Florida. This is a proposed EPA list addition during public review, not proof that a final revised list has already taken effect.
