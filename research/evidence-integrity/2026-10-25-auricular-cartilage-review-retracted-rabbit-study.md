# Evidence-integrity review: auricular cartilage stem-cell systematic review cites subsequently retracted rabbit study

**Review date:** 2026-10-25 UTC  
**Case status:** Independently verified study-level inclusion and narrative reliance; editorial reassessment not established.  
**Risk tier:** Tier 0 public-source research. No journal or author contact authorized by this note.

## Exact records

- **Retracted primary study:** Hassan et al., *Auricular cartilage regeneration using different types of mesenchymal stem cells in rabbits*, *Biological Research* 55:40 (published 2022-12-26), DOI **10.1186/s40659-022-00408-z**. Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40659-022-00408-z
- **Retraction notice:** *Biological Research*, published **2026-10-20**, DOI **10.1186/s40659-026-00737-3**. Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40659-026-00737-3
- **Downstream review:** Liu et al., *The application and progress of stem cells in auricular cartilage regeneration: a systematic review*, *Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology* (published **2023-07-26**), DOI **10.3389/fcell.2023.1204050**, PMID **37564374**. Full text: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2023.1204050/full

## Independent source-level checks

1. The publisher's October 20, 2026 notice retracts the exact Hassan et al. DOI. The editors cite overlapping/duplicated figure regions, reuse of control images across conditions, and insufficient verifiable underlying data, and state they lost confidence in the article's data and conclusions. The authors dispute the retraction. The notice postdates the 2023 review; this is not evidence the reviewers knowingly used retracted work.
2. The Frontiers review reports **33 included research articles**. **Table 2** (14 bone-marrow mesenchymal stem-cell studies) expressly lists **Hassan et al. (2022)** as a rabbit in-vivo study and summarizes its claim that BMMSCs outperformed ADSCs and ear-derived cells. This is an **included-study dependency**, not merely a background bibliography citation.
3. The review also relies on the Hassan finding in the **BMMSC discussion** (the passage beginning “For auricular cartilage reparation by injecting BMMSCs…”) and in **Conclusion and prospects** (BMMSC superiority and wound-repair mechanisms), alongside other cited studies. The original DOI appears in its bibliography.
4. This is a **narrative systematic review**, not a demonstrated pooled effect-size meta-analysis. There is **no verified recalculation**, no proof the review's overall conclusion would change without Hassan et al., and no confirmed editor notification/correction. The review's own abstract says the field remains at an early animal-experiment stage.

## Implication and bounded next action

**Verified finding:** One of the 33 research articles incorporated into Liu et al. 2023 was subsequently retracted, and at least two substantive narrative passages use its findings. A neutral editorial evidence check may be warranted, but the independent effect of exclusion is unknown.

Check the publisher's article/updates for an already issued notice before considering a human-reviewed neutral inquiry. Do not allege misconduct by the review authors or suggest clinical treatment changes.
