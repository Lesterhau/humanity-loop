## Precise finding

Wiley published a formal retraction notice on **October 29, 2026** for El Sayed, Kandil and Ghoneum (2021), *Probiotics Fermentation Technology, a Novel Kefir Product, Ameliorates Cognitive Impairment in Streptozotocin-Induced Sporadic Alzheimer's Disease in Mice*, DOI **10.1155/2021/5525306**. The publisher's notice (DOI **10.1155/omcl/9795039**) describes unexpected similarities involving hippocampal-region images and Western blot bands; it states that the provided responses/raw data did not resolve integrity concerns and that the study's conclusions can no longer be considered reliable. The authors disagree with the retraction. This describes the publisher's finding, not an independent misconduct determination.

The 2025 systematic review by Tanure et al., *Potential benefits of kefir and its compounds on Alzheimer's disease: A systematic review*, DOI **10.1016/j.bbii.2025.100115**, explicitly includes **El Sayed et al. 2021a** among **seven selected studies** and cites its results in the narrative synthesis of oxidative stress, amyloid/tau-related markers and neuroinflammation. The reference list identifies El Sayed et al. 2021a as the exact DOI now retracted. Four of the seven studies were rodent studies. This is an **included primary-study dependency**, not a mere bibliography-only citation.

The 2025 review predates the October 2026 retraction. The review also includes a distinct **El Sayed et al. 2021b** paper (Frontiers in Pharmacology, DOI **10.3389/fphar.2021.666502**); **do not conflate the two**. No quantitative recalculation, materiality determination, correction, clinical benefit or harm has been verified.

## Why this merits editorial clarification, not a verdict

- A source that contributed to a seven-study synthesis has been formally retracted **after** publication of the synthesis.
- The retraction does not automatically invalidate the review's other six included studies, its human-study observations, or its overall conclusion.
- The review is descriptive, not a verified pooled estimate recalculated here. No numerical effect-size change can be claimed.
- The review's authors may not yet have had time to respond because the retraction notice is dated today.
- The appropriate next step is a proportionate, factual editorial clarification after checking for an existing update; do not send repeated or accusatory correspondence.
