## Precise source chain

1. **Citing review:** Liang X et al., *Non-pharmacological executive function interventions for children and adolescents with neurodevelopmental disorders: a systematic review and network meta-analysis*, *eClinicalMedicine* 100:104228. Published online September 28, 2026; DOI [10.1016/j.eclinm.2026.104228](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eclinm.2026.104228). Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13651499/ . The review reports 118 included studies (5,863 participants) and 141 intervention arms, with 83 studies involving ADHD populations.
2. **Exact inclusion:** The review's study-characteristics table, under **“Pair-wise category: Mindfulness training”**, lists **Ponomarev et al. This is stronger than a passing background citation: the study is listed in the review's included-study table. However, a contribution to any *specific pooled estimate or network ranking* has **not** been independently isolated or recalculated.
3. **Original article:** Ponomarev R et al., *Digital Cognitive Training for Children with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder*, *Journal of Psycholinguistic Research* 52:2303–2319 (2023), DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-023-10003-2 .
4. **Publisher retraction:** The original article's Springer page explicitly labels it **“RETRACTED ARTICLE”** and dates the retraction **October 23, 2026**: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10936-023-10003-2 . Retraction notice DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-026-10324-y (publisher notice dated October 23, 2026). The notice's explanation is behind a subscription preview in the accessible source; **do not assert a retraction reason** without direct notice text.

## Verified conclusion and limitations

The review was published **before** the retraction and visibly lists the now-retracted Ponomarev trial among its included mindfulness studies. This warrants a focused evidence-dependency and sensitivity check. It **does not establish** that the review's pooled effect sizes, comparative intervention rankings, conclusions, or clinical recommendations are materially wrong. The review already cautions that overall certainty is low and most domain-specific evidence is low or very low.

No author/editor acknowledgement, correction, reanalysis, clinical change, or beneficiary effect has been verified. Do not portray the publisher's retraction as caused by Humanity Loop.
