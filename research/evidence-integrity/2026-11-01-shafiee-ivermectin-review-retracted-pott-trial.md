# Evidence-integrity review: Shafiee et al. 2022 and retracted Pott-Junior trial

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-01 UTC. **Status:** verified, narrow study-level inclusion; editorial clarification candidate only. **Owner:** Humanity Loop Evidence Integrity / main coordinator. **No external contact.**

## Precise question

Does the *Virology Journal* systematic review and meta-analysis, Shafiee et al., *Ivermectin under scrutiny: a systematic review and meta-analysis of efficacy and possible sources of controversies in COVID-19 patients* (published 2022-06-13; DOI 10.1186/s12985-022-01829-8; PMID 35698151), merely cite Pott-Junior et al. 2021, or actually include the trial in its synthesis?

## Independent primary-source observations

1. **Formal retraction:** The National Library of Medicine identifies the original Pott-Junior et al. pilot trial (DOI 10.1016/j.toxrep.2021.03.003; PMID 33723507) as a retracted publication. Its linked *Toxicology Reports* retraction notice (DOI 10.1016/j.toxrep.2022.04.009; PMID 35528725) is dated **2022-05-02**, before the review's June 13 publication. The notice concerns unreliable findings; do not infer misconduct by the citing authors.
2. **Not just bibliography:** The review lists Pott-Junior as reference **33** in its original bibliography. Its **Results / Negative RT-PCR** subsection explicitly lists **[33]** among studies contributing to the pooled endpoint (log OR 0.25, 95% CI -0.18 to 0.68, reported as not significant). Its **Results / Time to negative RT-PCR** subsection explicitly lists **[33]** among **three** RCTs (138 patients total) used for that pooled endpoint (SMD -0.36, 95% CI -0.89 to 0.17, reported as not significant). This is an identified quantitative dependency, not merely a reference.
3. **Limited scope:** The review's **mortality** pool cites other trial references, **not [33]**. It reports high risk of bias for many trials and an overall conclusion that ivermectin does not have significant benefit for most outcomes. No recalculation excluding [33] was performed here, and no change in the review's conclusions, clinical practice, or patient outcomes has been established.
4. **Timing:** Review search cutoff was 2022-02-20 and its manuscript was received 2022-03-24, both before the May 2 retraction. Acceptance was 2022-06-02 and publication 2022-06-13, after the retraction. This chronology alone does not establish when authors or editors learned of the notice.
5. **Current annotation:** The publisher's accessible HTML reviewed on November 1 showed the original article and no verified correction specifically removing Pott-Junior [33]. This is not an exhaustive check of all publisher communications or supplements.

## Assessment, falsification, and next step

**Verified:** The retracted trial was included in at least two published pooled virological endpoints. **Unverified:** Whether the journal has a separate published reanalysis or supplementary exclusion, whether removal would materially change either estimate, whether the editors were aware before publication, and whether any real-world decision relied on this detail. Do not accuse authors of misconduct or describe the whole review as invalid.

**Deduplication:** Related but separate 2025 *Indian Journal of Community Medicine* review already tracked under issue #42; the 2022 *Virology Journal* review is a different paper. Other 2022 reviews explicitly excluded retracted trials; do not generalize across them.

**Next review: 2026-11-08**, or upon substantive new publisher evidence. Check publisher notices/supplementary analyses and the official editorial channel. If still unresolved, consider **one human-reviewed, narrowly factual editorial clarification** asking whether [33] was removed/reanalyzed in the negative-RT-PCR and time-to-negative-RT-PCR estimates, and whether a reader-facing annotation is warranted. Do not send automatically or count this investigation as a correction or beneficiary outcome.
