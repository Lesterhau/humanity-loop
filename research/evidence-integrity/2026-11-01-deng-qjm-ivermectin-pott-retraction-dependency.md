# Evidence-integrity triage — Deng et al. (2021) QJM ivermectin review and subsequently retracted Pott-Junior pilot

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-01 UTC  
**Status:** verified inclusion in study-characteristics table; exact contribution to pooled estimates **not established**  
**Risk/authority:** Tier-0 source review only. No editor contact, clinical advice, correction claim, or human-subject action.

## Narrow question

Does the QJM systematic review *Efficacy and safety of ivermectin for the treatment of COVID-19: a systematic review and meta-analysis* (Deng et al., 2021; DOI [10.1093/qjmed/hcab247](https://doi.org/10.1093/qjmed/hcab247)) incorporate a clinical trial subsequently retracted for unreliable findings?

## Primary-source observations

1. The [QJM review's full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8500108/) identifies **Pott-Junior et al. (2021)** in **Table 1**, which the authors describe as the characteristics of *included studies*, and lists it as reference **31**. The row describes an open-label Brazilian randomized pilot, NCT04431466, with ivermectin-dose arms and a standard-of-care arm. The review reports **14 randomized controlled trials and three observational studies** overall.
2. The [publisher-linked retraction notice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35528725/) (DOI [10.1016/j.toxrep.2022.04.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.toxrep.2022.04.009)) is dated **May 2, 2022** and explicitly retracts Pott-Junior's 2021 pilot (DOI [10.1016/j.toxrep.2021.03.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.toxrep.2021.03.003)). The notice states the findings were considered unreliable; it is not a finding about Deng et al.
3. Deng et al.'s review appeared **before** that retraction. A separate [QJM correction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35325252/) (DOI [10.1093/qjmed/hcac072](https://doi.org/10.1093/qjmed/hcac072), first online March 2022; journal issue October 2022) concerns a displayed advance-access publication date, **not** a documented reanalysis removing the Pott-Junior trial.
4. The review reports pooled outcomes for viral clearance, hospital duration, mortality, ventilation, and adverse events. **Table 1 alone cannot establish which outcome-specific forest plots include the Pott-Junior pilot or how much weight its data carry.** Supplementary forest plots and extraction sheets require separate verification before any quantitative or clinical assertion.

## Interpretation and uncertainty

**Verified:** A subsequently retracted primary trial is explicitly listed among the review's included-study characteristics. The retraction postdates the review, so there is no basis to suggest knowing inclusion of retracted work at publication. This is a **study-level evidence dependency candidate**, not proof that any particular pooled estimate changes, that the review's conclusions are invalid, or that patients were harmed.

**Not verified:** Whether the journal has published a post-May-2022 reader-facing annotation specifically addressing this trial; which pooled endpoints include it; any sensitivity reanalysis excluding it; or whether an editorial inquiry would add value beyond existing correction channels.
