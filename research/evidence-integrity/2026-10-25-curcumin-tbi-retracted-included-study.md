## What the 2024 review actually pooled

The published text explicitly lists reference **22** in eight pooled endpoints: **SOD (3 studies), GPx (3), Nrf2 (2), MDA (4), brain water content / edema (7), Bcl-2 (5), caspase-3 (7), and apoptosis index (5)**. These are *reported study counts*, not a recalculation. The review reports leave-one-out sensitivity analyses for these outcomes, often stating its reported conclusions were not materially changed by excluding one study; those statements are **not an independent post-retraction reanalysis**.

Source locations: Guo et al. §3.2 Table 2 and §§3.3.2.1, 3.3.2.3–5, 3.3.3.1, 3.3.4.1, 3.3.4.3–4, plus reference 22.
