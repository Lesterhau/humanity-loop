# Evidence Integrity triage — retracted auricular-cartilage study cited substantively
Date: 2026-10-22. Status: source-verified candidate, not clinical conclusion.
Source queue: runtime/evidence-integrity-backup/review-queue.json, PMID 37564374.

The 2023 Frontiers review "The application and progress of stem cells in auricular cartilage regeneration: a systematic review" (DOI 10.3389/fcell.2023.1204050) **includes Hassan et al. (2022)** as a studied experiment in Table 2 and directly repeats its rabbit-study BMMSC-vs-ADSC findings in the BMMSC narrative. This is substantive use, not an incidental background citation.

Hassan et al. DOI 10.1186/s40659-022-00408-z was retracted on **2026-10-20** (notice DOI 10.1186/s40659-026-00737-3). Editors reported overlapping images, repeated controls and insufficient verifiable raw data, and lost confidence in that study's conclusions. The authors disagree with the retraction.
