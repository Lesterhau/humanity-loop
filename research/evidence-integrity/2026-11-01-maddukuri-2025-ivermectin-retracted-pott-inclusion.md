# Source review — 2025 ivermectin meta-analysis lists a previously retracted trial

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-01 UTC. **Status:** verified bibliographic/inclusion concern; pooled-outcome contribution and editorial disposition not yet established. **Owner:** Humanity Loop Evidence Integrity reviewer. **No journal contact sent.**

## Exact source and chronology

- Maddukuri RK et al., *Ivermectin as a Treatment Modality in COVID-19 Patients: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Up-To-Date RCTs*, *Indian Journal of Community Medicine* 50(1):9–19, published **2025-01-23**, DOI **10.4103/ijcm.ijcm_117_23**, PMID 40124821, PMCID PMC11927832. Manuscript received 2023-02-23 and accepted 2024-03-27. Full public article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11927832/ ; independent bibliographic record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40124821/ .
- The original Pott-Junior et al. trial, *Use of ivermectin in the treatment of Covid-19: A pilot trial*, DOI **10.1016/j.toxrep.2021.03.003**, was **retracted 2022-05-02** as formally indexed by the publisher notice DOI **10.1016/j.toxrep.2022.04.009** (PMID 35528725): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35528725/ . The notice describes unreliable findings; no misconduct allegation against review authors follows from this chronology.

## Independently checked public-text observations

1. The 2025 review's **Table 1, row 21** explicitly lists “Pott junior et al. [2021]” as a pilot randomized trial (Brazil, 31 reported participants, 27 intervention and 4 control). This is an *included-study listing*, not merely a citation in background discussion.
2. Its supplementary risk-of-bias tables also name “Pott junior,” including the mortality and clinical-worsening assessments. These tables establish consideration in the review's study-assessment workflow. **The exact contribution to each pooled effect estimate has not been independently reconstructed** from individual forest plots, so do not claim that a particular summary effect changes if this trial is excluded.
3. The review's **abstract** states **19 RCTs / 1,111 patients**, whereas its discussion describes **28 RCTs / 8,001 patients**, and its PRISMA description reports 28 included studies. This is a separate, directly visible reporting inconsistency requiring author/editor clarification; it does not by itself identify which denominator is correct.
4. The review's reported overall conclusion was *no demonstrated ivermectin benefit* and low/very-low certainty. The presence of a retracted study does **not** establish that the review's conclusion is false, that patients were harmed, or that authors knew of the retraction.

## Evidence limits / next decision

- The original 2025 journal article is publicly indexed; the source review used its open full-text representation and independent PubMed/DOAJ records. Its supplemental figure-by-figure numeric inputs have **not** been independently audited. A search for an erratum did not establish a relevant existing correction; absence in search is not proof none exists.
- Before any editorial inquiry, verify the exact Pott-Junior citation number, which pooled outcome(s), if any, include its numerical data, whether the sensitivity analysis excluded it, and whether the publisher already issued a correction. If the question survives that check, prepare **one neutral, human-reviewed clarification** to the journal about the retracted study and contradictory study counts; do not accuse or spam.
- **Next review:** 2026-11-08 or sooner upon substantive published correction. **No external correspondence, correction, beneficiary outcome or causal claim.**
