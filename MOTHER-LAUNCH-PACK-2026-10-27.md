## Supporter contributions — planning, not a live payment solicitation

There is a useful case for **optional one-time and recurring support**, particularly to pay transparent hosting, accessibility, audits and contributor onboarding costs. **Wait until the public page can disclose actual costs, identity and legal status**. Recommend launching financial support only after core introduction, evidence links and tested payment receipt flow.

- Humanity Loop is operated under the owner's LLC/organization unless and until independent nonprofit/tax-exempt status is actually verified. **Do not state that supporter payments are tax-deductible charitable donations.** Offer clear label **“Support Humanity Loop's operating costs”**, not `501(c)(3) tax-deductible gift`.
- Stripe donation/tip processing depends on its platform terms, a stated eligible purpose and local restrictions; the user should confirm classification with their accountant/payment provider before accepting funds.
- Use Stripe's properly verified business account, legal entity name and business-payout destination. The same email address does not itself verify whether a Stripe account's legal owner is the LLC or a personal account.
- Mercury's official guidance allows adding Mercury checking/routing as Stripe's business payout bank; make sure legal account holder matches. Never publish bank numbers or ask the user to paste them in chat.
- Proposed public offer: **one-time supporter payment** or **monthly supporter contribution**, with optional cancellation, clear recurring terms, refund/contact policy, no claim of charitable deductibility and a publicly verifiable **cost ledger / periodic financial transparency** appropriate to LLC operations.
- Proposed nonbinding tiers for later testing: one-time choose amount; $5 / $15 / $30 monthly. No paywall for reading findings or joining free contributor research pilot. Do not promise services, voting influence, donor access to case priorities or donor priority in public-interest decisions.
- Human approval and a Stripe/Mercury test transaction + actual payout verification are required before a public “Support” link is added. No Stripe product/price/payment link was created by this document.
