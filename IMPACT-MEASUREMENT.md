# Humanity Loop — Impact Measurement and S.T.A.R. reporting

**Status:** Proposed reporting policy for adoption with independently sourced evidence, November 1, 2026. This document does not claim population-level impact already verified.

## Every win: S.T.A.R. plus reach
- **Situation:** Describe the specific documented problem, source/date, affected service, and plausible stakeholders.
- **Task:** State the intended intervention, accepted scope, permission and success criterion.
- **Action:** Record actions that actually occurred, actors, dates and independently reviewable receipts. Distinguish human action, AI work and third-party actions.
- **Result:** Separate actions and institutional acknowledgments from independently observed corrections and from demonstrated beneficiary consequences. Cite dates and provenance. Never infer exclusive causation from before/after alone.
- **Reach and benefit:** Present four noninterchangeable layers:
  1. **Potential audience** — estimate how many people are in a relevant program/geography/language/need, with dated denominator and uncertainty. This is not exposure or benefit.
  2. **Exposure** — actual distinct page/tool/document users after the correction, only with authorized trustworthy institution-provided analytics or appropriately privacy-preserving measurements.
  3. **Affected decisions** — observed comprehension or eligibility/navigation decision changes supported by actual measurement rather than speculation.
  4. **Confirmed benefit** — verified people who experienced an improved outcome. Use "not measured" where no valid data exist; never silently use zero for unknown.
- **Attribution:** Label a reported issue followed by a correction as chronological observation. An official response crediting the report can be called direct institutional acknowledgment, but not proof of exclusive causation or quantified downstream effects.

## Guardrails
- Do not multiply all beneficiaries in a program by a presumed exposure or behavioral effect to fabricate "people helped."
- Do not substitute Medicare demographics for VA survivors or VA language users, nor count English destination pages as translated content.
- Model-based estimates require the population denominator, timeframe, source, assumptions, uncertainty bounds, sensitivity and independent review.
- Do not aggregate estimates across overlapping programs as if their audiences were disjoint.
- Never publish private correspondent details, visitor-level health data or identifiable beneficiary records without lawful basis and explicit consent.
- For Outcome Tracker OT-001, OT-002 and OT-008, existing public correction evidence supports reporting three verified changes, not a verified aggregate beneficiary count.
- Published views are not human volunteer service hours. AI runtime is never reported as human hours.

## Public-display language
Use labels "Potential audience", "Observed exposure" and "Confirmed benefit" separately. If not measured, say so. A correction remains valuable without pretending we measured who encountered it.

## Governance
Review proposals for partnership data-sharing or cohort studies for privacy, ethics, consent, representativeness and independent validity before collection. Connect Impact Passport recognition only to verified contribution records with participant opt-in and revocation.
