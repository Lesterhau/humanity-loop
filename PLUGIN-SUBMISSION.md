## Human submission steps

1. Sign in to the OpenAI plugin publisher portal with the account/organization that will own Humanity Loop.
2. Complete publisher identity verification if the account has not already done so.
3. Upload `humanity-loop-plugin-0.2.1.zip`.
4. Connect the production MCP endpoint:
   `https://humanity-loop.vercel.app/api/mcp`
5. Complete the domain verification challenge for `humanity-loop.vercel.app` or the final verified project domain required by the portal.
6. Upload/select a primary plugin icon.
7. Provide the reviewer-accessible demo recording requested by the portal.
8. Review the prefilled positive/negative test cases and policy URLs.
9. Complete the required policy/review attestations.
10. Submit for OpenAI review.
11. After approval, publish the listing.
12. Record the listing URL and approval date in issue #2 and `DISCOVERY.md`.

## Do not claim before approval

Do not describe Humanity Loop as listed in the OpenAI Plugins Directory until the portal shows it as approved/published.

Official MCP Registry publication is already complete and is separate from OpenAI Plugins Directory approval.
