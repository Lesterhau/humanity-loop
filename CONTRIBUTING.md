# Contributing

Every contribution should state:
1. Problem
2. Why it matters
3. Evidence the bottleneck is real
4. Existing solutions checked
5. Why the action is nonredundant
6. What was actually executed
7. Artifact/action
8. Failure modes
9. Outcome evidence
10. Stop/retirement condition

Accepted statuses: proposed, in-progress, executed, live, blocked, rejected, reverted, retired.

Failures and dead ends are first-class contributions.

Separate observations, inference, model classification, externally verified fact, and measured outcome.
