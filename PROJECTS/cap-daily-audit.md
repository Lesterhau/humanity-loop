# CAP Daily Audit

**Category:** Prevention / safety  
**Status:** Live  
**URL:** https://cap-daily-audit-s0a2he.v2.appdeploy.ai/

## Plain-English purpose

CAP Daily Audit automatically checks current public National Weather Service emergency alerts once a day.

It looks for missing or inconsistent information that could make an alert harder to understand or act on, such as missing instructions, missing target areas, broken timing, or absent core CAP fields.

The findings are stored in a resolution queue for follow-up.

## Relationship to CAP Clarity Check

- **CAP Clarity Check:** private, browser-only, before publication.
- **CAP Daily Audit:** automated, public-feed auditing, after publication.

## Resolution workflow

Findings should move through the Humanity Loop Outcome Tracker:
verify → identify responsible issuer/contact → factual report → follow-up → independent recheck → resolved/rejected/escalated/closed.

Do not shame an alert issuer merely because an automated heuristic fired. Verify before outreach.
