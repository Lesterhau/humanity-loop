# Federal Policy Delta

**Category:** Civic / policy transparency  
**Status:** Live  
**URL:** https://federal-policy-delta-p49z0b.v2.appdeploy.ai/

## Plain-English purpose

Federal Policy Delta watches major official U.S. federal policy sources every day.

When an official source visibly changes, the system records the change, explains it in plain English, and identifies legal-authority questions that may deserve qualified review.

It currently watches the White House, Congress.gov, and Federal Register pages for major executive departments and agencies including FDA, Defense, State, Homeland Security, Agriculture, Commerce, Education, Energy, HHS, HUD, Interior, Transportation, Treasury, Justice, and Veterans Affairs.

## What it does not do

- It does not assume every change is illegal.
- It does not tell people which party, candidate, or policy position to support.
- Legal-authority notes are research flags, not court rulings or legal advice.

## Future resolution workflow

For material changes:
1. preserve before/after evidence;
2. identify the relevant statutory/regulatory/constitutional authority;
3. independently verify the legal question;
4. identify appropriate oversight, inspector-general, ombudsman, congressional, or watchdog channels;
5. prepare a factual evidence packet;
6. publish a neutral public status record with receipts where appropriate;
7. track the issue until resolved, rejected with reason, superseded, or intentionally closed.

Political persuasion and automated calls to pressure people toward a specific political outcome are outside the automated workflow.
