## Activation rule

A task should use the **smallest competent team**.

The Project Manager selects specialists based on task needs. Do not instantiate all roles simply because they exist.

A specialist earns persistent/warm status when recurring workload and marginal value exceed its compute/coordination cost.

## Inheritance rule

Every role template must define:
- mission;
- tools allowed;
- prohibited actions;
- evidence standard;
- budget;
- maximum descendants;
- escalation path;
- evaluation metrics;
- expiry/review conditions.

Children may not exceed parent permissions.
